## Failure-resolution ladder (spec 6.1, R-OPT-01)
For every failed check, the decision entry names the rungs tried, in order:
1. Subtract (remove a feature/part) 2. Modify geometry 3. Rearrange 4. Change mechanism/structural approach 5. Add material/parts.
A rung-5 fix must state why rungs 1–4 do not work.

## Trade table D-212 rev 3: horn ATTACHMENT (T-063, after the second review)
Diagnosis first. Rev 2 put the horn moment into the elevon as **peel of a 1 mm plate on the tape skin**. On foam, a thin plate is a beam on an elastic foundation: the peel concentrates within 1/lambda (about 5 mm) of the stem, so plate area does not help (review r2 C-1: 416-1832 kPa against 129 kPa). That is a **load-path error, not a size error**. The same check also shows that the elevon itself, taken as foam only, cannot carry the VD hinge moment in torsion: Saint-Venant on the section, 234 kPa (504 kPa at x 2.156) against the 86 kPa EST shear. So the tape skin carries the elevon torsion, and no foam-only check of any attachment can pass at the project's EST foam allowables. The attachment therefore has to (1) be impossible to peel off, (2) put the moment into the full elevon depth (both skins and the foam between them) and not into one skin, (3) spread it along the chord. The proof is a coupon or bench test.

Common inputs: hold F 17.05 N (`elevon_dynamics.py`), stall F 51.0 N quasi-static (0.397 N m / (8.5 mm x 0.916), review r2 M-1). Factors: printed / bonded 2.156 at hold, 1.4375 at stall (1.15 x 1.25; the load factor is dropped because the stall force is the actuator's ceiling); foam shear 86 kPa EST (foam_103.py), PETG 20 MPa EST in-layer and bearing (fea_arm.py, arm_cup_checks.py). Linkage geometry (rev 2 row E) unchanged: hole 9.155 mm over the hinge, arm 8.5 mm. "Works?" = function. Rows A-E are attachment options; F-I are the M-4 / M-5 sub-choices.
