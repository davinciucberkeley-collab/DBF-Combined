## 4. Mechanism (U5): the blocking finding

Cause: the hinge moment is 0.146 x 1.44 = 0.210 N m, and the shorter horn (7.39 mm) raises the rod force:
- At hold: 17.05 -> 30.44 N.
- At stall: 50.99 -> 63.25 N.

Rows that passed before and now FAIL:
- Z-bend leg (A228, stall): 1727 MPa needed, against 1680 MPa.
- Horn hole bearing (hold): 32.8 MPa, against 20 MPa.
- Horn shear-out (stall): 13.8 MPa, against the 11.55 MPa EST allowable.
- Keeper bridge MEC-008: utilisation 1.22 (hold) and 1.69 (stall).

Rib-in-foam, elevon torsion, free play and horn bearing at stall failed before and still fail.

**Conductor decision needed (no further fix brief written):**
- **(a) Raise the servo, arm and rod 1.77 mm.** This restores HORN_R to 9.155 mm and ARM_R to 8.5 mm.
  - Expected: the stall force goes back to 51 N, so the leg, shear-out and keeper rows return to their old values. The keeper at hold would be about 0.71.
  - But the hold bearing would still be about 26.5 MPa, against 20 MPa, so it still FAILS.
  - The servo case top (6.7 mm) is already above the skin aft of x 127 (zu 8.4 -> 4.45 mm over the case). This raises it further, which affects the cover/fairing and enclosed-wiring CAD.
- **(b) Keep the geometry and modify the linkage parts.**
  - Thicker horn boss or pin at the hole (bearing, shear-out).
  - Stronger keeper.
  - Thicker or graded rod (leg).
  - Possibly a servo stall-current or endpoint limit to cap the stall force.
- **(c) Accept these rows as test-gated,** like the existing horn/rib rows (TP-15 to TP-18). The load values in the coupons would change (36.8 N -> 2.156 x 30.44 = 65.6 N, stall 51 -> 63 N).

Option (a) plus a horn boss for bearing seems to be the smallest change. That is the tactician's reading, not a decision.

Options, not decided:
- **(i) A stronger servo of similar size.**
  - Needed: stall torque >= 1.5 x 1.5 x (0.2174 + 0.0093 + damper slew) at 7.2 V, about 0.53 N m. The ES3054HV is 0.397 N m.
  - Cost: a part, BOM and CAD change (case dimensions from a vendor drawing). Needs a scout.

  - The cold claim would pass. My estimate (not run): R2 rises slightly to about 1.10, because the servo is torque-limited.

- **(iii) Lower the hinge moment.**
