## 2. Soundness

Category assignment (MISSION.md racetrack 30 deg bank, R 51 m, no precision tracking): reasonable. Cat B for cruise, Cat C for the terminal transition phases, Cat A not a mission phase. The Table IXa alignment caveat is kept. OK as an engineering analogy, not a compliance claim.

Single gust vector: valid as a bound, with two disclosures missing. (a) The RFS limit (SCHEDULE-A.md:12, mean <= 5, gust <= 8 m/s) is a horizontal wind speed. It limits the 3 m/s increment to the horizontal plane; it says nothing about a vertical gust. Allowing the 3 m/s to point in the vertical-side plane rests on the isotropy assumption (reserve.md s2, EST), not on the RFS limit. (b) "Any direction" was evaluated only in the vertical-side plane; a streamwise component (3 m/s is 33 % of dynamic pressure at 18 m/s) was not examined. Choosing the worst direction in that plane is correct for the stacked linear sum (a unit vector along the two gains).

Linear stacking: conservative in transition (hand 19.18 against SITL RQ5 14.2) but not in cruise (below).

Hand cruise sum (17.58, +2.42 "YES") is contradicted by its own SITL: RQ4 reaches 19.95 per surface (0.35 s above the 19.0 PWM clip, 0.2 s above 19.5). The hand model omits the load factor of the banked turn (overshoot bank 40-46 deg, n up to about 1.3) and uses a roll rate of 44.8 deg/s where the plant reaches 66 deg/s (66/8.8 = 7.5 deg, not 5.1). RQ4's first leg also overshoots to +46 deg (target +30; t = 110.56 + 1.0 s), not only the reversal (-39.8 against -30); the executed bank change is about 85 deg, more than the 60 deg Category B definition, which makes RQ4 harsher than the requirement and still a touch. D-254 only mentions the reversal overshoot.

RMAX 45 "set to the requirement": the cap equals the requirement's steady rate, with no margin for the 0.363 s lag, and the plant exceeded it (49.7 on leg one, 66 on reversal). Whether Category B (60 deg in 1.7 s) is met with the cap was not measured; only the elevon peak was. Roll authority for gust recovery is also reduced from 150 deg/s; RQ4/RQ5 ran with it, one sample each.

Transition hand sum uses pitch peak 8.7 (D-253 T1, RMAX 150) but this decision's own RQ1 (RMAX 45) measured 9.0 (0.60): 9.0 + 3.6 + 6.88 = 19.48, margin +0.52, still YES but not the +0.82 stated.

b/V scaling: reserve.md s1 shows the scaled criteria are not attainable and that adopting an unscaled or relaxed roll criterion is a team decision. D-254 picks the unscaled MIL numbers (45 deg/s for a 0.9 m wing) without repeating that caveat. It is the engineering team's derived requirement (AM-057, "Yes, derive"), so permitted, but the choice should be visible.

Honest bounding of the 0.2 s and 0.60: D-254 does not overclaim. It says "touches", "not clear of the stop", "adoption pending", "TP-11 / TP-19 stays the decider", "repeat scatter 0.01-0.03". Only the "stop" wording needs tightening.
