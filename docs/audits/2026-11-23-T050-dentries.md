## 0.1 Rule origin

- R-OPT-04 (spec 6.4, line 92): "Research benchmarks/literature; compare several concepts"; acceptance "Each major mechanism decision cites >= 1 benchmark" (source dated). Spec text: `docs/MASTER_PROJECT_SPEC.md` line 469, "Use benchmark designs, literature, and known physical principles to evaluate alternatives."
- T-024 (VERIFIED COMPLETE) put the ladder and the trade template in `docs/engineering-decisions.md` (lines 7-14, including "Cite at least one benchmark/literature source for mechanism choices (6.4)").

## 0.2 Method and criteria
Scope: D-208 to D-240 (33 ids) plus the un-numbered tooling rows between D-212 and D-213 (T-028, T-020); D-212 has four rows (first version, rev 2, rev 3, minors). The CHANGE-LOG rows were read; the reports they cite were opened and searched for trade tables (a table with alternatives and a chosen row) and for sources. Line numbers are CHANGE-LOG lines at the working tree of 2026-11-23.
- Trade table: a table (or ladder-ordered option rows) comparing at least two alternatives on the spec 6.2 columns; prose "rejected X because" with no table does not count. The table may sit in the report the entry cites.
- Benchmark: a dated source for a comparable design, practice or literature value for the choice itself. Vendor data sheets, material properties and ArduPilot parameter pages used as inputs are NOT counted (they are data sources, cited properly in many entries) but are noted. An internal reference to an earlier project design (K09-L) is noted as "internal only".
- Type: mechanism / structure (M/S) = a decision that changes load path, geometry, joints, structural layout or a moving mechanism. N/A = analysis, records, sourcing/BOM, tooling, programme/board choice, or an implementation of an earlier entry.
- Verdict: COMPLY needs both (a) and (b); GAP if M/S and either is missing; N/A otherwise.
