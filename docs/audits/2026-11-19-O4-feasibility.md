Formulas used. Window = (hi - 1.33) / (hi + 1.33), with hi = 0.80 x 4 T_sag x 0.97 x 0.93 x cos 17 / (m g) (the `mission_gate_a.py` convention). Mass for ±5 % = 0.80 x 4 T_sag x 0.97 x 0.93 x cos 17 / g / (1.33 x 1.05 / 0.95). At a fixed torque (current) the thrust is T = (CT/CQ) Q / D for similar props: at equal current a larger diameter gives *less* thrust. That is why the 10 in prop loses at the A-P16 limit and gains only where it spends current.

## 4. O4b quantified (best path; EST unless stated)

- **Mass:** 1,634 g (S5 itemised, 1,626 flat) + 5 g pack = **about 1,639 g itemised, about 1,632 flat**. FC swap mass "—" (±5 g EST). This is 169 g over 1,470 g. The gate is re-derived by C3 as AM-005 allows; the limit is not relaxed.
- **Transition window:** +8.2 % at 1,639 g (`full(14.92, 1.0)` 9.12 N x 0.97 x 0.93 x cos 17). Mass for ±5 %: 1,747 g at derate 0.93, 1,653 at 0.88, 1,597 at 0.85. **O4b holds ±5 % only if the bench derate is 0.873 or more** (the present design already rests on 0.93). - **A-P16:** one motor ≤ 12.1 A at all voltages by construction (11.6-11.7 A at sag with duty 1.0; 12.1 A at fresh with 0.90). Pack current at sag with all four at duty 1.0 is 43 A, about 25 % of a 2.3 Ah 75C pack's 172 A (well inside); the existing `Q_M_BAT_CURR_MAX` 41 A in `A.param` must be re-checked.
- **Cruise:** 5.03 min (2.8 Ah, 275 g `U`; D183.md s3 gives 141 x 43 x 22 mm, price and US stock "—"); 3.60 min at 2.4 Ah. With the Tattu 2300 it is 3.24 min and fails.
- **Vibration:** A-P10(a) passes at kd and nom (69-79 Hz against 87.9 or more). The hi corner (EST skin x1.3) is 86.3 Hz (rotary inertia) to 89.8 Hz (none), against a limit of 87.9 (derate 1.0) to 90.8 Hz (derate 0.93): marginal, and the tap test decides. The FE masses predate T-067: the heavier cups and tray lower the mode (favourable, not computed). A-P10(b) forced response is 17.9-25.6 m/s2 against 30. A-P10(c) and A-P17 are unchanged (no 10 in band shift).
- **Structure:** wing skin T-071 passes (P15 wrinkling 1.23, foam 1.34, skin 1.37). C6 is 1.30 rigid-body and 0.87 at x1.5 EST, both at 1.50 kg (F-5: about 1.19 / 0.80 at 1.64 kg, EST). R1''/R3fix/R4 pass with CAD fillets (R4 junction 0.87 on an EST footprint). The cup EVA rows (0.01-0.15) and bond-integrity rows are coupon class. Tip-over is +0.51 deg over A-P9 at the pad edge.
- **What is EST or "—":** derate 0.93, I0, pack R; the skin laminate and the x0.7 / 1.3 spread; the dynamic factor 1.5; the T-070 R5-R8 deltas; the 2,800 mAh pack (`U`); Lua availability on V03 and an H7 FC price ("—"); the C3 window with the scheduled cap (not run); landing at 1.64 kg (not run).

## 5. Verdict

**EARLY NO-GO for O4 as approved (AM-005).** Its defining resize, the 10x4.5 props, cannot deliver the needed thrust within A-P16 (+0.8 % at most against 6.4-8.8 % needed). It puts a strongly coupled mode in the hover band that no layup, stiffness change in reach, or FC isolation fixes (A-P10(a), forced response 54.8 m/s2). And the smaller packs fail cruise without it. Under AM-005 the board re-sits now; there is no need to wait for 2026-12-15. I relax no limit.
