## 1. Accuracy

**SIMULATED tags are backed by executed computation.**
- **S3:** `mech_state` inverts the AR-001 table (the D-212 rev 3 run, committed in `82b9b4ba`) and eases the servo angle,
  not the elevon angle. The connection check holds to 1.5e-5 m.
- **S4:** the `sitl_pose` chain reproduces the logged pitch (checked offline to 2e-11 deg) and the heading. Ground track
  and nose agree in cruise (table above).
- **S5:** every number is read from the result JSON at draw time; none is typed in.

The tags are correct in kind. The S4 tag needs more context (M-4), and the S5 tags make incomplete pass claims (M-3).

**Elevon sign convention:** consistent end to end (table). There is no defect.

**Hub F2 "judged value".** The arithmetic is right: p99 envelope 5.87 × 2.156 = 12.7 against 20. The presentation is not
right:
- (a) The row "Single-point peak, not judged 19.2 MPa" stands next to "Allowable 20 MPa" without its factor. A viewer
  reads a 4 % margin. Factored, that point is 19.2 × 2.156 = 41.4 MPa (utilisation 2.07, field max 2.069), and it is
  excluded only by the D-207 singularity rule.
- (b) The colour shown is skin utilisation, whose p99 is 0.918 and which has 0.79 % red vertices. The judged 0.63
  (12.7/20) is a volume-element percentile over zones. The colour bar and the tag measure different things.
- (c) The tag says "max judged" for the whole shot. The sub says the colour covers "printed and carbon parts", and the
  darkest feature on screen is the bridge tube at 0.958 (F2) and **1.002 in S2**.

On S2, "max 18.3 MPa vs allowable 20" reads as a pass for everything shown, while the bridge exceeds 1 at its bond end.
That is the unsupported-claim class that spec 7.6 lists (M-3).

**Unsourced or stale on-screen text:**
- 1.3 "113 modelled parts, no stand-in geometry": the count is now 115 and the text is hard-coded. "No stand-in" is
  overstated: the servo ears and output boss are "—" in `electrical.py:538`, and the pitot probe, board and several
  hardware dimensions are EST (D-208).
- 1.2 "Packed 0.90 x 0.50 x 0.39 m" is hard-coded but correct (`box/packed_m`).
- 2.5 "MEC-001 to MEC-005" omits the rev 3 keeper MEC-008, which is in the shot (minor).
- 3.1 "-21 to +21 deg": these are the mechanical stops (`ELEV_STOP` 21); the flight limit is ±20 (`ELEV_LIM`). Say so (m-2).

## 2. CAD fidelity

- **D-212 rev 3 is shown.** The horn is the long rib blade, `MEC-005` "blade … + rib through a slot, 2.5–55 mm aft of
  the hinge". The keeper MEC-008 sits over the Z-bend on the arm (stills f2700, f3450). The elevon group (WNG-006/007/010,
  MEC-004/005) and the arm group (MEC-002/008, HWC-002) match `collide.moving_groups`.
- **Geometry is live:** `bb.build(False, MODEL_A)`. No stand-in boxes were added by the film. The servo body is the CAD's
  own simplified ES3054HV envelope (ears unknown, "—"); that is a CAD item, but the film must not claim "no stand-in
  geometry" (M-1).
- **Props static in S4 (M-6).** The project's own CAD-fidelity rule asks the film to look like the real aircraft. A
  quadrotor hovering, climbing and landing with stopped blades, under a "SIMULATED" tag, contradicts that. It also
  misleads newcomers, the spec 7.4 audience. "Stated, not hidden" in FILM-PROGRESS is not visible on screen.
  - Required: spin the real blade meshes about each motor axis at a fixed visual rate. Use a non-aliasing step per frame
    at 60 fps (for example 47°, not a divisor of 180°), or add the alpha blade-copy blur the flight film already uses.
  - Label it in the readout: "prop rotation illustrative, not to rate".
  - Optional and data-driven: use the logged `u1..u4` to scale the blur opacity.
  - Disks are not acceptable (CAD rule).
- **Cut-aways.** 2.3, 2.4, 2.5, 3.1 and 3.2 hide parts and leads (`wires: false`, `PRP-`, `PWR-`, `AVN-` hidden). Only
  3.2 says so in its sub; 2.3 must say "(props, pack, avionics and leads hidden)" (m-3).
- **Ground contact at t = 0 (accepted).** The pads sit 2 mm below z = 0 (0.316 against 0.314 m, the plant's contact
  compression). This is invisible at 2.6 m.
- **FEA geometry.** Hub d209 predates the D-208 tray-flange trim. I did not verify whether the FEA hub model includes
  that flange. State the run revision on the panel (it does: "run d209").

6. **S5.3 plate.** A full matplotlib figure on screen for 6 s (title, two subtitle lines, four legend lines, a

7. **S6.** As FILM-PROGRESS notes, the card appears abruptly (no 0.4 s fade) and 6.1 drifts the aircraft up the frame in
