### Wire-entry rule for concept A (replaces the K09-L square-entry rule)
Source: `collide_blender.py` D-86 (WIRE_END_MM 6 mm, END_SEAT_MM 0.5 mm) and D-89 (TERMINATION body names), and the concept A harness in `qp/A/cad/amodel/electrical.py` (leads are soldered to pads or cups on ESC, FC and servo, or crimped into JST-GH / XT30 housings; none is a push-through square entry). For each strand end inside one of its terminal parts' solids: (a) the strand runs at most 6 mm past where it first enters that solid; (b) the end lies in, or within strand radius + 0.5 mm of, a CAD body of that part whose name matches TERMINATION (pad, cup, joint, plug, socket, header, housing, lead exit ...), which is what collide_a applies per body; the GLB holds one united solid per part, so the named bodies come from the job. The K09-L "square to the face, flat ring" test is dropped: a lead lapped onto a pad does not arrive square. Seated ends (`end_seats`) are unchanged. This rule is my choice from those sources (EST); it needs the team's agreement.

## 2. Root cause (export artefact, shared CAD code)
`amodel/electrical.py` `build_assembly` copies the core's cut list into the skin plies: `ctx.components[pid].cuts = list(core_.cuts)` for WNG-011, WNG-012, WNG-014-R, WNG-014-L (T-077 p8). That bypasses `Component.cut`, which is what calls `backend.cut`. The CAD gates read `comp.cuts` (113 cuts on WNG-011) so they honour the openings; the Blender backend kept only 61 and the exported skin plies lacked the other openings (D-88 failure mode: wires, servo bodies, the pitot lines and the elevon-linkage parts shown through uncut skin).
Fix (in `electrical.py`, same loop): also reset the backend's list for that part and replay every copied cut through `ctx.backend.cut`. Guarded by `isinstance(getattr(ctx.backend, 'cuts', None), dict)`, so the Stub backend used by collide_a is unaffected.

### Diagnosis (step 1 of the brief, adapted)
The CAD is not B-rep: every body is a Blender mesh built from shape specs (`blender_backend.py`; the hub outer body is an OpenCASCADE/gmsh mesh), so there is no OCC BRepExtrema / BRepAlgoAPI_Common on "the model the exporter uses". The exact reference used instead is the job bodies minus their cutters (the CAD gate's own definition), measured in Blender with the repo's BVH code on the fixed export (scratch `diag.py`, `diag2.py`, `diag3.py`). Findings:

| Pair | GLB gate depth | Measured cause | CAD-reference number |
|---|---|---|---|
| CGE-001 x E-HRN-02..05 | 0.63-0.69 mm | Hub surface samples lie on the lead-exit hole cutter floor (|z| 6.9 mm) which is 0.1 mm inside the 7.0 mm bore radius; the curved bore wall at the hole edge is at z 6.4-6.6 mm, so a 0.3-0.4 mm sickle of hub stays across the lead. The same material exists in the CAD (strand 0 of E-HRN-03: 6 of 1,227 strand samples in CAD hub minus cutters, 13 in the GLB). The CAD gate excuses it: a point within CUT_TOL 0.02 mm of a cutter surface counts as removed (samples were 0.017 mm from the cutter). A real design clash, not tessellation. |
| E-HRN-11 x WNG-001 | 0.338 mm | Strand 2 inside the foam wall at the I2C groove corners (CAD reference: 19 of 437 strand samples in foam minus cutters, 21 in the GLB). Groove half width 1.7 mm too tight for the 4 mm bend and the +-0.5 mm bundle offset. Real clash. |
| AVN-009-S/T x WNG-001 | 0.236 / 0.189 mm | Tube outside the pitot slot cutters by 0.189 mm at the same point as the GLB hit (51.69, -422.2 / -417.8, 6.51); slot floor PT_ZT - 2.3 mm too high for the 4 mm bend. Real clash. |

So no export-tessellation cause (step 2 not needed; chordal sag of the 48-segment cutters is 0.017 mm and played only the role of hiding the film in the CAD gate). Step 3 applies: minimal CAD fixes, logged as **D-247** in `redesign/params/CHANGE-LOG.md` (together with the wire-entry rule of the GLB gate recorded as an engineering-team decision with its reasoning, as asked):
- `amodel/wing.py` ~l.1326: phase-lead exit hole cutter in CGE-001 starts at |z| 6.0 mm (was 6.9).
