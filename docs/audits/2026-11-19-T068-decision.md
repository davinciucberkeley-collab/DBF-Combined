## 4. D2: tip-over (A-P9 ≥ 30 deg)

Basis for not re-deriving: A-P9 is tagged PHYSICAL (`REQUIREMENTS-A.md` l.42). Its real margin is already thin dynamically: the pessimistic sim tips at 12 deg lean and 0.52 m/s with the lean in the direction of travel, and card-allowed SITL touchdowns reach 11-12 deg (T064r s2.5). Moving the limit to 29 deg to fit the CAD would be goal-post moving. What is logged as a definition, not a relaxation: **the pivot is the pad's outer edge on the side the CG is offset to.** That is the outermost support point, which is where static tip-over happens. The sim's pad-centre value is reported alongside as the conservative bound.

Sizing with the D3 cup (cup depth ~4 mm, pad lengthened 30 → 34 mm so the 20 mm stroke keeps 30 mm of free foam; X_GROUND 0.3375 → ~0.3415): h = 0.3415 − 0.02137 = 0.3201 m. For 30.5 deg: z_edge − z_cg ≥ 0.5890 × 0.3201 = 0.1886 → **z_edge ≥ 0.195 m (+12 mm)**. That gives edge 30.5 deg, pad centre ~29.7 deg (hand calc, CG of cad_mass.json). Dynamic gain from T064r `tip_options.txt` (+10 / +13 mm): about +2 deg at 0.16-0.52 m/s.
