The fix is small. See "Required to accept".

## Findings

### C-1 Critical: the horn-bond hold PASS rests on a rigid-plate assumption the drawn 1 mm plate does not meet

**Evidence.**
- `linkage_loads_check.py` uses peak peel = 6M/(bL²) over the full 20 × 30 mm plate. That formula assumes a rigid plate.
  (The first review proposed it; it is valid only for a plate that is stiff compared with the foam under it.)
- The drawn part is different (`electrical.py` `HORN_PX0/PX1/PHY`, `horn_blade_loop`):
  - a **1.0 mm** PETG plate;
  - a stem 5 mm long in x and **2 mm wide in y**, standing at the **front edge** of the plate;
  - the plate overhangs the stem by 14 mm each side in y and by 15 mm aft.
- Scratch FE model (beam on a Winkler foundation, consistent elements, free-free).
  - Inputs: plate E 2.0 GPa (PETG, recalled, not cited). Foundation k = E_foam / h with E_foam 5–10 MPa (repo basis
    "XPS modulus ~8–10 MPa", `proofs/out/CHAIN.md` ASM-D5) and h 4–8 mm (elevon depth under the plate 7.4–8.2 mm, from
    `zu`/`zl`).
  - The couple is applied as a linear pressure under the stem.
  - Rigid-limit check: E → 2e14 gives 80.4 kPa against the script's 81.1 kPa, so the model reproduces the rigid formula.

  | Case | M (N m) | rigid 20 × 30 | flexible, b = 30 mm (generous: assumes the stem spans the full width) | flexible, b = 12 mm (about the stem plus 2/λ) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | hold × 1.5 | 0.243 | 122 kPa | 416–733 kPa | 1040–1832 kPa |
  | stall into the stop (51.0 N) | 0.485 | 243 kPa | 830–1462 kPa | 2074–3654 kPa |

  - Characteristic length 1/λ ≈ 4.8 mm (E_foam 10 MPa, h 8 mm), against plate dimensions of 15–20 mm.
  - Even the most generous row is 3.2–5.7 × the 129 kPa EST allowable at hold × 1.5, and 2.2 × at hold with a factor
    of 1.0.
- **Conclusion:** the stated "122 kPa < 129 kPa, passes" is not shown. A horn that peels at the VD hinge moment means
  loss of an elevon. The record (`CHANGE-LOG` D-212 rev 2, `analysis-records` "Hand checks", trade-table row E) states
  the hold bond as passing.

**Required fix (any one, then re-state the result):**
1. Through-bolt the horn to a backing plate on the lower skin. This is common RC practice for foam surfaces, and the
   record already names it as the fallback. Model the screws and the backing plate in the CAD, with mass. The peel check
   then becomes a screw/bearing check.
2. Thicken the plate and add gussets so the rigid assumption holds. For λL ≲ 1 the plate needs roughly t ≥ 4–5 mm,
   which is impractical, so gussets plus a coupon are needed.
3. Keep the design, but change the hold result to "not shown (rigid-plate formula invalid; elastic-foundation estimate
   3–15 × over)". Make the pull coupon a pass/fail gate before first flight, with the load stated (≥ 1.5 × 17.1 N at the
   9.5 mm height).

Whichever option is chosen, correct the PASS wording in CHANGE-LOG D-212 rev 2, analysis-records and trade-table row E.

### M-1 Major: the "stall fault" rod force is the dynamic step peak (42.7 N), not the servo stalled in the stop (51.0 N); the leg check, the receipt test and the bond FAIL are all understated

**Evidence.**
- The `linkage_loads_check.py` docstring defines the stall force as "0.397 N m / lever is the servo driven into its
  stop". The code instead uses `dyn['rod']['peak_compression_N']` = 42.74 N, the transient peak of the step cases.
- Quasi-static stall against the stop: F = 0.397 / (8.5 mm × l_a). With l_a = 0.916 at +21° this is **51.0 N**
  (50.3 N at −21°; 46.7 N at neutral).
- Recomputed at 51.0 N:
  - leg nominal 481 MPa, **782 MPa with K** (655 reported);
  - bond rigid peel **243 kPa** (203 reported);
  - bond mean shear **85.0 kPa against 85.8 kPa EST** (passes by 1 %, not "passes" with the 71 kPa margin shown).
- The first review's F-2 fix asked for a factor of 1.5 at the stall case and a 45 N receipt test at its smaller force.
  Rev 2 uses factor 1.0 and a **43 N** test. A plain-steel rod can pass 43 N and still yield at the 51 N stall. At
  factor 1.5 the required yield is 1172 MPa; A228 music wire, at 1680 MPa (recalled), still passes.
- D3 uses the 42.7 N peak against Euler/2 = 55.6 N. The static 51.0 N still passes, but with only 9 % margin.

**Required fix.**
- Use F_stall = τ_stall / (R_arm · min l_a) over ±21° (51.0 N) in `linkage_loads_check.py`, or justify the 42.7 N.
- Apply the factor 1.5 the review asked for at stall, or log why 1.0 is enough.
- Receipt test at ≥ 1.5 × 51 N ≈ 77 N along the rod, no permanent set (or ≥ 51 N if the factor is justified at 1.0).
- Restate the shear margin.

### M-3 Major: the printed horn blade root is not checked; at the stall case it reaches roughly the strength of PETG

**Evidence.**
- Root section: 5 mm (x) × 2 mm (y), Z = 8.33 mm³. Lever from the hole (z 9.45) to the plate top (z 0.94) = 8.51 mm.
- Root bending stress: hold 17.4 MPa; **hold × 1.5 = 26.1 MPa**; **stall into the stop 52.1 MPa** (43.7 MPa at the
  42.7 N used).
- PETG strengths are not in the repo. The recalled values (not verified, no web access in this session) are about
  45–50 MPa in the layer plane and much less across layers. The guide gives no print orientation. With the plate flat
  on the bed, the root tension crosses the layers, so the hold × 1.5 case is marginal and the stall case fails.
- The drawn root has a sharp corner, no fillet.
- The hole is fine:
  - bearing with the 1.0 mm pin in the 2 mm blade: 8.5 MPa at hold, 25.5 MPa at stall;
  - shear-out over the 1.45 mm ligament: 8.8 MPa at stall.
  - The task's "1.2 mm hole" does not match the CAD: the drawn horn hole is 1.5 mm and the pin 1.0 mm.

**Required fix.**
- Add a blade-root check with a cited PETG allowable, for the print orientation actually used.
- Specify the print orientation: on its side (y up), so that root bending stays in the layer plane.
- Add a root fillet, and/or widen the stem (8 mm gives 20 MPa at 51 N) or thicken the blade to 3 mm. A thicker blade
  needs the clevis gap checked again (see m-5).

### M-4 Major (was F-8 Minor; rev 2 makes it worse): hole-clearance free play is about 5° at the elevon against the ≤ 1° guide, and the record understates it

**Evidence.**
- The CAD hole sizes, now prescribed by the build guide:
  - arm hole 1.5 mm with the 1.2 mm rod: 0.3 mm diametral, 0.3/9.155 rad = **1.88°**;
  - horn hole 1.5 mm with the 1.0 mm clevis pin: 0.5 mm, **3.13°**.
  - Total about 5.0° (it was 3.55° at R 12.9).
- The guide target is free play ≤ 1° (`review/QP-4/SERVO.md:35`).
- The AR-001 limitation reads "0.3 mm diametral over the 9.155 mm lever, about 1.9 deg if both holes are loose". That
  is one hole, and it omits the 0.5 mm horn-hole clearance.
- The build guide (D0) now tells the builder to "drill a 1.5 mm hole", which builds in the 0.3 mm clearance.
- The horn is now printed, so its hole can be sized to the pin.

**Required fix.**
- Drill the arm about 1.25–1.3 mm (state the drill).
- Print the horn hole undersize and ream it to the pin (≈ 1.0–1.05 mm).
- Update the CAD hole sizes, ALLOW_Z / ALLOW_C and K5.
- Sum the free-play budget in AR-001 and carry it to FLUTTER step 5 / E1 with a number.

### M-5 Major (F-4 partly open): the horn and the Z-bend are still not complete, buildable parts in the deliverables

**Evidence.**
- **Keeper / Z-bend lower leg.**
  - The CAD leg is still "flush with the arm underside; keeper under the arm '-'", and the arm still sits on the case
    top (`ARM_Z0 = SV_Z1`). A Z-bend's lower leg has no room there.
  - The record calls it the "standard L-end of the M02 rod (leg 2.75 mm)". The 2.75 mm is just ROD_Z − ARM_Z0 from the
    CAD, not M02 data, and an L-end with 0.15 mm of leg below a 2 mm arm can lift out of the hole.
  - K7 and the hi state assume a keeper 0.3 mm away that the CAD does not contain.
  - The record says to measure the boss gap at receipt. It does not say that a real boss gap raises ROD_Z, and that the
    horn hole height (9.155 mm) must then follow the measured arm-top height. The printed horn makes that easy, but it
    has to be written down.
- **Drawing and fabrication.**
  - The build text says "drawing: CAD MEC-004", but `qp/A/drawings/out/` has no MEC-004 drawing or STL.
  - Build step D3 has no `make`/`fab` print beat or orientation, and its `need` still reads "M02 linkage set: horns".
  - The print summary ("145 g in 43 parts") does not include the horn.
- **Generated artefacts not regenerated** (stale against `electrical.py` and `parts_links_a.py`):
  - `cad/model/mechanisms/interfaces.json` MEC-004: 1.0 g, nylon, buy;
  - `cad/out/parts-a.csv` MEC-004: "Bought: BOM M02 RMRC";
  - `BUILD-GUIDE-A.md:1716` D3 parts table: "bought | M02 RMRC | 2.0", with the running totals unchanged.
  - Only the prose lines were edited by hand.

**Required fix.**
- Model the Z-bend lower leg with an EST boss gap marked "-", or name a real keeper (clip, or the E/Z connector).
- State in D0/D3: measure the arm-top height, then print the horn with the hole at that height above the hinge axis.
