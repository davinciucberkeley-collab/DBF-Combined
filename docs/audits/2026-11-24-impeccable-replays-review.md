linkage replays in the `#sims` view of `redesign/site/app.html` (generated by `redesign/site/make_replays_view.py`, runtime
`redesign/site/replay.js`, styles `redesign/site/explorer.css` lines 347-422). Audience: a student build team and an instructor, desktop and phone.

## ceiling
These are what the technical-paper world already offers and the replays do not use:
- A one-line verdict under each replay head (`sh-v`) in place of the bold summary paragraph.
- One drawn timing strip under the slider (mode bands shaded and named, event ticks labelled) in place of two chip rows. That is the engineering-document form for this data.
- Values written on the plots: the peak load factor at its point, limit values on the limit lines, round-number axes with units.
- Tabular-figure readouts where the value carries the weight.
- The 3-D stage with a hairline caption strip under the canvas rather than text over the model.

## material_fixes
1. Pass/fail values (fidelity: verdict first, table missing in effect). Make Value / Limit / Result readable with no sideways scroll at 1280 and 400. Move `#rp-f-pf` out of the right column to full width under `.rp-main`, or render each line as a hairline `dl` row: "line, value against limit, pass/fail".
2. Verdict and hierarchy (REVIEW-STYLE, T-104 header pattern). Add one `sh-v` verdict line under each replay head, for example "Fails one line: elevon pitch use 0.65 against 0.60", "Passes: peak 9.93 g of 12 g, stroke 8.4 of 20 mm, end tilt 0.04 deg of 3 deg", and the linkage equivalent. Delete the repeated bold summary. State the "playback, not a new simulation" line once in the intro, and drop the per-panel bold label and all KB counts.
3. Plot readability (D-242). In `makePlot`, size each SVG to its own cell width, not the `.rp-plots` host. Set ticks and limit labels to at least 12 px at rendered size. Use round-number y ticks from 0 for non-negative quantities. Label the peak and limit values on the plot. Give the FBWA-to-cruise band and the event lines enough contrast to see in both themes. Take series colours from the categorical series tokens, not the status tokens.
4. Plain-language copy. Remove `t_ge85_s`, "V", "dl/dr", "clevis cheek -1" and other raw keys. Define UR / LR / UL / LL in words and mark them on the 3-D. Gloss each ArduPilot mode in plain words (for example "QHOVER: hover hold"). Give pitch use as a percent of +/-15 deg with the limit as a percent. Rewrite tilt as "now 0.07 deg; limit 3 deg on the final tilt, which is 0.04 deg (pass)". Put a unit on every figure. Put "designed contact" next to the 0.000 mm clearance. Merge the Events and Autopilot modes rows into one time-ordered list or the timing strip, on one clock.
5. Craft-floor refusals. Replace the Unicode check, cross and double-triangle glyphs with the site's drawn SVG icons or with words ("pass", "fail", "Previous event", "Next event"). Keep mono to numbers only (the run name, sign-convention notes and stage caption go to Plex Sans). Make readout values at least `--text-sm` and visually stronger than their labels.
6. Affordance and keyboard. Set `.rp-btn` and `.rp-chip` to at least 44 px tall (REVIEW-STYLE). Set `aria-valuetext` on each slider with value and unit. On Play/Sweep, use either the label swap or `aria-pressed`, not both. Add a visible "Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom" hint and a keyboard-reachable "Reset view" button on each 3-D stage. Say in the caption that the slider is the keyboard path for scrubbing the plots.
7. Reduced motion. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, hold the propellers still (or step them), make the linkage Sweep stop after one pass instead of ping-ponging, and make chip and event seeks jump. Capture one reduced-motion frame as evidence.
8. 400 px layout and the linkage panel. At 520 px and below, put a compact strip of three key readouts directly under the slider (flight: altitude, airspeed, pitch; landing: load factor, stroke, tilt; linkage: angle, hinge moment, clearance). Shorten the select option text to about 40 characters, with the full run name in the readout. Move the stage caption below the canvas. Fix the clipped "elevon" label. Remove the panel fill and border from the linkage schematics and size the side view to its content.

## keep
Keep the warm-paper and graphite grounds, the square hairline readout list, the real CAD posed from recorded samples, and the honest provenance (stated once, not three times).

## Fixes applied

Applied 2026-11-24 (T-105 continuation). Edited: `redesign/site/replay.js` (already in the tree from the earlier pass; reviewed, no change needed), `redesign/site/explorer.css`, `redesign/site/make_replays_view.py` (earlier pass). Regenerated `app.html`, `index.html` and the replay assets. No simulation, limit or verdict logic changed. Presentation only: pitch use shown as a percent (0.651 shown as 65.1 %).

1. Pass/fail values: applied. The pass/fail table (`#rp-f-pf`) sits full width under `.rp-main`, with Line / Value / Limit / Result columns. No sideways scroll at 1280 or 400 (check (c)).
2. Verdict and hierarchy: applied. One `sh-v` verdict line under each replay head (flight, landing, linkage). The bold summary paragraphs and all KB byte counts are removed. The "playback, not a new simulation" line appears once, in the intro (check_replays `label_count` = 1).
3. Plot readability: applied in part. Each SVG takes its own grid cell width (`.rp-plots` is now `auto-fill`, and `.rp-plot svg` has `width: 100%`). Rendered ticks and limit labels are 12 px or more (check (d): min 12, none below). Y ticks are round numbers and start at 0 for non-negative quantities. Peak and limit values are written on the plots. The FBWA-to-cruise band uses `--accent-line` at 0.55 opacity with an outline. Series colours use accent and text tones with distinct dashes. Deviation: `explorer.css` has no categorical series tokens, so the status tokens were dropped rather than replaced with a categorical palette. The band and event-line contrast in dark mode was not measured numerically.
4. Plain-language copy: applied. Raw keys replaced with words (for example "Airspeed", "Left elevon angle (trailing edge down +)", "left clevis cheek and horn blade"). Pads UR, LR, UL, LL defined in the caption and labelled on the 3-D. ArduPilot modes glossed (for example "QHOVER (hover, altitude hold)"). Pitch use in percent of +/-15 deg with the limit in percent. Tilt rewritten as "now X deg; limit 3 deg on the final tilt, which is Y deg (pass)". Units on the peaks. "designed contact" beside the clearance value. Events and autopilot modes merged into one time-ordered Timeline on one clock.
5. Craft-floor refusals: applied. Check and cross glyphs replaced with the words "pass" and "FAIL". Event buttons read "Previous event" and "Next event". Mono kept for numbers only (`.rp-read dd.txt` and captions use Plex Sans). Readout values are `--text-md` medium weight, larger than their `--text-sm` labels. Remaining Unicode in the UI is the dagger for clock-offset events, the math signs for limits, and degree, middle-dot and dash characters, none of them check, cross or triangle glyphs.
6. Affordance and keyboard: applied. `.rp-btn` and `.rp-chip` at least 44 px (check (e): min 44, none below). `aria-valuetext` on all three sliders (check (f)). Play/Pause is a label only, with no `aria-pressed`. Drag-to-orbit hint and a Reset view button on each flight and landing stage (the linkage has no 3-D stage). Captions say the slider is the keyboard path for scrubbing the plots. Deviation: the drag hint is text only, not an icon.
7. Reduced motion: applied, and checked (g). Propellers held still. The linkage Sweep runs one pass and stops (verified: stopped at index 84 of 84, button label reset to "Sweep", still after 3 s). Chip and event seeks jump (instant set, no tween).
8. 400 px layout and linkage panel: applied in code. At 520 px and below, a three-readout strip sits under the slider (`.rp-key`: flight altitude, airspeed, pitch; landing load factor, stroke, tilt; linkage elevon angle, hinge moment, clearance). Option text is short (about 35 characters). The stage caption sits below the canvas. The schematic panel fill and border are removed. Not verified separately: the clipped "elevon" label in the side view (no check covers it; the side view was widened by 20 % in the earlier pass), and the schematic label sizes (about 9 px in SVG units). Remaining deviation: the schematic horn stroke still uses the status token `--st-modelled`.
