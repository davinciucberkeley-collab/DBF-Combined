# Registry and ledger audit, 2026-11-18 (T-011)

Scope: every actionable instruction in `docs/MASTER_PROJECT_SPEC.md` (Parts 1–13, the 20 final rules,
the immediate execution instructions) and `docs/spec-amendments.md` (AM-001), checked against
`docs/requirements.md` (99 rows) and `docs/task-ledger.md` (48 tasks). Read-only audit: no file other than
this one was edited, and the spec was not touched.

Method: each spec line was mapped to the requirement row(s) whose Requirement, Interpretation or
Acceptance text covers it. "Covered" means the row's acceptance can be checked for that item. Items that a
row mentions only through an optional "e.g." or only in its interpretation are listed as partial.

## Verdict

- (a) Instructions with no covering requirement, or only implicit coverage: **21** (A1 and A3 have no covering row; the other 19 are partial or implicit).
- (b) Requirements that lump unrelated items that cannot be verified independently: **37**.
- (c) Requirements with no task, wrong parents, or a task whose acceptance does not test its parent: **11** (C1–C11), plus 2 audit-tool blind spots (C12–C13).
- (d) Tasks or requirements marked VERIFIED COMPLETE or READY FOR REVIEW without real evidence: **2** rows (T-009, R-TRK-01). No task is VERIFIED COMPLETE.

The registry is not yet a sound basis for R-TRK-01 to be READY FOR REVIEW. Part 13 has no rows, and the
audit tool passes anyway because it only checks task-to-parent links.
