# T-101 elevon servo scout (2026-11-24): replacements for the ES3054HV

Role: scout (sonnet). Question: which servo restores CL-A-SERVO-FACTOR / CL-A-SERVO-COLD to >= 1.5 at 7.2 V with the wider elevon (D-250), keeping R2 >= 1.0 at 6.0 V. Not a decision; the conductor picks.
Prices, stock, specs "checked 2026-11-24" are from pages opened in this run (list at the end). "—" = not found. Values marked (snippet) came from a search result I could not open and are NOT sources.

## 1. Result in one paragraph

Two servos in the ES3054HV envelope class (AGFRC A20CLS, KST X10 Mini) clear 1.5 on torque with margin (factor 1.83-1.90, stall 0.65 N m) and keep R2 above 1.5. No US vendor page for either could be opened (HTTP 429 from the shops), so the US price and stock are "—". Bigger servos (KST X10 V8.0, Savox SV-1250MG) pass easily but do not fit the pocket and break the $2,000 limit at the opened prices. No digital HV micro of 9-15 g reaches 0.53 N m (best found: ES08MA II HV, 12 g, 0.167 N m at 7.2 V). Two problems are not about the servo choice: (1) every stall-load row of AR-004 scales linearly with stall torque, so a 0.65 N m servo raises stall rod force by 1.64x on rows that are already marginal or failing; (2) R2 is computed at hinge 0.2102 N m in `linkage_loads_check.py` but the claims use 0.2174 N m; at 0.2174 the current servo gives R2 0.961, below 1.0.

## 2. Baseline and formulas (from the repo)

Claim code, `redesign/qp/A/proofs/claims_a2_struct.py`:
- CALC-A-SERVO, lines 237-243. `req = load_factor x (hinge_vd_nm + preload_nm + damper_slew_nm)`; `req_cold` uses `damper_slew_cold_nm`.
  - `factor_25c = stall_72_nm / req` (equal arms).
  - `factor_0c_real_arms = stall_72_nm x horn_mm / arm_mm / req_cold`.
  - `ratio_to_hinge = stall_72_nm / hinge_vd_nm`.
- Claims CL-A-SERVO-FACTOR (line 247, output `factor_25c`) and CL-A-SERVO-COLD (line 249, output `factor_0c_real_arms`), limit `req.servo_factor_min` = 1.5 (`params-A.json` line 145).
- Inputs (`params-A.json` lines 1021-1075): stall_72_nm 0.397, hinge_vd_nm 0.2174, preload 0.0093, damper_slew 0.0098, damper_slew_cold 0.0196, load_factor 1.5, arm_mm 7.0, horn_mm 7.54.
- Reproduced: req = 1.5 x (0.2174 + 0.0093 + 0.0098) = 0.35475 N m (the 1.5 load factor is already in req), req_cold 0.36945 N m; ES3054HV gives 1.119 and 1.157, as in the T-101 chord audit s7.4.
- Required stall torque at 7.2 V for a factor of 1.5: 25 C needs >= 1.5 x 0.35475 = 0.532 N m; cold (real arms) needs >= 1.5 x 0.3693 x 7.0 / 7.54 = 0.514 N m. The 25 C claim governs: 0.532 N m = 5.43 kgf cm.

R2 code, `redesign/qp/A/mechanism/linkage_loads_check.py`, `servo_speed()` lines 293-322 (stored result `servo_speed_margin_r2` 1.015, `params-A.json` line 1365).
- `tau_load = g_end x (HINGE_NM + PRELOAD_NM + DAMPER_SLEW_NM)`; `omega = OMEGA_NL x (1 - tau_load / tau_stall_6V)`; `rate = g_end x omega`; R2 = rate / (2 pi x 2.2 Hz x 11 deg = 152.05 deg/s). OMEGA_NL = 60 deg / (6 V no-load time). g_end = 0.9297 (run from the module).
- HINGE_NM = 0.146 x (73.50/61.25)^2 = 0.2102 (line 59), NOT the claim's 0.2174. I reproduced 1.017 (stored 1.015; difference is rounding / state). With 0.2174 the ES3054HV gives 0.961.
- The model uses the 6.0 V stall and 6.0 V no-load time as a published pair; the 7.2 V speed is not used.

Interpolation for 7.2 V: linear between published 6.0 V and 7.4 V (or 8.4 V) torque, the same method the BOM uses for the ES3054HV (EST). All 7.2 V values below are EST.

## 5. What else changes with a stronger servo (not in the brief, but decisive)

- AR-004 stall rows are linear in the stall torque (`linkage_loads_check.py` `stall_84` comment: "every check below is linear in F"). The stall rod force at 7.2 V goes by 0.653 / 0.397 = 1.64x (A20CLS) or 1.63x (X10 Mini); for the 0.532 N m minimum it would be 1.34x. In the T-101 s7.2 record, the rows that pass only after U6a (Z-bend leg at stall, shear-out at stall, keeper at stall 18.85 vs 20 MPa) would fail again, and the horn bearing at stall (24.8 MPa vs 20, already FAIL) gets worse. The rib-in-foam, elevon torsion and FOAM-SERVO stall claims also scale. Capping the stall force (servo end-point or current limit) is in the s4 option list of the chord audit and has not been scouted.
- The D-243 / TP-3 and CL-A-FOAM-SERVO rows (servo stalled into the stop, shear in the pocket walls) also scale with stall torque.
- Stall current and BEC draw: "—" on every page opened (the Kakute servo rail is 3 A continuous, 4.8 A peak per SERVO.md s2). Faster, stronger servos draw more; measure at the bench (A-P18).
- Free play and horn stiffness K are "—" for every candidate.
- R2 hinge mismatch: see s2.

## 6. Findings, without a pick

- Torque needed is 0.532 N m at 7.2 V (25 C claim governs). Only AGFRC A20CLS and KST X10 Mini are within roughly one pocket dimension of the ES3054HV and pass with margin (1.83-1.90); A20CLS passes R2 with the most margin and is the smallest (23.1 x 11.9 x 27.4) and lightest (+1.0 g for the pair).
- The A20CLS and X10 Mini US price and stock are not verified. If the price is above $30.31 per servo (the BOM holds at $2,000 only below it, with a $10.71 shipment) the BOM limit fails unless another line is cut. The two prices seen in snippets ($49.99, $50.00) would fail.
- The X10 V8.0 and SV-1250MG fit neither the pocket nor the budget, and are back-ordered / sold out.
- "None fits" is not the finding for torque: A20CLS fits with at most a 0.7 mm y change. It is the finding for the 9-15 g digital HV micro class and for the BOM at the snippet prices.
- Before a pick: AGFRC A20CLS case drawing (ear and spline offset), a verified US vendor, and the stall-force consequence in s5.
