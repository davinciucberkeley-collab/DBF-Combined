Rev 3 is a sound engineering response to the second review:

M-2 are text and graph edits. With M-1, M-2 and m-1 done, rev 3 can be committed carrying the five FAILs as gated open

## Findings

### M-1 Major: the five FAIL claims have no path to closure, so the stated gate cannot close them

**Evidence.**
- `redesign/proofs/build_graph.py` `_st_claim` (≈ l. 250–290) marks a claim VERIFIED only if `passed and not
  n.get("fails")`. A claim with a negative margin stays FAILING even when a test that verifies it has passed.
- The five claims (`claims_a2_struct.py`, new block after l. 167) are `stored(...)` analysis values compared with the EST
  allowables. A passing E1 coupon changes neither number, so the claims stay FAIL and A-P13 stays strict-failing for
  good. "Gated on E1" is therefore true in the prose but cannot be expressed in the graph.
- For CL-A-ELEVON-TORSION-FOAM this is not a technicality. The claim is foam-only by definition, so it passes only if
  the measured foam shear is ≥ 504 kPa: about 5 × the 103 kPa data-sheet compressive strength of the same foam. No
  plausible M11 result closes it.
- None of the new claims is linked to a test node. `claims_a0_base.py:133` T-A-ELEVON-E1 lists only the flutter claims
  and CL-A-SERVO-COLD. The coupon exists only in the claims' `how` text (and `CHAIN.md`).
- The second step of the coupon, "then the 51 N stall force (record what yields first)", is not pass/fail. So nothing
  defines closure of CL-A-HORN-RIB-FOAM-STALL or CL-A-HORN-BEARING-STALL.
- The "bench stall test (5 s into the stop)" named in `analysis-records` Open item 2 does not appear in
  `BUILD-GUIDE-A.md`. D5b only says to set the endpoints short of the stop (l. ~1840).

**Required fix.**
- Add a planned test node (extend T-A-ELEVON-E1, or add T-A-HORN-RIB) that verifies test-form claims. For example,
  CL-A-HORN-RIB-COUPON = coupon load held without damage / required load, limit ≥ 1, and an elevon-section torsion
  coupon claim. Keep the analysis claims as the documented "not shown by analysis" record. State in the record that they
  are superseded by the test claims once the tests run, so A-P13 has a defined route to pass.
- Give the 51 N step and the bench stall test pass/fail criteria. For example: 51 N for 5 s, no crack, no hole
  elongation over 0.1 mm, set < 0.5°. Otherwise declare the stall/jam fault outside A-P13, in the way A-P20 is "declared
  not survivable", and give the reason.
- Write the bench stall test into the build guide (D5b) or the E1 procedure.

### M-2 Major: the coupon load (25.6 N = 1.5 × hold) is below the 2.156 × hold basis the claims are judged on, and the fallback does not cover the elevon-torsion FAIL

**Evidence.**
- The claims use KP = 1.5 × 1.15 × 1.25 = 2.156 (`linkage_loads_check.py` FACTORS; `fea_arm.py`). The 1.15 (printed) and
  1.25 (bonded) factors cover part-to-part and bond-quality scatter, and one coupon does not remove them.
- The coupon in `claims_a2_struct.py` COUPON and `analysis-records` Open item 1 is pulled to 1.5 × 17.1 = 25.6 N. At the
  claims' own basis the load is 2.156 × 17.05 = **36.8 N**.
- Coupon boundary conditions: both ends clamped 50 mm either side of the horn. Each side then carries 25.6 × 9.155 / 2 =
  0.117 N m, against 1.5 × 0.0746 = 0.112 N m outboard in flight, so the torsion is about right. But the clamps sit 50 mm
  from the rib, where in flight the elevon continues for about 160 mm. State that the result is local only.
- The named fallback, "a 0.4 mm ply or CF doubler over the rib on both skins", fixes the rib introduction only. It does
  nothing for CL-A-ELEVON-TORSION-FOAM, which applies to the whole elevon span beside the horn.

**Required fix.**
- Set the coupon hold at 36.8 N for 30 s. Alternatively, log in CHANGE-LOG why a test may close the claim at 1.5 ×, for
  example by testing ≥ 3 coupons and taking the lowest.
- Name a fallback for the elevon torsion as well (see M-3).

### M-3 Major: no trade row for a spanwise torsion member in the elevon, the option that addresses the elevon-torsion FAIL

**Evidence.**
- The rev 3 diagnosis (`engineering-decisions.md`, trade table D-212 rev 3) is that the foam-only elevon cannot carry the
  VD hinge moment in torsion, and that the tape skin carries it.
- Rows A–I all change how the horn meets the foam locally. None changes how the elevon carries torsion along its span.
- The usual RC answer for a long foam control surface driven from one horn is a torsion member: a torque rod or tube
  along the hinge line, or a hard leading-edge strip, with the horn clamped or bonded to it. That turns both the
  rib-introduction FAIL and the elevon-torsion FAIL into checks against a cited tube or strip allowable, and it spreads
  the horn load along the span.
- Rough size, as a scratch estimate with densities and strengths recalled (not looked up):
  - CF tube 3 × 2 mm along the 310 mm elevon: about 1.9 g per side. J = 6.4 mm⁴, τ = 0.467 N m × 1.5 mm / 6.4 = 110 MPa
    at stall, marginal for a pultruded tube in torsion.
  - 4 × 3 mm tube: about 3.3 g per side, τ ≈ 54 MPa at stall.
- The tube sits on the hinge line, so it hardly changes the static balance. Its mass and its effect on the flutter
  inputs (D-187 v2 balance, FIXES2 s1) would still need checking.

**Required fix.**
- Add a trade row (mass, cited tube allowable, flutter-input change, build).
- At minimum, name it as the fallback for CL-A-ELEVON-TORSION-FOAM if the E1 coupon or the elevon-section test fails.

## Engineering assessment (brief item 3)

1. **Rib model.**
   - Inputs: E_PETG 2.1 GPa EST; foam E 5–10 MPa, ν 0.1 EST; l 2–8 mm EST. Only G/l matters, so the six cases are three
     distinct foundations.
   - λL is 2.7–4.5, so the rib is not rigid and the choice of a flexible model over a weld-group formula is correct. The
     peak sits at the loaded front edge.
   - Validity limits are stated (Winkler, no shear coupling). Unstated: the free `l`, shear deformation, and the load
     reference (m-10).
   - The rigid limit (126.2 kPa) bounds the family from below and is already 1.5 × the unfactored allowable. **The FAIL is
     robust; the model is adequate for "not shown, test it".**
2. **51.0 N.** Correct, and reproduced: static stall over the minimum lever at +21°. The factor choice is defensible but
   rests on an EST torque ceiling (m-8).
3. **Bearing and blade.** Numbers reproduce. The blade passes by about 3 ×. Bearing fails at stall on a conservative EST
   20 MPa; a bushing fixes it by design (m-6).
4. **Free-play budget.** Arithmetic correct. It leaves out servo backlash and the cheek holes ("-"). It is a control
   item with the damper in place, but the FIXES2 probe level must follow the built value (m-7).
5. **Elevon foam-only torsion (504 kPa vs 86 kPa).** This is the most important result in rev 3, and it is not a linkage
   result. By the project's own basis, the foam-only elevon cannot carry its own VD hinge moment: 234 kPa unfactored,
   2.7 × the allowable. One or more of three things is true:
   - **The 86 kPa allowable is a guess.** ASM-A-FOAM-ALLOW is the old 100 kPa scaled by 103/120; the FOAMULAR 150 sheet
     has no shear row. It may be low, but not by 5 ×.
   - **The load is conservative.** 0.146 N m at VD and 20° means |C_h| = 0.146 / (½ × 1.20 × 24² × 0.31 × 0.06125²) ≈
     **0.36**: a high hinge-moment coefficient (judgement, not cited).
   - **The tape skin is primary structure.** The skin then needs its own allowable and test. A closed-cell shear flow of
     152 N/m at hold is small for tape, but the tape-to-foam bond and creep are "-".

   The correct consequences:
   - Treat it as an elevon-level (WNG-006) open item, with an elevon-section torsion test at the factored load and the
     M11 foam shear block.
   - Read the 0.86 rib/elevon ratio as "the attachment is no worse than the surface it drives", not as reassurance.
   - Carry a named design fallback (M-3).

   The claim is correctly labelled FAIL. It is gated on the wrong closure (M-1).
6. **Buildability and drawings.** The CAD, drawings and build text agree on dimensions:
   - slot 2.2 mm, x 2.4–55.1 mm aft, y 191.5 mm;
   - rib 0.2 mm inside the skins;
   - keeper z 8.7 / 10.2 / 11.0.

   The rib is buildable with a slot guide. The tail is fragile (m-14). The drawing omits the print orientation (m-5). The
   keeper is buildable, but its clamp joint and bridge are not checked (m-4). The horn mass moves aft by about 2 mm,
   about 2 g·mm: negligible for the balance.
