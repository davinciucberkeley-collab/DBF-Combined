### 1.1 Pad model: the rate term carries both the peaks and the stroke reserve (`rc_levers.py` V1, `rc_grid2.py`)

In the wide cases the struck feet move at up to about 1.9 m/s, so c v is up to about 17 N. That is the 41-47 N peak above the 30 N plateau, as T-076c says (confirmed). ### 1.2 Inertia: uniform scaling and the point-mass wing (`rc_levers.py` V2-V4)
- `landing_a.mass_props` scales every ITEMS lump by k = 1,640 / 1,271.04 = **1.290**. ITEMS sums to 213 + 125 + 353.04 + 64 + 46 + 20 + 270 + 110 + 30 + 40 = 1,271.04 g. So the motors become 113.9 g against 88.26 g (`landing_a.py` l.17) and the pack 348 g against 270-275 g (A-D4). Real motors and packs do not grow; O4b growth sits in the printed parts, the hub, the glass skin and the allowance.
- The wing (213 g) is one point at y = 0. The model's Izz is 68.4 g m2 at 1.64 kg, against FE 108.5 g m2 (`T076c_data/step2_d1_stdout.txt` l.2) and CAD 71.8 g m2 at **1.273 kg** (`sitl/cad_mass.json` `I_cad_axes_kgm2`). At CAD mass ITEMS gives 0.0625 x 1.273 / 1.50 = 53 g m2, so ITEMS is low by about 26 % even before growth.

### 1.3 The demand hardly depends on the sink-rate case (`rc_levers.py` V0 per case)
Sleeve-exit moment (N m): hard 10 wide + drift 6.80, hard 7/7 7.08, hard 16 wide + drift 6.92; **SITL-record cases (sink 0.30-0.34 m/s, card-allowed): Tb_h5 wide 6.21, Z5_b45 wide 5.93, Ta_h5 wide 5.54, Z5_gb45 wide 4.89**; normal 0.5 m/s wide + drift 4.63. In every case the struck feet reach the plateau: SITL cases 34.6-41.1 N with 5.6-8.6 mm of stroke. **The root of the demand is the per-foot force cap (plateau plus rate term) acting at y 0.38 m, not the 1.2 m/s case.** Removing the hard cases (which I may not do anyway) would buy 13-31 %.

### 1.4 Arm-tube rows: what the FE value is and what beam theory says (`rc_hand.py`; `fea_hub_c6.py` l.513-577; T-076c `tubes_stdout.txt`)
- **Partition.** `t076_main.land_combos_sim` l.68-101: A = the whole hub-side wrench (ITEMS 2, 3, 4, 6: motors, arms, hub, **pack**) as d'Alembert loads on the four motor points, carried through the stubs. B = arm group (ITEMS 2, 3) through the stubs, hub + pack on the hub aft face. The pack tray plate is on the hub ("HUB_X1 (tray face)", `common.py` l.42), so **B is the physical path for the arm tubes**. A is the documented bound for the hub body (T-076 s1: "bound for the sleeves and block").
- **The "shear" row.** `tube_stress` returns hypot(tau_s,r, tau_s,theta), that is through-thickness (interlaminar) and in-plane shear combined, and `evaluate` takes the **maximum** over all tube Gauss points more than 15 mm from the stub ends. T-076c's governing points sit at s = 24.9-25.2 mm. **The CF doubler (GEO `DBL`=1, `DBL_S0` 11, `DBL_S1` 25, as in the CAD, `common.py` l.50 "doublers s 11-25") ends 1 mm past the sleeve end (L_SLV 24)**: two stiffness steps 1 mm apart, both tied, square-ended and unfilleted.
- **Beam theory at s = 25 mm** (partition B, worst over 21 cases, with torsion from the 12 mm motor-point offset): sigma **80.5 MPa** (RF 2.52 RB / 1.68 x1.5 against 350 / KF), tau **2.6 MPa** (RF 6.7 RB). The FE gives axial 114-127 MPa (B), a 1.4-1.6 ratio that fits a sleeve-edge concentration. It gives "shear" **18.3-20.2 MPa (B), 7-8 times beam theory.** That is the singular tied-edge field, judged by its raw maximum. The hub body is judged under the escalation s2.4 rule (0.5 mm exclusion, convergence, hand rows); the tube rows are not ("not asked for", T-076c s4).
- **The bond row is real.** It is a mean over each arm's bonded length (the lap-shear quantity). Hand check: each tube carries the arm-group couple across the hub, about F x R = 7.8 N m per side (7.08 x 0.276 / 0.251). Transferred by axial shear alone over L 24 mm: tau0 = M / (pi r^2 L) = 7,800 / (pi x 36 x 24) = 2.87 MPa, mean |tau| 1.83 MPa. The FE's 1.0 MPa mean (part of the load goes by bearing and pins) is the same order. **The bond needs either more length or a measured allowable.**

Hard 10 wide + drift 0.70: Mz_arm 17.93 (+2 %), exit M 6.96 (+2 %). Hard 10 narrow + drift 0.70: **stroke 19.6 mm (reserve 0.4 mm)**. Hard 7/7 + 0.70 diagonal: exit M 6.95. ### 1.6 Factors
KF = 1.5 x 1.15 and KP = KF x 1.25 (`fea_arm.py` l.96) are the project's ultimate x material / fitting factors, correctly applied in both the FE and the hand rows. The x1.5 floor: T-071b EST (`landing_centre_a.py` l.94), kept by the escalation as max(DAF from D-1, 1.5). D-1's hub Mz DAF (case-max 1.21 against the sim RB) is below 1.5. For the arm tubes no DAF against the sim exists. The D-1 dynamic wrench, applied directly, gives RF 0.63-0.65 (A). That lies between RB and x1.5, so x1.5 is the binding rule for the tubes. The D-1 forcing is one-way and has a 1.25 ms contact onset: 1.5 mm ramp at 1.2 m/s, with the c v step at first contact. That onset is what excites the 30-80 Hz content (escalation V7) and the arm flapping (Fz x 3.99). It is a model property that the drop test must confirm. **No factor is wrong; the demand they multiply is uncertain by more than the margins being chased.**

## 4. Recommendation and implementation scope

### 4.1 The demand fix (one)
**Define the landing demand once, from a specified and measured pad, on a corrected mass model, and judge the whole path on it.**
- Pad specification (target, to be confirmed by test): plateau 30 N per foot, equivalent zeta <= 0.02 (peak foot force <= 37 N at 1.2 m/s in the model), usable stroke >= 24 mm (free foam about 36 mm at the present 2/3 usable ratio, EST). X_GROUND +6 mm, so the pad edge moves out 3.5 mm to keep the 30.5 deg A-P9 design target (z edge 0.1985 m). That adds about 7 mm to the z extent, so check it against the 0.40 m box proxy and the measured trunk (T-078, height still to measure). Δmass +2 to +4 g EST.
