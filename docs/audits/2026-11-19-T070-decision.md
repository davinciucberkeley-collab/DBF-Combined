## 3. Wing: is the failure real, and the fix

### 3.1 Load path (the question asked)
- **Torsion (My, about the span).** The feet push along x at z ≈ +-0.18 m on the fin. The difference between the pad pair, times about 0.177 m, is a pitch moment that has to rotate the aircraft. Most of the pitch inertia sits on the hub side: the motors at z +-0.156 on the hub arms (about 37 % of Iyy, EST: 4 x about 75 g x (0.156^2 + 0.09^2)) and the other fin. Only the loaded fin's own inertia (about 20-25 %, V7) relieves the torque before it enters the wing. The fin gives the moment to the wing at two points: the rib-saddle on the spar (rib bending → spar torsion) and the root block on the trailing edge (→ core torsion). Both end in the wing. The V struts lie in the z = 0 plane and are torsionally negligible (V6). The bridge takes the torque only at y ≈ 0. **The full torque, less about 20 %, is carried by the wing from y 0.38 to the centre, as T-069 states.**
- **In-plane bending (Mz).** Outboard of the strut clamp (y 0.285-0.38) the wing carries it alone, with 13.3 N m at the fin station. Inboard, the strut-and-spar truss carries the chordwise-force part, so the 23.3 and 38.7 N m that T-069 applies at y 0.228 and 0.060 are over-stated. The inboard demand is roughly the moment at the clamp node: about 13.3 + 66 N x 0.095 m ≈ 20 N m, EST, where 66 N is the chordwise force implied by T-069's Mz increase between y 0.38 and 0.228. That is still more than the 12x10 spar plus the XPS can take on the foam tensile EST, so P16 also needs a structural change, but a small one (s3.3).
- **Skin-less bound.** It is a bound, not the design case, but crediting the tape does not rescue it (F-2): there is no product, no thickness and no allowable; the seams are PSA lap joints; and the centre cell is open. **The 2.5x gap between demand (9.63 N m) and the ductile-sum capacity (3.76 N m) is larger than any of the EST uncertainties.** In SITL-envelope landings (3-4.5 N m) it still fails against the elastic-share capacity of 1.45 N m.

### 3.3 Skin hand calc (scratch, formulas)
Cell ahead of the hinge: A = 3.393e-3 m2, s = 0.384 m (V5). GJ_skin = 4 A^2 G t / s. Torque shared by GJ with the spar (3.162 N m2) and the foam (3.016 N m2, T-069; it includes elevon foam, so it is an upper bound). q = T_skin / (2A). Skin tau = KF q / t. Foam max shear = KP T_f x 56,429 Pa/N m (T-069 Prandtl `tau_unit`). Laminate EST: 48 g/m2 cloth with 40 g/m2 resin, t 0.054 mm; G(+-45) = E / (2(1 + nu)) = 15 / 2.26 = 6.6 GPa; G(0/90) 3.0 GPa; E along the fibres 15 GPa (wet lay-up, EST). Results are in the table above. **P16 with the skin:** the skin adds EI of about 660 N m2 (E 8.9 GPa spanwise for +-45, I_skin ≈ 1.38e-3 t). At 20-23 N m the foam strain at the hinge face is about 2.4-2.8e-3, which is still about 0.7-0.85 of the RF on the 129 kPa / 30 MPa EST tensile strain. **One pultruded CF strip on the cove face** (about 3 mm2, about 3.5 g over 0.76 m; EA d^2 ≈ 120e9 x 3e-6 x 0.12^2 ≈ 5,000 N m2, EST) brings the foam strain down by roughly 4x. Phase 1 sizes it.

## 5. Verdict

**Concept A at its present propulsion cannot credibly reach ≤ 1,470 g, or ≤ 1,478 g with the window spent.** The credible prediction after everything the engineering team can do without money is **about 1,620 g**. With Dillon's pack and sheet choices it is **about 1,550-1,570 g**. With credits not yet earned it is about 1,520 g. By V8 the time-domain window at those masses would be roughly +-0.3 % to +-1.6 % (EST), against the +-5 % floor of A-P3. The aircraft would not transition with margin. This is the K09-L pattern the QP-1 board warned about (BOARD s0 "lesson carried"): the board's estimate of about 1.23-1.37 kg is now about 1.55-1.62 kg predicted. **I relax no limit.** The 1,470 g gate, A-P3 +-5 %, A-P9 30 deg, KF 1.725 / KP 2.156 and the 1.2 m/s hard cases all stand.
