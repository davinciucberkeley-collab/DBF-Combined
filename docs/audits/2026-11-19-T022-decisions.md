## 2. Antenna-length source check (65 mm)

The 65 mm figure is sourced, but only weakly:
- `redesign/review/QP-5/CAD-FIDELITY-AUDIT.md:94` (web pages read 2026-11-15) cites readymaderc 85670 (https://www.readymaderc.com/products/details/85670-radiomaster-rp3-expresslrs-2-4ghz-nano-receiver-v2): "2 x 65 mm T antennas".
- `redesign/team/F5/RC-LINK.md:15` and `redesign/team/F5/claims.json:50` give the same figure from three retailer listings that agree. The maker page returned HTTP 429, and the status there is UNVERIFIED.
- `AM/electrical.py:903` labels it "65 mm V" but gives no URL on that line.

Gaps in the source:
1. Nobody read the maker's own page.
2. No source says what "65 mm" measures: the coax lead from the U.FL connector, or the overall length including the element.
3. The listing calls them "T antennas", but the size of the T element (crossbar span and thickness) is not in the repo. The CAD draws a straight leg with an EST OD of 1.1 mm and no T.

Source needed: the RadioMaster RP3 V2 product page or user manual, with a dated URL, giving the antenna lead length, element type and element dimensions. If that cannot be found, measure the antennas on arrival (bench item, same class as the servo ears). Until then, keep the 65 mm length as "V (retailer, UNVERIFIED)" and mark the T-element span as "-".

## 4. Decisions

### Q1 Antennas: a variant of the "rearrange" option, chosen over (a), (b) and (c)

**Decision.** Draw each antenna at its real 65 mm length (V, retailer, UNVERIFIED). Lay the two antennas in a 90 degree V on the wing upper surface, each straight at ±45 degrees to +x, taped to the skin and following its contour. The RX does not move.
- Antenna 1 (the outboard exit, y -0.050) runs aft and outboard.
- Antenna 2 (the inboard exit, y -0.042) runs aft and inboard.
- Each leaves the RX aft edge (x 0.124) along +x for about 1.5 mm and then turns 45 degrees with a smooth bend (D-199 smooth-wire convention; radius at least 4 mm, the project wire rule; the coax-specific minimum is "-").

**Rejected options.**
- (c), keep 50 mm: the length is not unknown, it is sourced. Drawing 50 mm would knowingly draw the wrong part, and it keeps F1 and F2. Rejected.
- (b), move the RX 15 mm forward: it rests on the undefined 65 mm, keeps F1 and F2, and moves E-HRN-09, the RX pad and the build sequence. It is kept as the fallback only.
- (a), fold the last 15 mm back: this is an arbitrary assumed shape over the cover and keeps F2. Rejected.

**Reasoning against the hard constraints.**
- Real length, real routing:
  - The lay angle is an installation choice, which we own and log; it is not a vendor shape we would be inventing.
  - The geometry follows the skin, as the aircraft will actually be built (fixes F1).
  - The T element is marked "-" rather than invented.
- Reach (computed from section 1):

| Lay | Tip x (m) | Margin to hinge (XH 0.18375) |
|---|---|---|
| Straight at 45° | 0.124 + 0.065 x 0.7071 = 0.1700 | 13.8 mm |
| With the 1.5 mm stub and the bend | about 0.171 | about 12.7 mm |

  - The tips never reach the moving elevon. ELEV_Y0 = 0.04, so the inboard part of the elevon starts at the hinge.
  - The lay keeps a 5 mm margin for any straight length up to (0.18375 - 0.005 - 0.124) / 0.7071 = 77 mm. That makes it robust to the "65 mm" ambiguity.
  - A T crossbar at the tip adds 0.707 x its half-span in x. The 5 mm margin holds for a half-span of up to 12.4 mm.
- RF (fixes F2):
  - The antennas are 90 degrees apart, and the tips are about 100 mm apart.
  - The carbon spar (axis x 0.060, OD 12) is at least 58 mm ahead of any antenna point.
  - The arms and hub are at x about -0.069.
  - The ESC aft edge (x 0.117) is at least 50 mm from the tips, where the active element is.
  - All of these exceed the about 31 mm quarter wave of `ASM-F5-ANT-CLEAR`.
- Clearances checked on paper:
  - Antenna 2 reaches the cover's \|y\| 26.5 only at x about 0.140, which is aft of COV_X1 = 0.1265, so it clears the cover.
  - Antenna 1 ends at y about -0.096, far from the servo pocket (\|y\| 0.1775..0.2225).
  - The pitot, I2C and servo grooves lie under the skin.
  - The collision gate confirms all of this.
- Failure-resolution ladder:
  - rung 1 (subtract): not applicable, because the antennas are vendor parts;
  - rung 2 (modify geometry): shortening a vendor part is a purchase;
  - rung 3 (rearrange): **chosen**, because it only re-lays the antenna polylines inside AVN-003;
  - rung 4 (change approach), the fallback (b): use it only if the measured T element or the collision gate defeats the V;
  - rung 5 (add material): none.

**Fallback (engineering, pre-authorised).** Use the fallback if either of these happens:
- the collision gate finds an interference that cannot be fixed by nudging the angle within 40-55 degrees;
- on arrival the T half-span is more than 12.4 mm (or the overall reach, including the element, is more than 77 mm).

Then also move the RX forward (option b, by the amount the measurement requires). In that case Q1 and E-HRN-09 must be done together.

**Mass/CG.** AVN-003 keeps its catalog mass of 4.6 g (V, with antennas). Only the part's internal geometry changes, so the CG change is well below 0.01 mm. For comparison, option (b) would have moved the CG 4.6 x 15 / 1273 = 0.054 mm forward.

### Q2 Hinge tape: joint only (as the report recommended), with an explicit mark

**Decision.** Do not draw a tape solid. Keep the CT-TAPE-HINGE joint, and state in the edge note and the not-drawn list that the hinge tape is a film whose product and thickness are "-".

**Reasoning.**
- No product has been chosen: BOM-A R05 lumps "hinge tape" in a $20 EST line. Any thickness or width would therefore be invented, which the CAD-fidelity rule forbids.
- The whole tape skin (WNG-001) follows the same convention, so drawing only the hinge strip would be inconsistent (`BUILD-GUIDE-A.md:149`).
- A film solid would sit in designed contact on both skins without adding a resolvable shape at the 0.05 mm gate tolerance.
- Ladder: rung 0 (no change), record and mark.
- Revisit only when R05 names a product with a datasheet thickness.

**Mass/CG.** None (already lumped in WNG-001 at 100.1 g).

**Note for Dillon (information only, not blocking).** "Tape films (skin and hinge) are not drawn" is the team's reading of the hard constraint that the CAD looks exactly like the aircraft. He may overrule it.

### Q3 Servo retention: epoxy in the pocket as drawn

**Decision.** Keep the epoxy bond (edge MEC-001, `electrical.py:739`). No ear screws and no ear tray.

**Reasoning.**
- No proof claim fails on the servo-to-pocket load path. The six known failures are horn, rib and linkage items.
- Ear screws need a hard mount (a printed or ply tray), because screws do not hold in XPS. That is rung 5 (add material) with no failure to justify it.
- Serviceability: a damaged servo is cut out and replaced. BOM-A M01 already carries a spare (qty 3).
- Ladder: rung 0, no change.

**Separate requirement: CAD fidelity.** Whatever the retention, the received servo's ears and output boss must be measured and drawn. This is gap 1 and is unchanged.

**Receipt check, with a numeric criterion.** Measure the ear span and the case axes. If the ear span plus 1 mm clearance is more than the 41 mm pocket length (`wing.py:654`, x 0.1245..0.1655), the pocket must lengthen. That would be a geometry change requiring a gate rerun, not a retention change.

**Mass/CG.** None now.

### Q4 Enclose the RX UART (E-HRN-09) and the I2C lead (E-HRN-11): yes

**Decision.**
1. **I2C (rung 3, rearrange; rung 2, a small groove extension).**
   - Route E-HRN-11 inside the bay cover from the FC I2C pad. It descends before the roof step (the roof is 45 mm up to x 0.080 and 32.5 mm from x 0.0833, `cov_outer()`).
   - It then runs aft in the lane between the ESC edge (\|y\| 17.5) and the cover wall inner face (\|y\| 25.9).
   - It drops into the core through the part of the left plate window that lies inside the cover, at about (x 0.095, y -0.0235). The servo-lead drop slot ends at x 0.0865, so there is no conflict.
   - Move the existing drop slot inboard to centre about y -0.0235, and extend the deep groove along y inboard to meet it. The rest of the groove is unchanged.
   - Target: E-HRN-11 exposed at most 0.005 m per strand (now 0.071).
2. **RX UART (rung 5, add material, justified).**
   - Rungs 1-4 cannot enclose it. The RX cannot fit under the cover: the lane between the ESC and the wall is 8.4 mm, and the RX is 13 mm wide. The RX also cannot move inboard, because the plate window (\|y\| 21..40, x 80..122) would leave it unsupported.
   - Any rearranged route still crosses about 20 mm of open window. CLAUDE.md then requires enclosure "where possible", and here it is possible.
   - Extend the existing cover AVN-008 with a low annex (hood) over the RX on the -y side. It is one part with the cover, not a new part.
   - Hood dimensions (design values of a made part, EST):
     - same 0.6 mm wall as `COV_T`;
     - x about 0.0995..0.1265;
     - y from the cover wall out to about -0.0537 (RX outer edge -0.0525, plus 0.6 mm clearance and the wall);
     - roof about ZP + 8 mm.
   - Remove the main cover's -y wall over the hood's x range.
   - The aft wall gets two antenna exit slots, open at the bottom (D-199 slot precedent), so the cover can still be lowered from above over the RX and the laid antennas.
   - Route E-HRN-09 inside the cover: pad, then up, then into the -y lane, then aft below the roof step, then into the hood, then onto the RX pads.
   - Target: E-HRN-09 exposed 0 m.
3. **Subtract (rung 1).** Once both leads stay inside, delete the two roof-open -y "sensor lead notches" at x 0.0725 and 0.0512 (`electrical.py:928-930`, the `sgn == -1` entries). That closes the cover. The implementer first confirms that no other route uses them; the GPS uses the +y slot.

**Expected effect.**
- Exposed wiring falls from 1.025 strand-m to about 0.45 strand-m. That removes 0.576, which matches the 0.58 in the question.
- Remaining exposure: E-HRN-13 0.215, E-HRN-12 0.085, servo stubs 0.072, E-HRN-14 0.054, GPS 0.018, XT60/phase 0.004. All of these are already listed with reasons.

**Mass/CG.**
- Hood about 1.0 g (EST: about 1,380 mm² of 0.6 mm PETG x 1.27 g/cm³). The notch removal adds about 0.05 g. The I2C groove extension removes negligible foam, and the I2C and UART lengths change by only a few mm.
- Net about +1.0 g at (x about 0.112, y about -0.046):
  - ΔCG x about +0.07 mm aft;
  - ΔCG y about -0.03 mm.
- This is negligible against the CG band. Report the actual change from `build_a.py`.
