# T-101 elevon reserve beyond the pitch peak: roll, gust and combination (2026-11-24)

Task T-101 step 4 (D-251), units U-A (hand derivation, `docs/audits/2026-11-24-T101-reserve-part1.md`, kept as the bulk record) and U-B (SITL case C3G, this file). Design state: elevon chord fraction 0.25, C3 controls (fw_hold 1.0 s, TECS_SPDWEIGHT 0.5), 1.881 kg EST, pitch-use peak 9.76 deg (0.651 of 15 deg, `T101C3_T074`, AR-017), hardware stops +-20 deg. EST = estimate; "-" = unknown. Record: AR-019. Decision: D-252.

## Verdict

**Pitch peak + roll + gust <= available deflection to the stops: NO.** The SITL case C3G (pitch peak, then 3 m/s vertical and side 1-cos gusts and a roll doublet at about the A-P11 rate) drives both elevons to the +-20 deg stops, in the doublet reversal and recovery (see the attribution below): left and right peak 20.0 deg, 0.8 s at the stop (1.1 s by the collector line, 1.25 s at the 19.04 deg PWM-clip level), so the margin is **0 deg or worse (the demand beyond the stop is not recorded, "-")**. Attribution (corrected after review R13-2, checked against `sitl_T101C3G_T074/case.fwd.csv`): both stop contacts (at or above 19.0 deg: 106.04-106.65 s and 107.51-107.92 s) are in the doublet reversal and recovery, with roll rate -108.5 to -33.5 deg/s and +30.5 to +85.5 deg/s in the two windows (reversal peak -110 deg/s, above A-P11 90), after the gust window ended at 105.34 s. They show the roll reversal at the A-P11 rate already reaching the stops with the pitch peak present; they are not a gust effect. In the gust plus first-leg window the worst surface was **-19.59 deg (right) at 104.564 s**, above the 19.04 deg PWM clip. The NO rests on the hand sum (which includes the gust) and on that first-leg excess over the clip, not on SITL stop contact in the gust window.  The hand sum is 29.49 deg against 20, **margin -9.49 deg** (cross-check). CL-A-PITCH-USE stays FAIL at 0.651 and no derived line is proposed; D-252 names the next ladder rung.

## 1. Roll requirement

**Source of A-P11 (roll rate >= 90 deg/s).** `REQUIREMENTS-A.md` line 44 and `params-A.json` `req.roll_min_degs` (source `SRC-A-REQ`) cite `review/QP-3/BOARD.md` s1 item 3, gate G5 (line 148: "roll >= 90 deg/s within +-20 deg"). No derivation was found in REQUIREMENTS, BOARD QP-3, CHANGE-LOG (D-195 only tests against it: 106.9 / 106.4 deg/s at 1.455 / 1.47 kg) or docs/. The 90 is an inherited design number (the K09-L origin is not traced: "-").

**Plant roll model (hand, EST).** Cl_delta = y_e CLD / b = 0.195 x 0.3226 / 0.90 = 0.0699 /rad (antisymmetric deflection; `a_aero.py` l.104-107); steady p = (Cl_delta / |Clp|) delta (2V / b) = **8.80 deg/s per deg** at 17 m/s (Clp -0.30 EST); roll lag tau = I_roll / L_p = 0.1011 / 0.2783 = 0.363 s; the mixer's single-axis cap (MIXING_GAIN 0.65 x 20 deg) is 13 deg = 114 deg/s steady.

**Comparison (differential elevon needed, deg; full table in part 1 section A):**

**Governing pick: A-P11 at 90 deg/s, 10.23 deg of differential.** Reasons: (1) of the unscaled criteria it needs the most elevon; (2) it is the repo's own gate (G5) and was already tested in SITL; (3) the b/V-scaled rows cannot be met inside the stops at any gain, rest on an unsourced reference airplane, and the team has not adopted them. Adopting a scaled or relaxed roll criterion is a **team decision, not made here**; the table is evidence.

**Inertia axis.** `a_aero.py` line 2: body frame FRD, x nose; `a_plant.py` l.66-68: `I = (Ixx, Iyy, Izz)` from `cad_mass.json` `I_cad_axes_kgm2` scaled by M / mass_basic, diagonal terms the same in body axes. Roll axis = body x, **I_roll = Ixx = 0.0882 x 1.1461 = 0.1011 kg m2** (pitch Iyy 0.0402, yaw Izz 0.1129 kg m2). `A.param`'s I_z 0.05 / I_y 0.03 comment is an older EST and is not used.

## 2. Gust

**Level carried: 3 m/s, 1-cos, length 2H/V = 1.08 s (H = 30 ft = 9.144 m, the 14 CFR 25.341 minimum gradient, form only).** Logic: RFS limits are mean <= 5 m/s, gust <= 8 m/s (`SCHEDULE-A.md` line 12), so the gust increment is 3 m/s; Dryden sigma_w = 0.1 x W20 (MathWorks / MIL-F-8785C, scout b2) gives 0.8 m/s at W20 = 8 m/s, so 3 m/s is about 3.75 sigma (the 3-sigma factor is not sourced). Vertical = horizontal increment (isotropic, EST). Sensitivity: 1 m/s.

**Hand (EST, `reserve_hand.py`):** static attitude-hold demand 3.79 deg per m/s (11.4 deg at 3 m/s, an upper bound); dynamic short-period model with an EST loop gives **3.71 deg at 3 m/s** (1.24 deg at 1 m/s); side gust 3 m/s through CLB -0.04 /rad (EST) needs **5.79 deg** differential (1.93 deg at 1 m/s).

**SITL C3G (what was injected).** Same case as `T101C3_T074` (T-074, mass 1.881, wind 0, hold 25, cruise_s 8, r 1.33, fw_hold 1.0, TECS_SPDWEIGHT 0.5, chord 0.25, no Lua; instance 2), with the events placed at the AR-017 pitch peak (baseline mean-elevon peak -9.76 deg at plant t 104.361 s, 1.14 s after the CRUISE command at 103.224 s; in C3G the CRUISE command is at 103.124 s and the events start 1.14 s later):
- **Vertical 1-cos gust 3 m/s, 1.08 s, downdraft (positive down in the plant's NED world frame; the sign that adds to the nose-up pitch demand), start 104.264 s.** The existing plant gust is horizontal only, so an opt-in extension was added (`a_plant.py`: `GV` dict and one branch in `air_wind`, cmd keys `gv_t0 gv_dur gv_vert gv_side`; `run_fwd.py`: key `c3g=1`). Default off; all other branches are untouched (the diff adds 5 lines in `a_plant.py`; the only changed existing line in `run_fwd.py` is the cruise `run_for`, moved unchanged into the `else` branch).
- **Side 1-cos gust 3 m/s, 1.08 s, same start, east (air moving toward +y = body right wing in cruise; heading 2.9 deg).** Supplied by the same extension, in the world y component.
- **OU turbulence: not simulated.** The existing `turb` key runs for the whole flight from 40 m altitude (hover and transition included), which would change the baseline; no cruise-only switch exists and none was added.
- Each gust and the doublet are in one run, so the contribution of each cannot be separated.

## 3. SITL result and combination

In CRUISE the stick is a bank-angle demand (ROLL_LIMIT_DEG 45, so +-33.75 deg), converted by the roll loop to a rate demand clipped at RLL2SRV_RMAX 150 deg/s. A true 90 deg/s rate held 1.5 s would need 135 deg of bank, past the 45 deg limit, so the doublet is a bank-angle step pair. `PIDR.Tar` (dataflash log) was 86.4 deg/s at the first-leg start and -150 deg/s (the RMAX clip) at the reversal; the plant roll rate reached **+82.0 deg/s on the first leg (104.818 s, below A-P11 = 90), -110.0 deg/s at the reversal, and +88.5 deg/s in the recovery (108.021 s) (22 % above A-P11)**. The roll limit did not clip the demand before the rate was reached. Stick start 104.324 s; the plant clock and the SITL clock agree to within 0.06 s (gust event logged at plant t 103.67 s for a start 104.264 s).

## 4. Conclusion and D-252

CL-A-PITCH-USE stays **FAIL at 0.651** (not edited). Even with the pitch use on the 0.60 line (9 deg), A-P11 plus the carried gusts leave a shortfall. D-252 (CHANGE-LOG) records the trade table and names the next ladder rung: **rung 3, rearrange: cut the cruise trim (CG / reflex / incidence; the steady trim is 6.4 deg of the 9.76 peak in the C3 T-074 run)**, evaluated in SITL against the same C3G case, with the roll and gust criteria as team decisions in parallel (every roll criterion alone, including FAR 23.157 5 s, still fails with the side gust at the 0.651 pitch peak). Hand scaling EST: trim to zero leaves a pitch peak of 3.36 deg and a sum of 23.09 deg with A-P11, vertical and side gusts (NO, -3.09) or 17.30 deg without the side gust (YES, +2.70).

## 7. Limits

- One SITL sample (repeat scatter 0.01-0.03 of pitch use, T-076b); the doublet is a bank-angle step pair, not a held 90 deg/s rate; the reversal reached 110 deg/s; vertical gust, side gust and doublet are in the same run, so the share of each is not separated; no Lua variant and no Z5 case were run; no OU turbulence; gust sign chosen to add to the pitch demand (downdraft), side gust one sign.
- The unclipped demand is not recorded (servo output saturated at 1000 / 2000 us), so the SITL margin is "<= 0", not a number.
- The ATT log is 10 Hz, RCOU 25 Hz, the plant csv 20 Hz; time at the stop has 0.05 s resolution.
- Plant aero (CLD, Clp, CLB, CMQ), the dynamic gust loop gains (Kq 0.230, Kth 3.46 1/s) and the 3-sigma factor are EST; the hand sum is a deliberate worst case (all items adding at one instant).
- MIL-F-8785C Table IXa alignment unconfirmed (scout a1); b/V scaling rests on an unsourced reference airplane; the 90 deg/s derivation is missing; the K09-L value is "-".
- Roll-rate overshoot, pitch-peak timing and stop contact depend on the plant, not on a flight test; the first flight logs (TP-11, TP-19) are the check.
