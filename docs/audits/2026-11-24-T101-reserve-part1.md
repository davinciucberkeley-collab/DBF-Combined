# T-101 reserve part 1: hand derivation of the elevon reserve beyond the pitch peak (2026-11-24)

Unit U-A, step 4 of T-101 (D-251). Design state: elevon chord fraction 0.25, C3 controls, worst pitch-use peak 9.76 deg (0.651 of 15 deg, T-074 + Lua; D-251 / AR-017), stops +-20 deg. This is a hand estimate. **The SITL unit (C3G) overrides it.** Script and numbers: `redesign/qp/A/mechanism/out/T101_data/reserve/reserve_hand.py` (reads `sitl/a_aero.py`, `sitl/cad_mass.json`, `params-A.json`, `A.param` at run time), outputs `reserve_hand.json` and `reserve_hand.txt` in the same folder. Sources and criteria text: `docs/audits/2026-11-24-T101-reserve-scout.md` (s1 to s4). EST = estimate; "-" = unknown.

Plant values read at run time: V 17 m/s (A.param AIRSPEED_CRUISE), q 177.0 Pa, S 0.22 m2, b 0.90 m, c 0.245 m, CLA 3.737 /rad (Helmbold EST), CLD 0.3226 /rad (cf/c 0.25, tau 0.5 EST), elevon centroid arm |x_e| 0.1923 m, mean span station y_e 0.195 m, SM 0.0761, Clp -0.30 (EST), CMQ -1.0 (EST), CLB -0.04 /rad (EST). Mass 1.8807 kg EST = 1.64098 x 1.1174 + 0.032 x 1.47 (`params-A.json` mass.*; AR-018 form).

## A. Roll requirement

**(i) Source of A-P11 (roll rate >= 90 deg/s).** `REQUIREMENTS-A.md` line 44 and `params-A.json` req.roll_min_degs (source `SRC-A-REQ`, note "A-P11 (G5)") cite `review/QP-3/BOARD.md` s1 item 3 and gate G5 (line 148: "roll >= 90 deg/s within +-20 deg"). I found no derivation of the 90 in the REQUIREMENTS, BOARD QP-3, CHANGE-LOG (D-195 only tests against it: 106.9 / 106.4 deg/s) or docs/ greps. The derivation is missing; 90 deg/s is an inherited design number (the K09-L value is not traced here: "-"). `linkage_loads_check.py` line 78 turns it into "about +-11 deg" by the roll-arm ratio (EST).

**(ii) Criteria compared** (all converted by the plant's first-order roll model below; "scaled" = b/V method, own observation, no source; reference airplane b 10 m, V 40 m/s is my EST, b/V 0.25 s against 0.053 s for this aircraft):

Roll model: steady p = (Cl_delta / |Clp|) delta (2V/b) with Cl_delta = y_e CLD / b = 0.195 x 0.3226 / 0.90 = 0.0699 /rad of antisymmetric deflection. a_aero.py lines 104-107: each elevon force is Fz = -qe S CLD d 0.5 (the 0.5 is the split of the mean-deflection CLD between the two elevons), Mx = EL_Y (FzR - FzL); +-delta gives |Mx| = y_e q S CLD delta, so the 0.5 factors cancel against the two elevons: Cl_delta = 0.0699 (checked against the code, `fwd_checks.py` l.30-32 uses the same form). Steady p = (0.0699 / 0.30) x (2 x 17 / 0.90) = **8.80 deg/s per deg** of differential deflection (so 20 deg gives 176 deg/s; the mixer's single-axis cap of 13 deg gives 114 deg/s). Roll lag: L_p = q S b^2 |Clp| / (2V) = 177.0 x 0.22 x 0.81 x 0.30 / 34 = 0.2783 N m s/rad; tau = I_roll / L_p = 0.1011 / 0.2783 = **0.363 s** (time to 90 % of steady 0.84 s). Time to bank with lag: phi(t) = p_ss [t - tau (1 - e^(-t/tau))].

Unscaled time-to-bank requirements are well under A-P11 because they are modest bank-change times. Scaled by b/V they ask for more than any deflection within the 20 deg stop can give: the airframe's roll lag tau 0.363 s is 6.9 times b/V (nondimensional tau V/b = 6.9), against roughly 1 for a light airplane (EST, no source), because the motors and batteries sit out on the wing (Ixx 0.101 kg m2 on a 0.9 m span).

**Governing criterion for this aircraft: A-P11 at 90 deg/s, differential 10.2 deg.** Reasons: (1) of the unscaled criteria it needs the most elevon (10.2 against 7.2 for MIL L1 Cat A 60 deg); (2) it is the repo's own gate (G5), already tested in SITL; (3) the scaled MIL rows are not a usable criterion: they cannot be met inside the stops at any gain, they rest on my own reference-airplane EST, and the team has not adopted them. Level 1 is carried (A-P11 is stricter than Level 2 times). **Team decision, not made here:** whether to adopt a scaled MIL / b/V criterion at all; the table is evidence, not a ruling.

**(iii) Roll axis in the plant.** `a_aero.py` line 2: body frame FRD in cruise, x nose (chord), y right wing, z belly. `a_plant.py` lines 66-68: `I = (Ixx, Iyy, Izz)` from `cad_mass.json` `I_cad_axes_kgm2`, scaled by M / mass_basic, with the comment "CAD x aft/y right/z up: diagonal terms are the same in body axes"; no cruise remap is applied (the hover-to-cruise attitude change is the body-to-world rotation, not an inertia change). **Roll axis = body x, I_roll = Ixx = 0.0882 x 1.1461 = 0.1011 kg m2** (pitch Iyy 0.0402, yaw Izz 0.1129 kg m2). `A.param`'s I_z 0.05 / I_y 0.03 comment is an older EST and is not used.

**(iv) Differential elevon and plant check.** delta_roll = p / 8.80 = 90 / 8.80 = **10.23 deg** (the `linkage_loads_check.py` EST was 11). Plant check against D-195 (1.455 kg): the mixer cap is 13 deg per axis (section C), so the highest steady rate the loop can command is 114 deg/s; 106.9 deg/s at 1.455 kg is 93 % of that and implies 12.1 deg of differential by this formula, i.e. the SITL roll step was running near the mixer cap, consistent with the formula. This is a consistency check only: the D-195 elevon deflection during the roll step is not recorded in params-A.json ("-").

## B. Gust

**Level carried: 3 m/s vertical, 1-cos, length 2H/V = 1.08 s (H = 30 ft = 9.144 m, the 14 CFR 25.341 minimum gradient, form only; shorter gusts are not carried).**

Logic: the RFS limits are mean <= 5 m/s, gust <= 8 m/s (`SCHEDULE-A.md` line 12), so the gust increment above the mean is 8 - 5 = 3 m/s (scout O-G1). Dryden sigma_w = 0.1 x W20 (MathWorks / MIL-F-8785C, scout b2): W20 = 8 m/s gives 0.8 m/s; 3 sigma (EST, the 3 is not sourced) is 2.4 m/s, so 3 m/s covers it. The vertical component is taken equal to the horizontal gust increment (isotropic, EST, conservative). Sensitivity carried: 1 m/s.

Static demand (rigid attitude hold): delta_g = c CLA SM (w_g/V) / (|x_e| CLD) = 0.245 x 3.737 x 0.0761 x (w_g/17) / (0.1923 x 0.3226) = **3.79 deg per m/s** (11.4 deg at 3 m/s). This is an upper bound: the airframe's own static stability (SM 7.6 %) weathervanes into the gust rather than holding attitude.

Dynamic estimate (EST, `reserve_hand.py`): states theta, gamma, q; alpha_local = theta - gamma + w/V; gamma' = Za alpha_local; q' = -Malpha alpha_local - Mq q - Mdelta delta, with Malpha = q S c CLA SM / Iyy = 67.5 1/s2 (short-period wn 8.2 rad/s), Mdelta = q S CLD |x_e| / Iyy = 60.1 1/s2, Mq = q S c^2 |CMQ| / (2V Iyy) = 1.71 1/s, Za = q S CLA / (m V) = 4.55 1/s, Iyy 0.0402 kg m2 (pitch axis). Loop: delta = Kq (q + Kth theta), Kq set for a 2.2 Hz rate crossover (AR-002 / CLAIMS-FIX, EST) = 0.230, Kth = 3.46 1/s (EST; the real PTCH / RLL gains are not in A.param: "-"). Peak elevon for a 1-cos vertical gust of 3 m/s, by gust length T = 0.5 / 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 6 s: 5.35 / 3.88 / 2.45 / 1.86 / 1.51 / 1.11 deg; at the chosen T = 1.08 s: 3.71 deg. For 1 m/s: 1.78 / 1.29 / 0.82 / 0.62 / 0.50 / 0.37 deg, 1.24 deg at 1.08 s. **delta_g = 3.71 deg at 3 m/s; 1.24 deg at 1 m/s**; the result depends on my EST gains and is bounded above by the static 11.4 deg.

Lateral disturbance from the same gust (not negligible): a 3 m/s side gust gives beta = 3/17 rad and Cl = CLB beta = 0.04 x 0.176 = 0.0071, held with differential delta = 0.0071 / 0.0699 = **5.79 deg** (1.93 deg at 1 m/s; static, the fins' weathervane is not credited so it is conservative; CLB is an EST).

## C. Hand combination at the worst pitch peak

Mixer: ArduPilot linear mixer, left = (roll + pitch) x gain, right = (roll - pitch) x gain (scout c1, c2). `A.param` line 52 MIXING_GAIN 0.65; SERVO5 1003-1976 us, SERVO6 1024-1997 us (trim 1500); no ELEVON_OUTPUT parameter in A.param ("-"). The plant maps 1000-2000 us to +-20 deg (`a_plant.py` l.45: ELV_SCALE 1.0), so: a full single-axis command gives 0.65 x 20 = **13 deg per surface**; the surface saturates when |roll| + |pitch| (each as a fraction of full command) exceeds 1 / 0.65 = 1.54; the SERVO MIN/MAX clip the PWM at **+19.04 / -19.88 deg (SERVO5) and -19.04 / +19.88 deg (SERVO6)**, i.e. the PWM range clips before the 20 deg hardware stop (worst side 19.04 deg). The pitch peak 9.76 deg is 0.75 of a full pitch command; 10.2 deg of roll is 0.79 of a full roll command; their sum 1.54 is exactly at the saturation line. The mixer does not change the deflection arithmetic below (it is linear), it only fixes the per-axis cap at 13 deg.

**Hand result for the governing criterion (A-P11 90 deg/s + 3 m/s vertical gust + 3 m/s side gust, simultaneous at the pitch peak): NO, margin -9.5 deg (combined 29.5 deg against 20).** Even without the side gust it is -3.7 deg, and pitch + A-P11 alone leaves 0.01 deg to the stop and -0.95 deg to the PWM clip. Reserve after the 9.76 deg pitch peak is 20 - 9.76 = 10.24 deg against 10.23 deg for A-P11 alone, so the roll requirement alone uses all of it. With the vertical gust (3 m/s, dyn) and the side gust (3 m/s) the roll rate that still fits is 0 deg/s; against the static bound the gust that fits beside 90 deg/s is 0.0 m/s. Caveats: the worst-case sum is deliberately conservative (full roll command, full pitch peak, a vertical and a side gust of equal magnitude, all in the adding sign at the same instant; no RSS source exists, scout c3); the real overlap is for SITL to decide. The 0.60-of-15 deg line (9 deg) leaves 11 deg, which does not change the verdict.
