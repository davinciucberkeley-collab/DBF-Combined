## 4. Limits and open items
- Landing: foam plateau, stroke and pad mu are unmeasured (coupon and drag tests pending). The failing case is the AR-008 hard landing (1.2 m/s sink, 10 deg lean, 0.5 m/s lateral drift). It exceeds the 20 mm stroke by 0.3 mm, then the rigid stop (5e5 N/m) produces the 184 N spike, so the n and RF figures are stop impacts, not crush loads. The stroke line is missed by 1.5 %; the same case at 1.50 kg was 15.4 mm (17.2 mm on the current geometry). Candidate ladder rungs (docs/engineering-decisions.md; none applied here): 1 subtract: a touchdown drift and lean limit on the flight card (the case combines 10 deg lean with 0.5 m/s drift; the AR-012 SITL records show lateral <= 0.52 m/s and lean 0-29 deg, so it is inside the SITL envelope); 2 modify: plateau between 30 and 45 N (about 33-36 N, not run) or a longer stroke; 3 rearrange: pad outboard / wider feet; 5 add material: the rib tube (rib-root RF).
- Rigid body only: no rib twist or fin flexibility (AR-012), no controller or thrust model during touch, mu and plateau EST; the landing mass is the 1.881 kg EST with growth spread by class (CG from the CAD).
- SITL: one sample per case (T-076b measured scatter 0.01-0.03 at fixed parameters, not re-measured); plant limits as SITL-FLIGHT.md s1 (no prop cross-flow, wash, battery drift; servo linkage not modelled); aero coefficients unchanged (CLmax 0.75 EST), motor lag 40 ms EST, pack R 30 mohm EST; inertia uniformly scaled; INS_POS not modelled ("—"); the `window()` helper still assumes the 0.90 cap, so the default r (1.3925) is a convention, not a design number; whether the toolchain binary equals the 2026-11-15 one is unrecorded (as AR-010). No wind or gust case was run (not requested).
- The elevon pitch-use basis (15 / 20 / 25 deg) is still undecided (AR-010 open item 5): the verdict flips with it, so all three are reported.
