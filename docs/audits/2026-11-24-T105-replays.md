## Decisions
- Default case: T101C3_T074, "Current design basis: C3 at 0.25 chord, hard case T-074 (T-101), without Lua". 16 runs in the picker; Lua runs labelled "with Lua motor cap and battery monitor".
- Pose: pitch about body y, nose up positive; roll and heading held at the last valid value while pitch >= 80 deg (README warning).
- Elevons: rotated about the hinge pivot and axis found in the GLB revolute joint nodes, trailing edge down positive. No proxy used.
- Props: th1..th4 mapped to LR, UL, LL, UR (from a_plant motor order); spin is visual only (12*sqrt(thrust) deg/s, capped at 36).
- Landing pads: scaled along their length by crush about the shoe end; pad order UR, LR, UL, LL against the file's (sy,sz) order; pose from the recorded quaternion and position.
- Flight CG offset is a hard-coded T-098-basis constant (display only).
- Linkage: all numbers read from linkage.json at runtime (will follow the post-T-101 re-export); display-only constants are the 10 s sweep rate and a 5x elevon length, labelled not to scale.
- One GLB (2437 KB) parsed once, cloned per panel; panels initialise lazily (IntersectionObserver).
- Weight: manifest total 3446.5 KB for all JSON; one flight run loads about 105 KB (T074) or 251 KB (Z5), landing about 50 KB, linkage 89 KB; plus the GLB once.
