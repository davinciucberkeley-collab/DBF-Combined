# Capability audit, concept A (T-016, parents R-CAD-01, R-PHY-01)

Audit date 2026-11-18, branch redesign/v1. (Note 2026-11-23, review round 6a: line numbers and counts below are as of 2026-11-18 and drift; current: 129 parts, 66 steps, exposed 0.597 strand-m, `redesign/qp/A/mechanism/out/T092_report.md` s2.) Read-only inspection of the repo; nothing was re-run except directory and file-date listings and two JSON reads (`wire_inventory.json`, `assembly.json`). "Last output" dates are file modification dates. All paths are relative to the repo root; `A/` = `redesign/qp/A/`, `AM/` = `redesign/qp/A/cad/amodel/`.

Headline: the project's "3-D physics" gate for concept A is a static interference check (collision at neutral and at the two elevon limits) plus a straight-line swept insertion check of build steps. The elevon servo, arm, pushrod and horn are modelled as rigid parts at neutral only; no script computes servo/pushrod/horn kinematics, moves the linkage through its travel, or integrates the dynamics of any mechanism (section 3). Real FEA, flutter and hover rigid-body flight simulations do exist (section 2), but none involves the elevon linkage.

## 3. Does any script compute elevon/servo kinematics, swept collision through motion, or mechanism rigid-body dynamics?

- Elevon/servo linkage kinematics (arm angle to rod to horn angle): none. Searched `A/**/*.py` and `redesign/collision/` for horn/pushrod/four-bar/linkage: the only occurrences are the part builders and notes `AM/electrical.py:540-550,659-673,944-978`, and the explicit statements that the path is not modelled: `A/cad/amodel/electrical.py:671` ("linkage path not modelled in the poses"), `redesign/collision/collide.py:20` ("the four-bar pushrod / servo-arm path ... held at neutral and listed as not swept"), `A/cad/out/REPORT.md` ("Not checked against the moved parts"; "four-bar path not modelled here"). The revolute joint at `electrical.py:1215-1216` for the clevis is registered with the axis only; nothing drives it.
- Swept collision through motion of the linkage: none. What does exist: (a) elevon panels, horn, caps and balance rigidly turned to the two limits and tested for overlap at those two poses only, `redesign/collision/collide.py:129-153,201` via `collide_a.py` (end-poses, no intermediate angles); (b) 2-D polygon rotation of the lead strip/tabs every 0.5 deg between -21 and +21, `A/cad/stop_check.py` (uses `AM/wing.py:138-141 _rot_pt`); (c) lead/tab hull over -STOP..+STOP at 2 deg steps used to cut the core pocket, `AM/wing.py:161-165`; (d) straight-line swept insertion paths for build steps, `A/cad/a_assembly.py`.
- Rigid-body dynamics of any mechanism: none. Rigid-body dynamics exist only at aircraft level: `A/sitl/a_plant.py` (hover, 6-DOF, stdlib) and `A/structures/landing_a.py` (touchdown, 6-DOF, numpy). The elevon flutter model `A/structures/flutter_a.py` has one hinge DOF (beta) with typical-section constants, which is a dynamic model of the surface, not of the servo/rod. No physics engine (Bullet, MuJoCo, pybullet, pymunk) is imported anywhere in `A/`, `redesign/showcase`, `redesign/collision` (grep for those names returned nothing).

Note for the model builder: because the servo arm turns in the x-y plane (vertical shaft) while the horn hole turns in the x-z plane about the Y hinge axis, the linkage is spatial (a spherical-joint rod between a circle about Z and a circle about Y), not a planar four-bar. The CAD comments call it "four-bar" (`redesign/collision/collide.py:20`); the geometry in `electrical.py:944-978` is the source of truth.
