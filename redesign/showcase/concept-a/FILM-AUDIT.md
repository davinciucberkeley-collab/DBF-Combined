## What was fixed, by cause

1. **Camera cuts.** Every step used a 0.7 s move whatever the change, so the picture jumped (zoom up to 4x, swings up to 75 degrees). Moves are now one smoothstep shot between the views of consecutive steps; the time grows with the zoom change (1.5 s per octave), the swing (1 s per 40 degrees) and the shift (`cam_duration`, 0.6 to 4.2 s), the distance is interpolated geometrically, the checkpoint pull-back time is scaled the same way and the next move starts from the pull-back view. The per-step views are computed by Blender (`--camplan`, `cam_plan.json`) and read by the schedule, so the film length follows from them (834 s, was 801 s).
2. **Stage openings.** The first step of a stage used the wide "everything so far" shot as its own view, so the new part was tiny. The camera now opens wide behind the title card, glides to the step view while the card is still up and the title card fades in (0.5 s) and out (0.7 s) instead of popping.
3. **Wing vanishing in the bench stage.** B0a-B0j2 hid the wing and showed it again at B0k. The wing now stays as an x-ray beside the frame (the frame is still built at the B1 start pose), and becomes solid when B1 starts to dock.
4. **Plugs in air (A4, A5).** The visual travel of A3-A5 is shortened (the plan travel is unchanged and swept): the plugs start just outside the tip, the x-ray camera follows them clamped over the wing and the spar and core ghosts stay in view.
5. **Servo (D1, D0, D2-D5b, E3b).** D1 and D0 see the core through (x-ray), the camera looks from the aft side (the cage arms and motors are forward of the pockets and were in front), the servo drops into its pocket and stays visible, D0 grows the lead from the servo out of its pocket along the foam groove to the slit with a bead at the fish-wire tip (CAD route E-HRN-07 / 08: 208 mm groove, 30 mm under the plate, 12 mm exposed), E3b completes the last 42 mm from the slit to the FC pad S5 / S6 (the soldering step). Right servo only is framed; labels of the left one are dropped as off-screen.
6. **Parts before their step.** The bridge saddle and the two strut clamps showed at B0k; they now appear with B1 (they have no bench step). Nothing else was early or gone.
7. **Callouts.** A label is drawn only if its part is on screen in the free band between the header and the step card (was: anywhere in the frame, which left labels stuck at the edge: B4 "Left damper"). Labels never shown because their target is off-screen or not yet drawn: A2 "Bonded over |y| <= 200 mm only" (point at the left tip, off-screen), B0a (cut stock is not a drawn part), B4 "Left damper".
8. **Stale labels.** The shipped stage clips were rendered before `a_build_steps.py` changed (hub 17.1 g "export pending"; step E3d missing). All stage clips and the full film are re-rendered from the current data: hub 18.3 g (CGE-001.stl, `qp/A/drawings/out/stl/`).

## Not fixed / reported (CAD or plan, outside the film)

- The plan lists D0 after E1; the guide and the film put D0 right after D1 (before the arms, horns and pushrods). Both satisfy the plan gate (`THREAD_BEFORE`: D0 before E2). Left as the guide has it.
- The phase and servo leads are drawn complete only at their LAY step (E6, E3b); before that the film shows the thread progress (reveal curve). Plan and wire gate unchanged.
- B0a has no part to draw (cut stock): the picture is the empty bench with the x-ray wing. A part-less step by the plan.
- The cage unit is shown at the B1 start pose below the wing during the bench steps; that pose is a presentation choice (B1 docks from 70 mm below), the plan sweeps the bench in its own run.
- `redesign/site/app.html` guide text still says "hub 17.1 g ... export pending" in the stage 2 guide section; it comes from the generated `qp/A/BUILD-GUIDE-A.md` / the guide generator, not from the film (not touched here).

## Decisions

- Camera durations are computed (not constants) and cached in `cam_plan.json`; the renderer refuses to run with a stale plan.
- Servo steps use an aft-side view (az 140 to 200) rather than the default nose-side view; D5b looks from the aft right so the left fin tube is not in front.
- The x-ray wing in the bench stage was preferred to hiding it: nothing is removed and re-added.
- A bead (4.5 mm) marks the fish-wire tip while a lead is threaded; the lead itself is drawn at its real 1.2 mm.
