Step card

One render, one thing to do. Numbered actions match the orange callouts in the figure. Parts and tools for this step only. Every step ends with a CHECK the student can do in a minute.

STEP7Clamp the left boom to the spar15 min
  1. Slide clamp BMS-004 onto the boom, open side up, 310 mm from the root mark.
  2. Seat the boom in the saddle; the laser line on the boom faces aft.
  3. Fit 4× M3×16 into the heat-set inserts. Tighten in a cross, 0.6 N·m, then blue threadlocker.

Parts

  • 1×BMS-004 boom clamp (PETG)
  • 4×M3×16 SHCS A2
  • 4×M3 brass insert (pre-fitted, step 5)

Tools

  • 2.5 mm hex driver, torque
  • Threadlocker, blue
  • Steel rule 500 mm
CHECK Boom does not rotate by hand; clamp-to-root distance 310 ± 1 mm both sides. Write the measured value in the build log.

Callouts

Four kinds, each with its icon and a bold lead word. Note for context, caution for damage or rework, stop for safety (LiPo, props, hot wire), check for a pass criterion.

Note Cure epoxy 12 h at ≥ 20 °C before loading the joint. Overnight at Jacobs Hall is enough.
Caution Do not exceed 0.6 N·m on M3 into brass inserts in PETG; the insert pulls out at about 1.2 N·m.
Stop Props off before any bench power-up. Battery in the LiPo bag until the arming checklist is complete.
Check Wing joiner slides fully home with hand pressure and releases with the thumb screws loosened; no play at the tip.

Proof graph nodes

Shape says node type, fill says status, size says importance. Evidence flows left to right: source → parameter → calculation → claim → requirement. Edges are neutral; a failing edge is red.

source parameter calculation claim requirement assumption test
The claim is UNVERIFIED because one input (ρ) has no source; the requirement inherits the worst status.
