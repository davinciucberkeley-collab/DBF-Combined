# D7. Airspeed sensor: RMRC MS4525DO or Holybro MS5525DSO

**Recommendation:** Keep the RMRC MS4525DO + pitot ($59.99, US stock) with `ARSPD_TYPE,1`, `ARSPD_BUS,0`; it avoids the 0x76 clash outright, and the repo's remaining MS5525 text (not the param file) is what needs fixing.

**Confidence:** High on the address/type logic (ArduPilot source + hwdef verified). Medium on the exact silicon RMRC ships (the listing says "MS4525DO equivalent"), which is why a bench check at arrival is in the follow-ups.

**Depends on:** D1/D2 only loosely. Assumes cruise 18-20 m/s and AIRSPEED_MIN 16 (vtol_c0.param:114-115); the sensor choice does not change for 18-22 m/s. Assumes the FC stays the CoreWing F405 Wing APP V2; if D-something swaps to Kakute H743-Wing (BOM fallback), re-check the baro address (not verified here).

## Reasoning

**Flight controller / baro (verified).** FC = CoreWing F405 Wing APP V2 (C0_BOM.csv line 4; firmware/README.md:30-31). ArduPilot hwdef `CoreWingF405WingV2/hwdef.dat` (master, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ArduPilot/ardupilot/master/libraries/AP_HAL_ChibiOS/hwdef/CoreWingF405WingV2/hwdef.dat) has `BARO SPL06 I2C:0:0x76`, `# only one I2C bus`, `I2C_ORDER I2C1`. So the param-file comment and the electrical note agree: the onboard baro IS at 0x76 on the only I2C bus, which is also the airspeed/GPS-compass connector.

**Addresses (verified from ArduPilot source).**
- MS4525DO: driver probes 0x28, 0x36, 0x46 (`MS4525D0_I2C_ADDR1/2/3`; AP_Airspeed_MS4525.cpp). Holybro's MS4525DO-DS5AI001DP is 0x28 (Holybro listing via search results). None collides with 0x76.
- MS5525DSO: driver addresses 0x76 and 0x77 (`MS5525D0_I2C_ADDR_1/2`; AP_Airspeed_MS5525.cpp). Chip's CSB pin selects it; Holybro's product page lists 0x76 as the default (page text seen in search snippet; Holybro page itself returned HTTP 429 on direct fetch, so retailer/snippet-level confirmation). So the Holybro board as shipped would sit on the same address as the SPL06. Two devices at 0x76 on one bus: unreliable baro and/or airspeed. Fix would be re-strapping CSB to 0x77 (soldering on a small board; I found no source saying Holybro exposes a jumper for it).
- electrical-architecture.md:35 ("MS5525 airspeed at 0x76") is therefore a real conflict statement, and is obsolete under the MS4525DO baseline.

**ARSPD_TYPE (verified, AP_Airspeed.h enum on master).** 1 = I2C-MS4525D0. 3 = I2C-MS5525 (auto-probe 0x76 then 0x77), 4 = MS5525 at address 1 (0x76), 5 = MS5525 at address 2 (0x77). The param file (`ARSPD_TYPE,1`, line 109; `ARSPD_BUS,0`, line 110) is correct for the MS4525DO. The old `ARSPD_TYPE,5` in firmware/peripherals.md meant "MS5525 at 0x77", i.e. it already assumed the CSB re-strap.

**Range / resolution at 18-20 m/s (own arithmetic, rho = 1.225).**
- Dynamic pressure q = 198 Pa at 18 m/s, 245 Pa at 20 m/s; 20 Pa at 5.7 m/s... (q at 5 m/s = 15 Pa).
- ArduPilot MS4525 driver: 14-bit counts, 10-90 % output window, +/-P_max with ARSPD_PSI_RANGE default 1 psi: one count = 2 x 6895 / (0.8 x 16383) = 1.05 Pa. dv/dq = 1/(rho v) = 0.041 m/s per Pa at 20 m/s, so quantisation is about 0.04 m/s at cruise, about 0.16 m/s per count at 5 m/s. Plenty for AIRSPEED_MIN 16 / Q_ASSIST 13 logic.
- Full scale ~106 m/s; cruise uses ~3.5 % of span. The RMRC listing gives accuracy +/-0.25 % of span (about +/-17-34 Pa by my arithmetic, that is up to ~7 % of q at 20 m/s if it were not zeroed). Offset is removed by the pre-flight ARSPD calibration; the remaining scale error is handled by ARSPD_AUTOCAL / GPS reciprocal legs, already planned (firmware/README.md:292, checklists E9; error budget 10 % = review M1).
- MS5525DSO: also a 1 psi class part in ArduPilot (the driver coefficient table is "001DS part, 1PSI range") with a 24-bit ADC at OSR 1024, so resolution is equal or better, but 18-20 m/s needs nothing finer than the MS4525DO gives. No advantage for this mission.

**Shipping / cost (verified from repo notes; RMRC page).**
- RMRC sensor: $59.99, "In Stock", "Fast Shipping from Ohio" (https://www.readymaderc.com/products/details/rmrc-digital-air-speed-sensor-pixhawk-ii-2, retailer). Same single ReadyMadeRC order as the FC, GPS, servos, so no extra shipping (BOM line 9; bom-savings.md:81).
- Holybro MS5525DSO: $59.99 plus $37.47 DHL/FedEx air from China (bom-savings.md:40, 81), plus possible duty and longer lead time (review/src/body.html:914). Roughly $97 delivered vs $60. Real shipping time not verified (no source states it); judgement is days to 2 weeks versus about a week domestic.

**Consistency with firmware/vtol_c0.param.** The param file, firmware/README.md 4.3, BOM, build-manual, test-plan E08 and review wiring table all already say MS4525DO / 0x28 / TYPE 1. The decision as the DECISIONS.md row frames it is already implemented; the contradiction lives in stale docs (below).

## Risks / what would change the call
- RMRC's page says "MS4525DO equivalent" in the spec list but "MS4525DO sensor chip" in the description, and does not list an I2C address. If the board turns out to be a clone at an address ArduPilot does not probe, it will not be detected. Mitigation: build-manual M9 / WP10 10.5 / E08 bench check on arrival; fallback is the Matek ASPD-4525 (TYPE 1, out of stock at US sellers on 2026-11-01, bom-savings.md:18) or Holybro MS4525DO (0x28, ships similar cost to the MS5525 kit, not verified in stock). Ask support@readymaderc.com for the chip/address before ordering if time allows (cheap).
- If a MS4525DO ships at 0x36 or 0x46, still fine (driver probes all three).
- Only flip to MS5525 if the MS4525DO fails the bench check and the CSB re-strap is acceptable; then `ARSPD_TYPE,5` plus a solder step.
- Pitot placement and tube leak test matter more than chip choice (peripherals.md section 4 wing-tip, outside prop wash).
