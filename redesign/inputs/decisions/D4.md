# D4. Payload and ballast (R12)

**Recommendation:** Confirm "no payload, no planned ballast" with the 0.15 m nose extension (CG 28 % MAC). Add one
contingency rule: if the weighed aircraft's CG is aft of the slide range, up to 100 g of nose trim mass is allowed,
counted inside the take-off mass cap. Also correct that cap to **7.75 kg** (R11 stall), down from 7.82 kg (R13 T/W).

**Confidence:** High on rejecting both 0.5 kg options, because each one fails a requirement on the current numbers.
Medium on the 100 g contingency figure, which is judgement sized from the CG sensitivities below.

**Depends on:** D1 (baseline = the 11-02 C0 sizing, MTOW 7.438 kg, chord 322.3 mm) and D2 (cruise speed unchanged).
If D1/D2 re-size the wing or the motors, the mass caps below move, but the ranking of the three options does not. That
would only change if a re-sized aircraft had about 0.5 kg of both T/W and stall margin to spare, and no candidate does.

**Reasoning:**

Verified facts (repo):
- The baseline puts the CG at 28 % MAC with no ballast. The neutral point is 38.2 % and the static margin 10.2 %
  (`analysis/results.json` C0 `cg.pct`, `cg.SM_noPayload`; `docs/DESIGN-BASIS.md:56`). AVL gives 10.4 % with the pod
  (`docs/build/BUILD-MANUAL.md:1172`).
- The pack slides ±30 mm on its tray (225 mm bay, 220 mm tray, rev G, `BUILD-MANUAL.md:6`), which moves the CG
  ±3.3 mm, or ±1.03 % MAC (`BUILD-MANUAL.md:1186`). The `cg.xbMax` 0.339 m forward station is a CAD fit check and is
  not usable travel on the built tray.
- The flight aft limit is 30.4 % MAC (8 % SM) (`docs/test/cards/DS-G01.md:5`).
- On the maker's table T/W is 1.68 (12.52 kgf total). The R13 floor of 1.6 sets a mass cap of 7.825 kg
  (`BUILD-MANUAL.md:1177`).
- At 7.438 kg the design stall is 11.75 m/s, against R11's 12 m/s limit (`DESIGN-BASIS.md:29, 53`).
- The joiner reserve factor is 1.41 on 275 MPa and 1.23 on 240 MPa, at a 91.0 N·m root moment (`DESIGN-BASIS.md:57`).
- No requirement R1–R21 asks for a payload. R9 is a flight demonstration only (`DESIGN-BASIS.md:18–39`). The review
  prompt says "payload if any" (`docs/VTOL-REVIEW-PROMPT.md:35`). No camera or sensor is in scope or in the $2,000 BOM.

My arithmetic (python -I):

| Option | Take-off mass | Hover T/W (R13 ≥ 1.6) | Stall (R11 ≤ 12 m/s) | Joiner RF 275 / 240 MPa |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| No payload, no ballast | 7.44 kg | 1.68 | 11.75 m/s | 1.41 / 1.23 |
| Fly with 0.5 kg ballast | 7.94 kg | **1.58, fails** | **12.14 m/s, fails** | 1.32 / 1.15 (moment ×1.067, all fuselage mass) |
| 0.5 kg reserve, re-sized | ≈ 8.1 kg (+0.7 kg, `DESIGN-BASIS.md:30`) | at the 1.6 limit | needs a bigger wing (more mass and cost) | lower again |

- **Ballast fails two requirements outright.** It also uses up the whole mass-growth allowance before the first part
  is built.
- **The reserve buys nothing the project needs.** No sensor is planned or budgeted. Carrying the reserve means a
  larger wing and a heavier aircraft, which is the 2026-11-01 result the team already reversed.
- **The binding mass cap is stall, not T/W.** Stall at 12 m/s comes at 7.438 × (12/11.75)² = 7.758 kg. The build
  manual and FTC-F01 use 7.82 kg (from R13). Between 7.76 and 7.82 kg the aircraft passes the W&B check but breaks R11.
  The real growth margin is +0.32 kg (4.3 %). That is small next to the 10–20 % growth that hand-built foam/glass
  airframes typically show (judgement, not sourced). Mass growth is a bigger risk to this aircraft than CG.
- **CG control margin is adequate without ballast, and growth location matters more than amount.** Growth spread
  evenly does not move the CG. Concentrated growth moves it by these amounts:

  | Location | Mass that moves the CG 1 % MAC aft |
  |---|---|
  | Tail glass, paint or tape (x −0.904) | 29 g |
  | Tail servos or pushrods | 47 g |
  | Pusher area | 73 g |

  From 28 % to the 30.4 % aft limit, plus 1.03 % from sliding the pack forward, the aircraft absorbs about 3.4 % MAC.
  That is about **100 g of extra tail mass** before trim is needed. Nose mass at x +0.60 moves the CG 1 % MAC forward
  per 35 g, so 100 g of nose trim recovers about 2.9 % MAC. Trim of that size costs 0.013 in T/W and 0.08 m/s of
  stall, and only when it is actually needed. Planned ballast would cost 500 g on every flight.
- **Hover:** the lift-thrust centroid sits at 28 % MAC with a 0.498 m arm each side (`results.json` geo.xFront/xRear).
  An aft drift to 30.4 % (7.7 mm) puts 50.8 % of the weight on the rear pair. The rear-pair effective T/W is then
  1.65, which still meets R13.

**Risks / what would change the call:**
- If the team adds a real mission sensor (camera, RTK, telemetry payload), it is a design change. Re-run the sizing
  with `mPayload` set and expect a larger wing and possibly larger lift motors. This decision does not cover it.
- If the weighed CG ends up more than about 3.4 % MAC aft (more than about 100 g of concentrated tail growth), the
  100 g trim is not enough. The fix is then to move ESCs or avionics forward with a `cg` re-run, as the manual already
  says.
- If mass growth goes past 7.75 kg, R11 is broken whatever is done about ballast. In that case, lighten the build,
  relax R11 (it is "Assumed"), or enlarge the wing.
- If D1 or D2 changes chord, motors or speed, recompute both caps: 4 × Tmax / 1.6, and m × (12 / Vs)².
- `docs/research/stall-margin.md:129` still cites "the existing 0.5 kg ballast rule". This is a stale reference.
