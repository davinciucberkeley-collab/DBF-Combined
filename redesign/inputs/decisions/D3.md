# D3. MTOW, wing area and chord (and R2 sign-off)

**Recommendation:** Adopt the 11-02 sizing (MTOW 7.438 kg, S 0.774 m², chord 322.3 mm, AR 7.45) unchanged as the baseline, and **revise R2 rather than sign it off as written**. Replace the 8–15 kg "working target" with a hard as-built take-off mass limit of **7.75 kg**. That limit is the tighter of R11 (stall ≤ 12 m/s) and R13 (T/W ≥ 1.6). Keep the < 25 kg hard cap.

**Confidence:** Medium-high.
- High that 11-02 is the right sizing. It is the only version calibrated to the CAD mass roll-up. Rev D and v0.3.1 look better only because they under-count mass.
- Medium on the margins. The design has only about 4 % mass growth margin, and the build has not been weighed.

**Depends on:**
- **D1:** assumes the 11-02 root baseline is chosen. If v0.3.1 is chosen, its 6.18 kg is not CAD-calibrated and would have to be re-run through `analysis/mass/mass_budget.py` before anyone believes its T/W 2.03 or RF 1.85.
- **D2:** cruise speed does not move wing area. Area is set by the 11.75 m/s design stall at CL_max 1.115. 18 m/s vs 20 m/s changes only the cruise CL, endurance and induced-drag share. The call holds either way.
- **D4:** assumes no payload or ballast. **This call flips if D4 restores the 0.5 kg reserve.** MTOW would go to about 8.1 kg or more, T/W to about 1.53, and stall to about 12.3 m/s, failing both R13 and R11. That would need a re-size, either a bigger wing or more thrust. DESIGN-BASIS R12 already records that the reserve "took hover T/W to the R13 limit".

**Reasoning:**

1. **Why 11-02 and not rev D or v0.3.1 (verified).**
   - The 11-02 masses are calibrated item by item to the CAD roll-up. The bottom-up total is 7.416 kg against 7.408 kg in the sizing loop (`analysis/mass/mass_budget.json`, `total_kg`, `sizing_mtow_kg`). The two agree to within 1 % per group, apart from fasteners (+57 g) and avionics (+21 g).
   - Rev D sized to 6.55 kg with T/W 1.91 (`analysis/stall_margin_trade.json`, Vs 11.75 row). Its fixed masses were the pre-calibration estimates.
   - The roughly 0.9 kg gap between rev D and 11-02 is real hardware that was missing from rev D: a 0.15 m longer pod nose, fasteners, wing hardware and the gear (DESIGN-BASIS "Rev 2026-11-01"). It is not a design choice that could be undone.
   - Rev D's better T/W and joiner reserve factor come from its lower mass. Choosing rev D would not get those margins back once the aircraft is built.

2. **Hover T/W 1.68 (verified numbers, judgement on adequacy).**
   - The four X4110S units with 17x5.5 props give 4 × 3130 g = 12.52 kg of static thrust on the maker's 22.2 V table (`analysis/results.json`, C0 `params.Tmax_g`). 12.52 / 7.438 = 1.68.
   - That meets R13 (≥ 1.6, T-Motor minimum). It is below T-Motor's 2.0 "recommended" (`docs/research/propulsion-margin-data.md` §6, vendor source).
   - The real weak point is pack sag. With thrust scaling as V²:
     - about 1.51 at 21.0 V (3.5 V/cell, the `BATT_LOW_VOLT` value, `firmware/vtol_c0.param:177`);
     - about 1.34 at 19.8 V (3.3 V/cell, `Q_M_BAT_VOLT_MIN`).
   - So at the final hover and landing the lift units would run at roughly 66–75 % of available thrust. That is flyable, and it falls inside ArduPilot's expected hover-fraction range of 0.3–0.6 at full charge (same doc, line 110), but it leaves thin authority in gusts. This is why the 25 °C limit (R21) and the 5 m AGL / 60 s hover limits (R14) matter.
   - Judgement: accept, provided the mass is held. Do not accept any mass growth past the gate.
   - **Growth margin:** T/W reaches 1.6 at 7.82 kg (+5.2 %). Stall reaches 12.0 m/s at 7.76 kg (+4.3 %).

3. **Joiner reserve factor 1.41 (verified; the premise in the question needs correcting).**
   - The joiner is **not** composite or printed. It is a **6061-T6 aluminium tube, 0.875 × 0.058 in**, slip-fit inside 1.000 × 0.058 in spar sockets (`analysis/sizing.mjs:297`).
   - The 1.41 is already at **ultimate**: 3.0 g limit × 1.5 factor of safety = 4.5 g (`sizing.mjs:302`). It is measured against the 275 MPa SOLIDWORKS library yield. On the 240 MPa minimum yield it is 1.23 (`docs/validation/VALIDATION-MATRIX.md:48`, STR-07). At limit load, that is a factor of about 1.85 on minimum yield.
   - For a metallic tube with a 1.5 factor already applied, a reserve factor above 1 on minimum yield is acceptable for a hobby-class UAV. Judgement: acceptable.
   - Two caveats remain open:
     - **Envelope edge.** With `AIRSPEED_MAX,25` (`vtol_c0.param:116`), the stall line allows n = (25 / 11.75)² = 4.53 g. That uses up the whole 1.5 factor: RF is 1.25 on minimum yield at that point (`analysis/validation/claims_struct.json:503`). The 3.0 g limit is only consistent up to about 20.4 m/s.
     - **Load paths the beam calc does not model.** Line-contact bearing and ovalisation at the socket mouth are not modelled. The hard landing gives about 105 N·m and RF 1.07 (EST, STR-11), above the flight case. Both are test items, S01 and S06, not reasons to resize.
   - Gusts at cruise are capped by stall: at 18 m/s the wing stalls at 2.35 g. My Pegg-formula estimate gives 3.6 g only for an 8 m/s vertical gust, which the stall line would cut off first.
   - Every 5 % of mass growth costs about 0.06 on the reserve factor.

4. **Low AR 7.45 (verified arithmetic, judgement).**
   - The low AR follows from fixed span (2.4 m, set by R3/R4 and the tube lengths) plus the stall-driven area. It is not a free variable.
   - At the cruise CL of 0.475 and e = 0.8, CDi is 0.0121 at AR 7.45, against 0.0106 at rev D's AR 8.45. That is about 2.6 % of cruise drag (total CD 0.0546).
   - Endurance still passes: 12.0 min available against R10's 10 min (`results.json`, `t_cruise_avail_min`).
   - A smaller, higher-AR wing would need a higher design stall speed, which eats R11's 2.1 % margin. A longer span would need new tubes and a new car-fit check.
   - The penalty is small and accepted.

5. **Mass budget realism (judgement).**
   - The budget is honest for what is modelled. It does not include:
     - the pod internal structure, which is undefined in the CAD (VALIDATION-MATRIX:103);
     - the spar-socket adhesive, whose product is unspecified (MFG-11);
     - the repair patches and tape that every student foam build gains.
   - Wiring (370 g) and part of the avionics mass are estimates.
   - No growth allowance is carried. Student builds commonly come in 5–15 % over the CAD mass. That would land at 7.8–8.5 kg, past the 4–5 % margin.
   - So the mass gate, not the sizing, is the real control.

6. **R2 (verified source, decision).**
   - R2 comes from `docs/VTOL-REVIEW-PROMPT.md:20-24`, which also says "Treat the exact MTOW as a design output … show me where it lands in the range and why."
   - The design lands below 8 kg because the $2,000 cap and the mission close at 7.4 kg. Nothing forces mass up into the range.
   - Signing off a target the design does not meet, as-is, leaves a requirement that is permanently "not met" and still says nothing about the limit that actually matters.
   - **Revise R2** to:
     - record the outcome (7.44 kg, below the original 8–15 kg working target, with the reason);
     - set the binding limit: **take-off mass ≤ 7.75 kg as weighed (R11 and R13)**;
     - keep the < 25 kg hard cap.
   - Because R2 is "Given", the mentor or team should acknowledge the change at the next meeting. It is a wording change, not a design change.

**Surprise / inconsistency found:** The test plan already gates take-off mass at **7.82 kg** as "the R13 take-off limit of §8 R2" (`docs/test/TEST-PLAN.md`, S09 pass and G01 pass, around lines 390 and 489). At 7.82 kg the design stall is 12.04 m/s, which breaks R11 (≤ 12 m/s) by a small amount. The gate should be 7.75 kg, or R11 should explicitly accept 12.05 m/s. I recommend 7.75 kg.

**Risks / what would change the call:**
- **Weighed overshoot.** If S09 shows an empty mass above 6.93 kg (7.75 − 0.82 pack), act before flight, in this order:
  1. remove mass, such as the gear fairings' infill or the fasteners;
  2. move to a lighter pack, since endurance has about 2 min of margin, and coordinate with the battery decision;
  3. only then consider a bigger prop or higher-KV lift set, which affects spend under D5–D9.
- **D4 restores payload or ballast.** Re-size; do not adopt as-is.
- **The thrust stand (TEST-PLAN C07) reads below the maker's table.** Every 3 % of thrust shortfall moves T/W by about 0.05. Below about 3.0 kg per unit at 22.2 V, R13 fails at 7.44 kg.
- **The team wants the full 2.0 T/W.** That is not reachable on this motor set without about 1.2 kg of mass reduction, so it would mean a propulsion change and spend.
- **S01 proof load** shows tip deflection or socket damage. Revisit the joiner wall or sleeve, at a small cost.
