# D14. Tilt-wing status

**Recommendation:** Confirm `explorations/tiltwing/` as archived (read-only reference, not a competing configuration), and harvest three small items into C0 docs; correct the DECISIONS.md wording that says the trade study "ranks C0 first".

**Confidence:** High on archiving. The tilt-wing fails a Given requirement (R1), the $2,000 cap, and the schedule by wide margins, and no plausible scoring change closes that. Medium on how far the study's absolute scores can be trusted: they carry a stale-data caveat, noted below.

**Depends on:** D1 (C0 stays the single baseline; assumed yes), D2 (cruise speed; does not flip this), D5-D9 (spend; the tilt-wing is over cap before any of them). Nothing here would flip unless R1 is formally waived by the sponsor or mentor, which nothing in the repo suggests.

**Reasoning:**

1. **What the trade study actually says.** `analysis/tradestudy.json` does NOT rank C0 first overall. Overall feasible ranking: TS-quad 7.13, TS-duo 6.05, then C0-b3.0 5.82 (ranking.feasible). The tailsitters break R1, so the study ranks them separately; C0-family variants lead the R1-compliant list (ranking.feasibleR1: C0-b3.0, C0-b2.8, C0-lean, ... plain C0 is 7th at 5.51). DESIGN-BASIS.md:61 words it correctly ("first among layouts that meet R1"); DECISIONS.md:166 does not.
2. **Tilt-wing result (TW-4).** Rank 28 of 29, total 2.30, `meetsR1:false`, `feasible:false`, flag "R6: total $2105.34 > $2,000 cap" (reserve -$105.34 vs C0 +$145.45). Per-criterion: cost 0, build 2.5, control 2, schedule 1, motor-out 2, growth 0.96; only energy (3.1 vs C0 2.0) and portability (7.6 vs 6.5) beat C0. Hover gust tilt needed 28 deg vs C0 9 deg (R16 exposure; the whole planform is a sail).
3. **Fairness check (same budget, same constraints).** Mostly fair, if anything generous to tilt-wing:
   - Same sizing loop (`model.js size()` unchanged), same 2.4 m span, same $2,000 cap, same 10.25 % tax and shipping method, same BOM base with explicit pivot parts priced from named vendors (tradestudy.mjs:250-262).
   - Generous: parked-rotor drag removed (CdA_rotor 0.0007 vs C0 about 0.0034), single 2.2 m boom (0.50 kg vs 0.74 kg), cruise L/D 10.7 vs 8.7, cruise margin 31 % vs 20 %. Pivot reinforcement is only a 0.15 kg estimate (no source), so real mass and cost are likely worse.
   - Judgement-based (the weak spot): controlRisk 8 and scheduleRisk 9 are rubric guesses, but backed by evidence: one documented ArduPilot tilt-wing build (CL-84, transition trouble, [S24]) and only a passing mention in the ArduPilot tilt-rotor page [S15]. Even halving both risks to 5 adds only about 0.85 points (2.30 to about 3.15), nowhere near C0's 5.51. Hard constraints (R1, R6 cap) are not rubric-dependent.
   - Caveat: the tradestudy.json header is dated 2026-10-25 but tradestudy.mjs already carries BOM edits "rev 2026-11-01". configurations.md quotes a different current table (C0-lean 6.04 first among R1) than the JSON (C0-b3.0 5.82 first). So the JSON is stale relative to the script; absolute totals will move, the TW-4 verdict will not (it is over cap by $105-149 in both runs, configurations.md:136).
4. **The archived study is a different aircraft.** `explorations/tiltwing/` models the Cakir and Kurtulus (2022) paper: 2.0 m span, 9.5 kg, 2.5 kg payload, 4 tilting wing rotors plus a tilting tail with 2 rotors, S1223 wing (explorations/tiltwing/model.js header; README). It is not sized to the team's $2,000 / 7.4 kg / 2.4 m problem, so it cannot be a drop-in competitor; making it one means a re-baseline (new CAD, BOM, FEA, ArduPilot plan).
5. **Mechanical complexity.** Per the trade study build index, tilt-wing 27 vs C0 16 (limit 32 = unbuildable): wing pivot through the joiner (worth 6x weight in the index), 2 tilt servos plus bearings, yoke, reinforced fuselage, plus a separate tail pitch fan. Transition means managing wing stall and a moving CG/pitch; ArduPilot support is thin (S15, S24). C0 uses the main documented path (QuadPlane Q_FRAME_CLASS 1, [S17]) and the firmware, params and CAD already exist.
6. **Cost and schedule to April 2027.** About 5.5 months remain (today 2026-11-09). C0 has CAD, BOM, params and analysis; tilt-wing has none at the team's scale, and the mentor's 11-04 guidance is explicit: converge now, one baseline, construction practicality first, do not spend effort on sims a design change invalidates. A competing configuration contradicts all three, and the tilt mechanism is exactly the kind of mechanism-heavy item that fails first builds.
7. **Why not "keep as a competitor in a limited form".** A live competitor needs maintained dashboards, a second BOM and a re-run of the trade study with each C0 change, and consumes the four-person team's bandwidth. Archive costs nothing; the files stay in the repo and can be revived after the April flight test.

**Risks / what would change the call:**
- R1 formally relaxed by sponsor or mentor (then re-run the study, but TW-4 still fails the cap and the tailsitters would win, not the tilt-wing).
- Budget increase above about $2,250 AND a mentor request for a transition-capable airframe: still would favour a tilt-rotor or tailsitter before tilt-wing on control maturity.
- C0 hover or transition testing fails badly in spring and a pivot is needed; the archived work is then a starting point, not a plan.
- The stale-JSON issue: if someone re-runs the study before quoting numbers, the ranking text changes slightly (not the TW-4 conclusion).
