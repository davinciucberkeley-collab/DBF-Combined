# D1. Design baseline for the whole team

**Recommendation:** Adopt the 11-02 resize (v0.4.0, MTOW 7.438 kg, chord 322.3 mm, SOLIDWORKS CAD rev G) as the single team baseline now, and do not reopen it. Migrate the v0.3.1 artifacts in the order given below, and freeze the Fusion model and the A1/A2 variants as historical records instead of rebuilding them.

**Confidence:** High on adopting it. The 11-02 numbers come from a bottom-up mass accounting and a measured-basis CL_max. The v0.3.1 numbers are known to leave out about 0.53 kg of hardware and assume CL_max 1.2 with zero stall margin, so reopening would mean going back to a design whose mass and stall basis are already shown wrong. Medium on the migration cost estimates: they come from reading the builders and notes, and nothing was run. The Google Sheet is private and was not seen.

**Depends on:**
- **D2 (cruise 18 or 20 m/s): does not flip D1.** The wing area is set by stall (S = 2mg / (ρ Vs² CL_max), `docs/research/stall-margin.md` line 61) and the pack is fixed (`params.EBattFixed`). So Vc moves cruise power, endurance and the param file, not the wing, the CAD or the BOM. Assumed: D2 stays at 18 m/s. If it goes to 20 m/s, re-run the sizing, but the geometry and this migration plan stand.
- **D5–D10 (plug, ESC, airspeed sensor, GPS, spare motor, spend):** these do not change the baseline, but they change the BOM that `inventory/` and the Sheet get re-seeded from. **Assumed: they are settled before step 4 below**, so the BOM is regenerated once, not twice.
- **D13 (build-reference CAD): assumed SOLIDWORKS rev G, with Fusion frozen.** If the team picks Fusion as the build reference instead, D1 still holds, but the Fusion rebuild below becomes required. It is the single most expensive item.
- D3 (R2 sign-off) and D4 (no payload or ballast) follow from this decision. Adopting 11-02 means accepting 7.438 kg and no ballast.

**Reasoning:**

*Why the resize was made, and whether it is sound (verified from files):*
1. **The mass was under-counted, and 11-02 fixes that by calibrating to the CAD.** Comparing `analysis/results.json` with `inventory/v031/analysis/results.json`:
   - v0.3.1 has no fastener, wing-hardware or tail-hardware mass at all (`base.mFasteners`, `mWingHardware` and `mTailHardware` are absent). 11-02 has 0.164 + 0.253 + 0.115 = **0.53 kg** for them *(calc)*.
   - Several items grew once they were drawn: avionics 0.25 → 0.418 kg, gear 0.325 → 0.517 kg, boom clamp 0.02 → 0.0445 kg per position, servos 0.216 → 0.266 kg.
   - The bottom-up roll-up comes to 7.416 kg against 7.438 kg from the sizing (`analysis/mass/mass_budget.json` `total_kg`, `groups`; script `analysis/mass/mass_budget.py`). The reason is recorded in `docs/DESIGN-BASIS.md` line 62 ("sizing calibrated to the CAD mass roll-up") and in validation next step 2 (`docs/validation/VALIDATION-REPORT.md` line 125: "motor mount 88.7 g vs 20 g allowance").
2. **The stall basis was optimistic.** v0.3.1 sized the wing on an assumed CL_max of 1.2 at Vs 12.0 m/s, which leaves zero margin to R11. 11-02 uses the aero-package CL_max of 1.115 at a design stall of 11.75 m/s, which holds a 4.1 % CL_max margin (`base.CLmax`, `base.Vs`; `docs/research/stall-margin.md` lines 18–20, 35). Higher mass plus lower CL_max is why S grew from 0.573 to 0.774 m².
3. **The aircraft that results is better in cruise and still passes the requirements.**
   - L/D rises from 6.51 to 8.71, because CD0 was re-derived from the drag build-up: 0.059 → 0.0425.
   - Cruise power falls from 365 to 296 W, and cruise time available is 12.0 min against 11.9 min (`sized.LD`, `CD0`, `P_cruise`, `t_cruise_avail_min`).
   - R10, R11 and R13 are met (`docs/DESIGN-BASIS.md` lines 52–59).
   - The CAD is built on the same numbers: `cad/params.txt` line 6 `chord=0.322333` matches `sized.chord`.
   - The CAD, the drawings (rev G), FEA, thermal, CFD, the param file and the BOM are all already on this baseline (DECISIONS.md baseline table).
4. **Reopening goes against the mentor guidance of 2026-11-04 (BRIEF).** Every downstream artifact the build needs (drawings, build manual, test plan, firmware) is on 11-02. Only the inventory, Fusion and research side lags, and that side is mostly data-entry and documentation. No v0.3.1 artifact has engineering content that would argue against the 11-02 numbers. The DBF-VTOL findings that still applied have already been carried across (`docs/research/team-crosscheck.md` §1–§3).

*Real weaknesses of 11-02 (judgement, with numbers). None of them argues for v0.3.1:*
- **Hover T/W of 1.68 against the R13 floor of 1.6.** With the maker's thrust of 3.13 kg per motor *(calc from `sized.TW` × m / 4)*, the aircraft can grow only **0.39 kg** before R13 fails, ignoring the wing snowball *(calc: 4 × 3.13 / 1.6 − 7.438)*. Of that mass, 1.155 kg is still estimates (printed parts not weighed, `mass_budget.json` `est_share_kg`).
- Joiner reserve factor is 1.41 at 4.5 g. The hard-landing joiner moment (~105 N·m EST) exceeds the 91 N·m flight case (`VALIDATION-REPORT.md` line 104).
- Wing and pod manufacturing readiness is "Not ready" (`VALIDATION-REPORT.md` lines 104, 109).

These are open items to close on the 11-02 baseline. v0.3.1 has the same hardware, and its higher T/W comes from the mass it leaves out.

**Risks / what would change the call:**
- **Weighed parts come in heavy.** If the printed and foam parts weigh more than estimated and MTOW rises more than about 0.39 kg, R13 fails. The fix is then a lighter build or a prop/motor change on the 11-02 baseline, not a return to v0.3.1. Weigh first-article parts early (test plan) and re-run the sizing once.
- **Thrust stand (TEST-PLAN C07) shows less than maker thrust.** The T/W margin then shrinks. The same response applies: it is a hardware change, not a baseline change.
- **D13 picks Fusion as the build reference.** Then the Fusion rebuild becomes required, and should be budgeted before any printing.
- **The Google Sheet already holds substantial live order data.** Then re-seeding needs a careful merge, not an overwrite. Check with Tristan before step 5.
- One thing would justify reopening: evidence that the 11-02 mass roll-up **over**-counts (e.g. weighed parts much lighter). Then a smaller wing would be possible. Nothing in the repo suggests that.
