# C4 fin / motor-tube structures: closure round 2026-11-14

Role C4 (fin and motor-tube structures). Brief: `RULES.md`, `WAVE.md` (this folder); A1 D-147 (`DESIGN-ROUND-2026-11-14/A1.md`);
my wave reports `DESIGN-ROUND-2026-11-14/C4.md`, `C4-W2.md`. I edited only `team/C4/`. I did not run git, `proofs/build_graph.py`,
`fusion/check.py`, `merge_check.py` or any other role's script. FE jobs ran at 4 threads, one at a time. `EST` marks a hand estimate; "—" means
unknown.

**Verdict.** The four failing C4 claims are all R1 concept-model rows for options that are not in the design. They are re-classed as
information, each with its reason (§1.1). The real open item was the knock-down tube case: `CL-C4-SIM-SEP` at E 78.6 / G 10.27 GPa was
**0.189**, below 0.20, so GT-16 decided R-VIBRATION. It is now a gated claim, **`CL-C4-SIM-SEP-KNOCK`**, and one design change closes it.
**Both motor-mount cheeks get 1.5 mm thicker, built outward: outboard 2.5 → 4.0 mm, inboard 2.4 → 3.9 mm.** With that change, in the 3-D model on
three meshes:

- Knock-down separation: **0.189 → 0.2045** (lower quarter governs; upper 0.228 → 0.248).
- Nominal separation: **0.244 → 0.2685**.

The pass at the pessimistic end stacks two low-end values. The tube is at the knock-down, and the mount rocking stiffness is taken at the low
end of the printed ASA modulus (1.8 GPa). Cost: **+10.1 g** for the aircraft (`EST`, +2.53 g per mount) and **$0**: the ASA spool is already on
the BOM (W08). The mass is the price. A3 / A1 must move 10 g from the growth reserve to the fins allocation (§4).

Other levers, not chosen:

- **Station collar position.** It already sits against the mount collar (gap 0.5 mm, `fin_detail.station_collar.gap_m`), so it cannot move toward the
  station. The grip row shows that moving the collar and brace ear away from the station costs more than the stiffer mount gains (−0.13 %).
- **Brace 10 × 2 → 10 × 3.** +0.37 % at nominal for +9 g (`CL-C4-SIM-LEVER-BRACE3`). Worse per gram than the cheeks.
- **Mount mass.** −10 g per station would give +3.7 % (`CL-C4-SIM-LEVER-LIGHT10`), but the mount is 11.67 g. No such saving exists.
- **Stiffer tube.** Rock West **35045-S** (https://www.rockwestcomposites.com/35045-s.html, checked 2026-11-14):
  - Filament wound, 0.875 × 0.625 in, ±15 / ±45 / hoop / ±45 / ±15. EX 1.048E7 psi = 72.3 GPa, GXY 3 632 000 psi = 25.0 GPa.
  - 0.194 lb/ft = 0.289 kg/m; **$49.99** per 12 in; backorder, 2–4 weeks.
  - EI × 1.29 and GJ × 1.96 against 35046-S `EST` (wall-section ratio 1.61). It costs **+91.5 g** (2 × 0.344 m × 0.133 kg/m), about $220 for 48 in `EST`,
    and a new 15.9 mm bore plug. Rejected on mass. The wave-1 roll-wrapped options (ECF 22 × 19, 22 × 18) failed already at nominal (`C4.md` §1.5).
- **Lower cruise rpm.** At the knock-down f1 the rule passes with cruise ≤ 251.56 / 1.2 × 30 = **6 289 rpm** (−0.9 %, `EST`). That is B4 / B1's
  decision (D-145 set 17 m/s), so no request is made. With the cheek change the limit rises to 6 371 rpm at the knock-down (§2).
- **CF-filled ASA for the mount.** A reseller table gives a flexural modulus of 3 740 MPa against 1 920 MPa for plain ASA
  (search result 2026-11-14; Bambu's own TDS could not be opened, so "—"). That would raise `k_rock` at no mass. Not chosen: the value is not verified
  at its source, the part needs a hardened nozzle, and the enclosed-printer availability is "—".

| Item | Today (R6) | Change (R7 request) |
|---|---|---|
| Outboard (slit-side) cheek `fin_detail.mount.cheek_t_m` | 2.5 mm | **4.0 mm** |

| Mount stiffness estimate (`CALC-C4-D3-MOUNT-K-EST`), weaker axis | 2 518 (E 2.0) / 2 266 (E 1.8) | **3 455 / 3 109** |
| Model value `ASM-C4-MOUNT-K-ROCK` | 2 200 | **3 100** after D3 R7 (rule: the estimate at ASA E 1.8 GPa, rounded down; the rule is now written into the assumption) |
| Mount mass, no hardware (`ASM-C4-MOUNT-CAD`) | 11.67 g | 14.20 g `EST` (slabs: free cheek length × depth 39 mm × 1.5 mm × 1.07 g/cm³), CG dx −10.5 / dy +0.2 / dz −9.5 mm `EST`; D3's CAD value replaces it |

Why the cheeks: in the fore-aft mode the station rocks on the mount, and the mount's weaker axis is the cheek body, not the 18 mm collar grip
(foundation 10 741 against body 3 289 / 3 349 N·m/rad). Cheek thickness raises both the nodding (∝ t) and the sway (∝ (y + t/2)²) stiffness.
