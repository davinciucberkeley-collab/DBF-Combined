1. The volts column holds the 4S / 3S LiPo nominal ratings (3.70 V/cell), and the W column is volts × amps

2. Read with held voltages, the five rows contradict each other: no single station fits them.
3. Read with the supply sagging, they agree. That is the first bound, built on the maker's own R_m.
4. Any second bound puts at least 1.5× the maker-stated winding resistance into the motor. The resistance beyond
   R_m belongs to the maker's rig (supply, leads, ESC). K09-L does not carry that rig: its pack, harness and ESC are
   modelled separately (`ASM-E1-PACK-CELLS`, `electrical.r_ext_mohm`, `ASM-E1-ESC`).

## 3. Design levers checked (9° cant, 2.6 kg, LiPo, E1 station model)
A "window" is the band of `Q_M_BAT_CURR_MAX` values that holds T/W ≥ 2.0 fresh and winding current ≤ 25 A.
B2′ is the least pessimistic bound-2 station that the maker's data allow (0.157 Ω, `CL-E1-R-UB-MIN`).

| Prop | First-bound window | Second-bound window (0.218 Ω) | B2′ window (0.157 Ω) | Overlaps the first bound? | Other limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11×5.5MR (carried) | 69.37–71.07 A | 92.30–94.80 A | 80.55–82.63 A | no | — |
| 11×5.5E / EP | 68.69–77.71 A | ≥ 89.09 A | 78.66–89.57 A | no (0.95 A gap to B2′) | +24 g |
| 11×4.5MR | 66.33–78.41 A | — | 75.72–90.29 A | B2′ only (75.7–78.4 A) | cruise 362.8 W (`CL-E1-CRUISE-P-45`) against `cruise_power_w` 295: fails R-MISSION |

- **Why no limiter value can serve both bounds.** Winding current and thrust both depend only on rpm (I_m =
  Q/Kt + I0). How much pack current buys that rpm depends on the station resistance. A pack-current limiter therefore
  cannot hold the same thrust on two stations that differ by 0.06–0.12 Ω.
- **No rpm limit in ArduPilot.** ArduPilot has no rpm-based output limit. The ESC rpm parameters are take-off checks
  only (`TKOFF_RPM_MIN` / `MAX`, https://ardupilot.org/plane/docs/common-tkoff-rpm-min.html, checked 2026-11-14).
- **Motor change: not pursued.** The bound comes from the maker's rig, not from the motor. Another motor's maker
  table carries the same ambiguity. Mass, cost and a new mount design are "—" (not sized). Five motors would be
  re-bought, against a budget that is already $512 over.
- **Limiter value per bound** (D-148's 93 A after the stand): **withdrawn**. It was a test-gated hold. At 93 A the
  first bound puts 28.9 A in the winding.

## 4. Risk found while checking: the R_m band of the 70.2 A limiter
- `CL-E1-ILIM-RM-LO` / `-HI` (new, information) give the band the limiter accepts: **85.48–86.30 mΩ** at 25 °C.
  The window moves about 0.18 A per mΩ of R_m, and the ±1.1 % sensor band uses 1.1 of its ±1.21 %.
- The cause is the prop, not the limiter. At 25 A of winding current the 11×5.5MR gives T/W 2.029
  (`CL-E1-ILIM-25A-TW`), only 1.5 % above 2.0. Any electrical limiter must hold thrust inside that 1.5 %.
- The maker publishes no R_m tolerance (—). A copper temperature 2 K above 25 °C at a fresh-pack full-throttle event
  already moves R_m out of the band.
- **Robust option (`EST`, same model): 11×5.5E / 11×5.5EP at 73.2 A, sensor ±1.1 %.**
  - R_m band **66.4–108.9 mΩ** (−23 % / +27 %).
  - Fresh T/W 2.078 at 24.2 A winding; worst 1.799.
  - Cruise about −9 % (E1.md wave 1, `design_round_1114.json`).
  - Mass **+24 g** flying (4 × (23 − 17) g).
  - Price: $4.17 against $3.83 each (APC pages, checked 2026-11-14, `review/DESIGN-ROUND-2026-11-14/E1.md` §2.1).
    For B03's 12 props that is +$4.08 before tax. Dropping B04 (4 × 11×4.5MR stand props, $16.89) gives a net of
    about **−$12.4**.
  - The hub is 0.41 in thick against 0.35 in, so the stack and `CL-E1-TIP-GAP-TUBE` must be re-run (D3 / E1) before
    the change.
- **E1 recommends** the 11×5.5E/EP if A3 can carry 24 g. If not, keep the MR. The 85.5–86.3 mΩ band then has to be
  written down as an accepted risk (A1 / J6). It is not a fail today; I am not picking a side silently.

### 2. How long does a motor sit at the cap? (B3's data)
