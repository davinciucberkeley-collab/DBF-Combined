**Why this box passes the drop and carry claims:**
- **Floor drop, R-TRANSPORT 0.3 m.** The load path is the PE crush column, not the shell. The columns stay 60 × 50 ×
  120 mm, now three 2 in layers with one layer cut to 18.6 mm. `CL-D7-CRADLE-STROKE` 66 mm vs 54 mm needed, and
  `CL-D7-DROP-N` 10.09 vs 12.11: both unchanged.
  - The floor is backed by the ground in a flat drop, so the board does not carry the column load.
  - The column layers are stacked and tape-wrapped instead of LSE-laminated. Stacked layers in compression act in
    series, like a laminate. The tape buckles and carries no axial load.
  - The Walmart foam density is "—". `T-D7-CUSHION` on this foam sets the column footprint, a free cut size, so that
    the static stress stays in the 2–7 kPa band. The stroke (strain × 120 mm) does not depend on the footprint.
  - The test stays as confirmation (M5).
- **Floor and lid splices.** The floor and lid are two 610 × 565 pieces plus a 30 mm strip each. Every splice and
  fold score runs across the 0.565 m span, so the floor still spans wall to wall without a hinge.
- **Hand holes** (`CALC-D7-HANDHOLE`).
  - Load case: one-person side carry, loaded box at 2 g on one hole: F = 5.81 × 2 × 9.807 = 113.9 N.
  - Line load: q = F / (3 layers × 0.085 m grip) = 0.447 kN/m.
  - Allowable: ECT 32 lb/in = 5.60 kN/m per layer, divided by SF 2 (humid air, uneven grip, `EST`) = 2.80 kN/m.
    Margin +84 %.
  - Positions: the side hole is on the **aft** wall at y 0. A front-wall hole would put the fingers into the 12 mm nose
    gap; on the aft wall they reach x ≈ 0.36, 40 mm aft of the wing TE, above the feet.
- **Lashings.** Two paracord loops, slipped trucker's hitch, over folded edge saddles. They replace the cam straps for
  the 5 mm pillar preload. The setup time is unchanged: pulling the slipped-hitch tails takes about as long as
  undoing a cam (30 s step, `CL-D7-SETUP-MIN` 4.75 min, `EST` until `T-D7-SETUP`).
- **Mass.** Box 3.59 → **3.48 kg**, loaded 5.92 → **5.81 kg** vs 6.0 (+1 % → +3 %).
  - Removed: 3 handles, ply strips and the cam straps. Added: hand-hole patches and cords.
  - The shell is still costed at the Coroplast 0.76 kg/m² as an upper bound. The board's areal mass is "—" until a
    blank is weighed.
- **Known weakness.** Board loses strength when wet. The box rides in the car and is rebuilt from 4 new blanks
  ($15.35 incl. tax) if soaked. This is written in `CHECKLIST.md` and `SPEC.md`.

**Smaller design rejected:** cam straps kept (Husky 6 ft 4-pack, $9.87 at Home Depot, snippet), joined two per loop
because a 6 ft strap cannot go round the 2.21 m girth. That design costs $49.90 incl. tax and fails the $40 limit.
