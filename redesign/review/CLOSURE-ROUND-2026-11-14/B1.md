### 3. Fixed-wing climb, 17 m/s, 2.70 kg, THR_MAX 41 with FWD_BAT compensation (F4 D-174)
Model: four props at output 0.41 on 17.4 V (fresh) and output 0.41 x 17.4 / 14.4 = 0.4954 on a 13.9 V loaded bus (3.6 V/cell resting), hot winding, thrust times cos 10 deg, trimmed drag with the turbulator.

| Pack | Axial thrust | Drag | **Rate of climb** | `TECS_CLMB_MAX` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fresh 17.4 V | 11.82 N | 5.25 N | **4.22 m/s** (F4 `EST` 4.9) | 3.0 PASS (+41 %) |
| Sagged 3.6 V/cell | 10.31 N | 5.25 N | **3.25 m/s** (F4 `EST` 2.9) | 3.0 PASS (+8 %) |
| Sagged, pessimistic drag (CD0 x 1.3, k 0.1107, 6.20 N) | 10.31 N | 6.20 N | **2.64 m/s** | 3.0 not reached (-12 %) |

Without the turbulator the sagged climb is 3.43 m/s. In the pessimistic-drag sagged case the aircraft cannot deliver the 3 m/s TECS may ask for: TECS then saturates at `THR_MAX` and trades speed
within `TECS_SINK_MAX` (AIRSPEED_MIN 17 stays the floor). It is a controller setting, not a physical limit: F4 may lower `TECS_CLMB_MAX` to 2.5 to match (this loosens nothing), or leave 3 and accept
saturation late in the pack. B1 does not ask for either.
