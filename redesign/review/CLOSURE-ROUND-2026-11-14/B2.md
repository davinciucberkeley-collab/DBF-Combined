**Why the upper trip does not work.**
- On the free polar the upper-surface transition point moves forward with CL: x/c 0.57 → 0.38 over CL 0.26 → 0.69.
- The lower surface stays laminar to the trailing edge (`xtr_bot` 1.000 at every cruise CL).
- The asymmetric boundary layer at the trailing edge is what gives the positive dcm/dcl. An upper trip makes the
  asymmetry larger; a lower trip removes it.
- The lower trip still works if it acts late, anywhere from x/c 0.05 to 0.60. That covers the case
  (Gopalarathnam et al., below) where a real trip does not cause transition at the tape.

**CLmax with the trip.**

- Near clmax the natural upper transition is already at x/c 0.036–0.06, so an upper trip at 0.05 changes nothing there.

### 2.2 Trip drag and trip height (`EST`)

**Source for the device drag.** Gopalarathnam, Broughton, McGranahan, Selig, *Design of Low Reynolds Number Airfoils
with Trips*, J. Aircraft 40(4), 2003.
https://m-selig.web.engr.illinois.edu/pubs/GopalarathnamEtal-2003-JofAC-LRNAirfoiLDesignWithTrips.pdf
(read 2026-11-14).
- p. 770: XFOIL's fixed transition assumes instantaneous transition and carries **no device drag**. Real trips often
  act downstream of the tape.
- pp. 771–773: a 0.0135 in (0.343 mm) tape on a 12 in chord (k/c 0.00113, ours is 0.0022 at 0.7 mm), on the upper
  surface only, at Re 3e5.
  - At Cl 0–0.4 it measured a net drag change of −0.001 to −0.002 on the SA7025.
  - Forward trips (0.1c, 0.2c) raised the drag at Cl about 0.75 (loss of laminar run).
- That measured net effect includes the device drag.

**Hand model for the device drag** (`ASM-B2C-TRIP-CDK`, `EST`):
- Δcd_dev = C_Dk (k/c)(u_k/V)², with C_Dk = 1.0.
- u_k comes from a Pohlhausen (Λ 0) laminar profile on XFOIL's Ue and δ*, from a DUMP of the untripped wing just ahead
  of the tape.
- C_Dk 1.0 is a pessimistic value for a 2-D step whose top is inside the layer. I found no published C_Dk for zigzag
  tape. I tried Hoerner-based reports on DTIC and NTRS, and they were not readable.
- The XFOIL laminar-run loss plus this term gives Δcd +0.0040. That is an upper bound against the measured −0.002 … +
  small net effect above.

**Trip height.**
- Sized for Re_k = u_k k / ν ≥ 600 at 1.3 Vs and 2.64 kg (u_k / V 0.92; laminar layer k/δ 0.57). That gives **0.7 mm**,
  with Re_k 634 at 1.3 Vs, 703 at cruise and 429 near the stall.
- 600 is the 3-D-roughness value, as cited in the FFA-W3-270 zigzag-tape study:
  https://semarakilmu.com.my/journals/index.php/fluid_mechanics_thermal_sciences/article/view/2645 (search abstract,
  2026-11-14; the page returns 403, so this is secondary).
- A TU Delft thesis gives about 125–200 for zigzag tape (search result, 2026-11-14; secondary). 600 is therefore
  pessimistic.
- Below 1.3 Vs the trip need not act: the NP term binds at 1.3 Vs, and untripped clmax is higher (1.256 vs 1.246).

**Cruise drag and power.**
- ΔD = q S Δcd = 177.0 Pa × 0.368 m² × 0.00403 = **0.262 N**. The tape runs full span, and the pod is on the upper
  surface, so the full S is charged.
- ΔP_elec = ΔD V × P/(D V), where P/(D V) = 263.03 W / (4.928 N × 17 m/s) = 3.140. The inputs are E1's
  `CL-E1-CRUISE-P-55-V17` and `CL-E1-CRUISE-DRAG` at 2.64 kg and 10° cant. This is the average ratio, `EST`.
- Result: ΔP = **+14.0 W, +5.3 %** of 263 W.
- B3's Δcd 0.0067 would give +23.3 W by the same ratio.

**Lower x/c options** (same method, k sized the same way):

|---|---|---|---|
| x/c 0.05 | 0.0009 | 0.0124 | +16.2 W |

| **x/c 0.20 (chosen)** | 0.0007 | 0.0118 | +14.0 W |

- Upper 0.05 plus lower 0.05 reads cd 0.0173 and **fails** the cd line.

### Is `requirements.stall_max_ms` 12.3 still met?
**Yes, at both CG limits, clean and tripped.** The tightest case is the tripped forward-CG stall: 12.257 m/s against 12.3 (+0.043 m/s, 0.35 %; re-checked after B3's last run: unchanged). That is
thin; it is the same case that sets the transition gate. Nothing needs loosening, so the limit stays at 12.3.
Basis re-derived, as asked, for the record (`CL-B2C-VS-BOUND`, information): the 12.3 of D-96 was AIRSPEED_MIN 16 / 1.3. With the integer
AIRSPEED_MIN 17 (D-122) and the airspeed error `requirements.airspeed_err_max_ms` 0.5 m/s (an indicated 17 can be a true 16.5), the physical bound is
(17 - 0.5) / 1.3 = **12.69 m/s**. The 12.3 is therefore 0.39 m/s tighter than the transition gate needs. The gate itself passes with the error counted: 1.3 x tripped stall + 0.5
= 16.43 m/s forward, 16.35 m/s aft, both <= 17. I do not ask A1 to raise 12.3; it is not a physical limit B2 should relax.
