## 2. A2's profile, implemented

The changes to `energy_claims.py`:

- **Hover hold is derived.** `ASM-E2-PROFILE` no longer carries `hover_s` 120. A new helper, `hover_hold_s()`,
  computes the hold from `requirements.hover_time_min` and `requirements.goaround_hover_s`:
  - hold = 3.0 × 60 − 50/2.0 − 50/1.0 − 30 − 40 = **35 s** (climb 25 s, descent 50 s, landing hover 30 s, go-around 40 s);
  - the profile gains `landing_hover_s` 30;
  - the script stops with an error if the hold would come out negative;
  - `budget()` and every calc that calls it read the two params through `COMMON`.
- **Go-around in the `BATT_LOW` need.** `failsafe_rows()` adds `goaround_hover_s` at hover current to the landing
  need: need = back-transition + descent + 60 s return cruise + (30 + 40) s hover at the hover current.
  `CALC-E2-FAILSAFE`, `-BUDGET-240` and `-BUDGET-V17` all pass it through, and a new output `goaround_mah`
  reports the go-around's share.

## 3. Pack trade and pack mass

**Corrected trade model (`CALC-E2-PACK-TRADE`).**
- **Old test:** each candidate was set into `CL-A1-MASS-SIZED` (2.64 kg) and checked against the gate. Because
  2.64 kg is already above the 2.6 kg gate, no pack could ever pass.
- **New test:** each pack is judged as flown. The pessimistic aircraft stays at the 2.6 kg gate. The pack must pass
  five criteria:
  1. mass: listed + tolerance ≤ `CL-A3-PACK-ACCEPT`;
  2. mission: cruise to `BATT_LOW` at 17 m/s ≥ `cruise_time_min`, with that pack's own `BATT_LOW`;
  3. landing: charge left at touchdown ≥ `BATT_CRT`;
  4. current: continuous current ≥ the 70.2 A limiter;
  5. leads: 10 AWG or heavier.
- `CL-E2-PACK-LARGEST` (id kept for the graph links) now reads the named pack's Ah if it passes all five, else 0.
