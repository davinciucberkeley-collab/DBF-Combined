### XT60H fit at the pod side exits: FAIL

Scratch fit check (every body of every built part, axis-aligned boxes, conservative on the right pair's diagonal; `HRN-113-*` at the current
station centres): none of the four plugs fits the zones D2 cut for the 20.1 x 10.2 x 5.2 mm XT30U.

| Plug | Overlap with |
|---|---|
| LL (tunnel, outboard, low) | wing core `WNG-001` by 2.0 mm in z, PDB rib `POD-002` 2.9 mm |
| UL (tunnel, outboard, high) | cradle floor `PWR-006` 0.95 mm |
| LR / UR (last run to the right exit) | pod side wall `POD-001` 2.0 mm, handle leg `POD-007` 7.0 mm, bracket flange `POD-007` 1.2 mm |

A search for a free 30 x 15.8 x 8.1 mm pocket (either orientation, 1 mm grid) along each pair's pod route finds **none** (0 of 4); only
the nose, x -0.140 ... -0.108 (ahead of the pack), has room. Width 15.8 mm is the limit: with 10.2 mm width 12-24 positions per pair exist.
The XT60H bodies in `params.json` carry the true size, so the 3-D gate will report these overlaps until the pod is changed.

**Fix (D2, one of):**
1. Re-cut the pod for four 30 x 15.8 x 8.1 mm pockets: right pair stacked 17 mm tall at the exit (side-exit window is 12 mm tall: plug sits
   inboard of it), left pair in series along the tunnel (2 x 30 mm of the 56 mm cradle length, floor raised 1 mm), or
2. put the four plugs in the nose pocket (x -0.140 ... -0.108) with the 16 AWG pairs 0.20-0.25 m longer each
   (+0.9 m of pair at 32.3 g/m = +28 g `EST`, ESC leads then 0.8-0.95 m, within F1's 1.83 m allowance; A1 allocation +28 g more), which also removes the plugs from the congested tunnel.
