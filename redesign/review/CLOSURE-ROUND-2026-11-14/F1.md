# F1 power distribution: closure round 2026-11-14 (R6, then R7 / R8)

> **Read § D-173 first.** D-173 / D-173b / D-174 reverted the Kakute (D-161c). They replaced the motor, the props, the limiter (80 A) and the pack (LiHV). Sections 1–6 below are the R6 record; § D-173 is the design of record.

Role F1 (`redesign/TEAM.md`). Scope: Kakute H743-Wing power path (D-161c), the 73.2 A limiter re-run (D-161), A3
lever L6 (one ESC cap instead of two), the 17.4 V LiHV check (E2 D-163).

**Verdict.**
- The Kakute path passes. `PWR-003` and `W-PMOD` are gone. Main path AS150U → Kakute battery pads → Kakute ESC pads →
  bus board in 10 AWG, every lead through a ≥ 40 mm S-loop tied to the deck.
- At 73.2 A every F1 current, drop, connector and heating claim passes.
- **L6: no.** One cap per ESC fails the ripple-current rating by 2.5×, on either part. The real finding is wider: the
  booked design (two Panasonic FR 680 µF 25 V per ESC) was never rated for the ripple either (3.55 A per can against
  1.79 A). CR-F2-18 / CDR-EL-05 were open since 2026-11-10.
- **Fix (design change):** `PWR-008` becomes **three Rubycon 35PJV560M10X16.5** hybrid-polymer cans per ESC (560 µF
  35 V, 11 mΩ, 4.0 A at 100 kHz). Three cans pass at the pessimistic ripple-frequency factor. Two would pass if the
  maker table gives ≥ 0.86 at 48 kHz; that read can only release mass.
- **Mass:** power non-pack 70.7 g, so A1's new 612 g allocation needs **+8.7 g → 621 g** (request below). That is the
  only F1 fail left, and it closes with A1's edit.
- **17.4 V LiHV:** the ESCs (2–6S, 25.2 V), the Kakute (10–36 V), the caps (35 V) and the servo BEC all pass. The
  LiHV pigtails and bullets raise the path resistance to 10.74 mΩ against 13.99 mΩ allowed.

## 4. L6: one cap per ESC (A3 R-F1)

**Model (`CALC-F1-ESC-CAP`).**
- The ESC draws a square wave between 0 and the winding current I_ph at duty D. So
  I_cap,rms = I_ph √(D(1−D)) ≤ 0.5 I_ph.
- All of the ripple is put in the added cans. The LANRC's own input caps ("—") and the 0.57 µH lead loop are not
  credited; this is the pessimistic end.
- F2's `CALC-F2-CAP-RIPPLE` uses 0.5 × the *battery* current (9.0 A at the limiter). That under-reads by I_ph / I_bat
  for the ripple at a given duty (request to F2).

**Inputs.**
- Limiter: I_ph = `CL-E1-ILIM-I-RBAND` 24.62 A and I_bat = 18.05 A, so D = 0.733 and I_cap = **10.89 A** per ESC.
- Hover, heavier motor: I_ph = 24.62 × 1.06 × 1.111 / 2.045 = 14.18 A (torque ∝ thrust, `CL-E1-ILIM-TW-FRESH`), so
  I_cap ≤ **7.09 A rms**, continuous.
- Datasheets:
  - Panasonic EEU-FR1E681: 1 790 mA at 100 kHz / 105 °C; impedance 28 mΩ max at 100 kHz / 20 °C; "Do not apply ripple
    currents exceeding the rated value". Sources:
    https://industrial.panasonic.com/sa/products/pt/aluminum-cap-lead/models/EEUFR1E681 and the series sheet
    https://sy-dep-epc-lpc.web.cern.ch/components/datasheets/epc-lpc%20(converters)/FR-Aluminium-Capacitor_Panasonic.pdf
    (both read 2026-11-14).
  - Rubycon 35PJV560M10X16.5: 11 mΩ, 4.0 A at 100 kHz, 125 °C, from the Digi-Key / LCSC listings, search 2026-11-14,
    https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/rubycon/35PJV560M10X16-5/15964390. `UNVERIFIED`: the maker PDF did not
    open.
- Derates:
  - ESR × 1.32 `EST` (10 °C, below 100 kHz);
  - ripple frequency factor 0.70 at 48 kHz, the pessimistic end. A typical hybrid table steps to ≈ 0.7 for
    10–100 kHz; the PJV's own table is "—".
- PWM 48 kHz: Bluejay offers 24 / 48 / 96 kHz (https://github.com/mathiasvr/bluejay, 2026-11-14).
- Thermal model: R_th 152 K/W (still air, half the side taped, no prop-wash credit), C_th 2.2 J/K, τ = 335 s, ambient
  44 °C (`env.ambient_ceiling_c`).
