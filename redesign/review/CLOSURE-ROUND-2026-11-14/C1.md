## 1. Failing claims: cause and fix

### 1.1 `CL-C1-N-STALL-VD`, the stall-line load factor at VD

**Basis.**
- VD = max(`requirements.vd_ms` 27.2, 1.25 × VH 21.26 → 26.6) = **27.2 m/s** (`CL-C1-VD-ENV`). This is FAR 23.335(b) practice (`ASM-C1-VD-FACTOR`).
- Vs = 11.90 m/s at the 2.6 kg gate, from B2's trimmed CLmax (`CL-C1-VS`).
- The old claim value was (VD/Vs)² = **5.22**, against `CL-C1-N-LIMIT-POS` **3.4** (`requirements.load_factor_limit_pos`, D-29, set by the gust peak `CL-C1-GUST-N-POS` 3.38).

**Is the limit physical or derived?**
- n_lim 3.4 is a **physical design limit**: the structure is sized to 3.4 × 1.5 at every speed up to VD. I left it unchanged.
- The old *claim quantity* was not a structural demand.
  - In FAR 23.333(b), the manoeuvre envelope is cut at n_lim above VA (21.94 m/s, `CL-C1-VA`). The stall line beyond VA lies outside the envelope by definition.
  - Any aircraft with VD > VA fails "(VD/Vs)² ≤ n_lim". It was a wrongly posed check.
- The real physical question is whether anything can command more than n_lim at VD.
  - With a raw-stick mode, yes.
  - K09-L has none: modes 17/18/19/5/7/11 (`subsystems/flight-software/K09L.param` lines 374–386, `CL-F4-NO-RAW-MODES`).
  - In FBWA, CRUISE and RTL, the elevon follows ArduPilot's angle → rate controller.

**Corrected model** (`CALC-C1-N-REACH`, new):
- Normal load n = V(q − α̇)/g + cosθ cosφ. With quasi-steady α and |cosθ cosφ| ≤ 1, a pull gives n ≤ 1 + V·k·q_up/g.
  - q_up is the pitch-rate demand cap `PTCH2SRV_RMAX_UP`.
  - k = 1.25 is the rate-loop overshoot (`ASM-C1-RATE-OVERSHOOT`, `EST`, UNVERIFIED).
- ArduPilot clamps the **total** pitch-rate demand, turn-coordination offset included, before the rate loop:
  - `desired_rate += rate_offset;` then the `rmax_pos` / `rmax_neg` clamp, when not inverted.
  - Source: `SRC-C1-AP-PITCHCTL`, `AP_PitchController.cpp` at tag Plane-4.7.1 (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ArduPilot/ardupilot/Plane-4.7.1/libraries/APM_Control/AP_PitchController.cpp, checked 2026-11-14).
- So n_reach = min(stall line, 1 + VD·k·q_up/g). The claim keeps its id and graph edges. The bare stall line moves to `CL-C1-N-STALL-LINE-VD` as information, with no limit.
- **Design change, owned by F4.** With today's cap of 100 °/s, the rate bound is 7.05, so the stall line 5.22 governs and the claim fails.
  - The allowable cap is (3.4 − 1)·g/(VD·k) = **39.66 °/s** (`CL-C1-Q-UP-MAX`).
  - I ask F4 for **35 °/s**, which gives **n 3.12**.
  - The claim still passes with overshoot up to k = 1.42.
- **Push side** (new claim `CL-C1-N-REACH-NEG-VD`, the same physics in the other direction):
  - Bound: n ≥ −VD·k·q_dn/g. The gravity term is taken as 0, which is conservative because the aircraft stays upright with `ROLL_LIMIT_DEG` 45.
  - The negative stall line at VD is −2.88 (CLmin 0.44, `ASM-C1-CLMIN`).
  - The allowable cap is **24.79 °/s** (`CL-C1-Q-DN-MAX`). I ask for **20 °/s**, which gives **−1.21** against −1.5.
- **Back-transition entered at VD** (new claim `CL-C1-N-REACH-VT`):
  - `Q_TAILSIT_RAT_VT` 30 °/s (`K09L.param` line 201, `ASM-C1-RAT-VT`) gives 1 + 27.2·1.25·0.524/9.81 = **2.82 ≤ 3.4**. It passes today.
- **Flight authority check:**
  - A 45° coordinated turn at `AIRSPEED_MIN` 17 m/s needs q = g sinφ tanφ / V = **23.4 °/s**, which is inside 35.
  - 35 °/s at 17 m/s still gives Δn 1.06 for pull-ups.
  - A push to the −10° pitch floor at 20 °/s gives Δn −0.6 at 17 m/s.
  - Transitions and hover use the VTOL controller (`Q_TAILSIT_RAT_FW` 12, `Q_A_RATE_P_MAX` 120), which these caps do not touch.
- **Confirmation test, not a gate:**
  - F4 TUNING-PLAN: an FBWA full-pull and full-push step at `AIRSPEED_MAX`. The logged peak pitch rate should be ≤ 1.25 × cap.
  - The design passes at the pessimistic k = 1.25 today.
- **Residual for F4 to confirm in SITL:** angle-triggered Q-assist (`Q_ASSIST_ANGLE`, ArduPilot default 30°, not set in `K09L.param`). The question is whether, at speed, it can command pitch through the VTOL rate limit (120 °/s) instead of the FW clamp. If it can, F4 sets `Q_ASSIST_ANGLE` 0 or shows that assist is speed-gated.

### 5.1 Re-basing the thrust limit on the E prop (corrected model)
- `CALC-C1-ROTOR` took its rpm cap from `propulsion.rpm_max`.
  - Under D-161 that key is now APC's structural limit for the E prop (13 360 rpm), not a speed the aircraft reaches.
  - Read as a cap, it gave T_cap = **37.15 N**, which is not physical.
- The rpm cap now reads **B4 `CL-B4-RPM-MAX-FULL` 10 287 rpm**. This is the highest rpm anywhere in the envelope at F4's output caps, on a fresh pack with a cold winding: the FW path at VD with rudder differential.
- T_cap is the static PER3 thrust at that rpm:
  - T_cap = k_T · T_h · (rpm / rpm_h)² = 1.039 × 6.841 × (10 287 / 5 844)² = **22.03 N** (`EST`).
  - Inputs: `CL-E1-KT`, `CL-B4-HOVER-T`, `CL-B4-HOVER-RPM`.
- This is an upper bound on every path:
  - **Q path:** the Lua spin cap holds the terminal voltage to ≤ 11.2 V (0.746 at boot or fault, 0.746 × 16.8 = 12.5 V). E1's 25 A point is 8 139 rpm / 1 417 gf (E1.md §5), well below 10 287 rpm.
  - **FW path:** the motor reaches 10 287 rpm only with axial inflow at VD, and inflow lowers thrust at a given rpm.
  - **Limiter:** the 73.2 A limiter is filtered, so it does not bound a step (CR-C1-12). Its steady value is `CL-C1-T-ILIM` 13.23 N.
- **B4's suggested 21.8 N (output 0.92) is not the right basis.**
  - The 0.92 static cap was superseded by the voltage-scheduled cap (F4 D-161c).
  - The governing rpm is the FW path, not the Q path.
- B4's `CALC-B4-RPMFULL` still runs at spin 0.84, THR 70, cant 9° and 2.6 kg. Each of those is at or above today's caps, so 10 287 rpm stays a bound. A B4 re-run at THR 60 can only lower it.
- T_cap stays ≤ 22.1 N for rpm ≤ 5 844 × √(22.1 / 7.108) = **10 305 rpm**.
