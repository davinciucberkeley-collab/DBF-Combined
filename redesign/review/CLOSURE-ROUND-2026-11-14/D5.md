## 1. Failing claims and new checks: cause and fix

### 1.1 `CL-D5-FLANGE-SF` 0.754 → 1.111 (design change)

**Cause.** The flange overhang (4.8 mm outboard of the tine) bends under C1's foot-to-fin ultimate load. The load is
`CL-C1-FOOT-F-ULT`, now 67.87 N, applied at the pad edge. With σ = 6 F a / (b t²), a 1.8 mm plate gives 31.2 MPa
against 23.5 MPa (`ASM-D5-PETG-SIGMA`, C6 table).

**Fix.** `landing.piston.flange_t_m` 1.8 → **2.2 mm**.
- SF = 0.754 × (2.2 / 1.8)² × (66.95 / 67.87) = **1.111**.
- I kept the overhang a. Moving the pad outboard on its own would have lengthened a (see 1.2).
- **Cost:**
  - +0.17 g per piston (`EST`).
  - The rim end moves 0.4 mm forward, because the stack runs from the ground.
  - `CL-D5-RIM-AFT-OF-TE` falls 0.6 → **0.2 mm**. It still passes ≥ 0, so the rim does not overlap the fin TE.

### 1.2 Tip-over: worst corner 30.58° (design change)

**Cause.** The 53 mm foot raises the CG above the ground.
- At D-164's forward limit x 0.043 and z 0.025: atan(0.193 / 0.330) = **30.32°**.
- C5's 30.05° used the old forward limit, 0.0394.

**Fix.** `landing.pad.z_outer_m` 0.219 → **0.221**.
- **The whole foot moves 2 mm outboard:** pad, flange, sleeve and fin step.
  - The flange overhang is unchanged, so 1.1 still holds.
  - Pivot z_eff = 0.221 − 0.001 = 0.220.
  - Corner: atan((0.220 − 0.025) / (0.32 + 0.053 − 0.043)) = **30.58°**, margin +0.58°.
  - Narrow axis at the target CG: 31.88°.
- **Effects:**

  | Item | Was | Now |
  |---|---|---|
  | Fin step depth | 9.0 mm | **7.0 mm** (`CL-D5-FIN-STEP-Z` 0.2010 → 0.2030), still ≤ 10 |
  | Box height slack | 77 mm | **73 mm** |
  | Props-off envelope height (`CL-D5-ENVELOPE-GROWTH`) | — | grows by **4 mm**, 0.438 → 0.442 m |

- **Rejected option:** extending only the pad and flange outboard. That needs +1.4 mm of overhang and a 2.5 mm or
  thicker flange, which moves the rim onto the fin TE.

### 1.3 `CL-D5-SIDE-TINE-SF` 1.018 → 1.212 (design change; C5 estimated 1.04)

**Cause.** The lever from the pad to the sleeve end grows 41.4 → 46.8 mm (+5 from the stroke, +0.4 from the flange).
With the inspection window 3 mm aft of the sleeve end, the windowed section sees F (a − 3 mm). At 36.75 N ultimate
that gives SF **1.018**.

**Fix.** New key `landing.piston.window_offset_m` = **10 mm**. The tine window now starts 10 mm aft of the rim. It was
a fixed 3 mm in `landing.py`.
- Windowed section: F (a − 10 mm), SF **1.212**.
- Full section at F a: SF 1.85.
- The window is 30 mm long and still shows the cartridge stripe.
- The 6 mm replace mark now sits on solid wall, 4 mm ahead of the window. It is scribed, not knifed.

### 1.4 Shoe sleeve end, FEA re-run on the drawn R9 shoe

The FEA geometry check (`_fea_matches`) needs records of the drawn shoe, because L_cart, engagement and stations
changed. I re-ran the two governing meshes:

| `sim/out/fea_d5_h300_pz.json` | 0.4084 | **0.3894** (14.31 MPa) |

- The lip reaction falls: R = F (a + e) / (e + s0) = 81.1 → **78.7 N**. The longer engagement more than offsets the
  longer lever.
- `CL-D5-SIDE-RIM-SF` 1.200 → **1.259**.
- `-SIDE-RIM-SF-FULL` (no limit) 0.835 → 0.876.
- `-SIDE-AXIAL-RF` 1.51 → **1.66**.
- I stopped the h400 / h300 (+y) re-runs, which are information only. Those records are still R8 and nothing reads
  them (`_fea` takes the finest file).
