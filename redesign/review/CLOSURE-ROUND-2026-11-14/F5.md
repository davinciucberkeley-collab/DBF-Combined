  and the 2 count checks.

    `throttle_in` = 1.285 θh: 0.6297 at θh 0.49, ratio 1.2850.
  - L06 delay **1.9 s** → CH6 to the **FBWA** slot.
  - Override released **1.2 s** later.
  - At the flight controller: **2.08–2.38 s** in QSTABILIZE at the entry throttle, and release **≥ 0.62 s** after
    FBWA (`EST`).
- **FBWA, CRUISE and RTL are reachable only through that sequence.**
  - No switch position selects a fixed-wing mode.
  - A random-history check of the logic finds 0 violations.
  - The FLTMODE order requested from F4 puts Q modes at 988, 1500 and 2012 µs.
  - This closes B3 §2 entry path 1 (pilot mode error) on the radio side.
- **New finding: ArduPilot reads the throttle stick in whole percent.** `control_in` is `int16_t` on range 100.
  - One step moves the ratio by 1.39 %, more than B3's whole window (1.17 %).
  - The design hits the target exactly at the set θh. `throttle_curve` tracks θh from 0.4766 to 0.5056 with no
    change.
  - A pre-flight table (stick step, SF1 value, `Q_THROTTLE_EXPO`) covers θh 0.40–0.60.
- **SITL (stage A, logic, stock tailsitter) runs the radio logic model as RC overrides. All four cases pass.**
  - Nominal: QSTABILIZE hold **2.18 s**, release **0.94 s** after FBWA. ThI / ThH **1.2849**, held constant to
    "Transition FW done".
  - Stick moved to 30 % and 100 % in the pitch-over: ThI unchanged.
  - Abort after 1 s: no FBWA.
  - Flip from QHOVER: nothing happens.
