## 3. Q-assist safety review (B4, C1)

### 3.1 What the firmware does (VTOL_Assist.cpp, tailsitter.cpp, tag Plane-4.7.2, read 2026-11-14)
- **Speed trigger:** airspeed < `Q_ASSIST_SPEED` (14). Kept.
- **Altitude trigger:** height above ground < `Q_ASSIST_ALT`, held for `Q_ASSIST_DELAY` 0.5 s. `alt <= 0` disables it.
- **Angle trigger:** attitude outside the FW limits + 5° (|roll| > 50°, pitch > 25° or < −15°) **and** error to
  the FW target > `Q_ASSIST_ANGLE` for 0.5 s. `angle <= 0` disables it. Both need `Q_ASSIST_SPEED` > 0.
- **What assist does on K09-L:** `Tailsitter::output()` runs `hold_stabilize()` and the multicopter mixer. With
  `Q_OPTIONS` bit 8 (Mtrs_Only_Qassist) the elevons **stay on the plane controllers** (the function returns before
  the copter outputs overwrite the surfaces), and the motors run the copter rate loop. The ArduPilot page says the
  same: bit 8 limits tailsitter assistance "to the VTOL motors only" (assisted_fixed_wing_flight.html, read
  2026-11-14).
- `check_VTOL_recovery()` is disabled for tailsitters (`!quadplane.tailsitter.enabled()`), so there is no other
  recovery path that these settings touch.

### 3.2 Answer to C1: can `Q_ASSIST_ANGLE` bypass the fixed-wing clamp at speed?
- **Elevons: no.** They stay on `AP_PitchController` with the `PTCH2SRV_RMAX_UP/DN` 35 / 20 °/s clamp.
- **Motors: yes.** In assist the motors follow the copter rate loop, whose pitch demand is capped by
  `Q_A_RATE_P_MAX` **120 °/s**, not 35. Copter pitch is the same body axis as FW pitch (upper vs lower pair).
- **Size of the bypass (`EST`).** C1's bound with q = 120 °/s at VD: n ≤ 1 + 27.2 × 1.25 × 2.094 / 9.81 = **8.3**.
  A motor moment acts like an elevon on a stable wing: Δn ≈ ΔM / (SM × c × W). A full upper-pair / lower-pair split of
  ≈ 2 × 9 N × 0.19 m ≈ 3.4 N m (`EST`, prop thrust at J ≈ 0.5 from B4's class data) at SM 0.065, c 0.30 m, W 25.5 N
  gives Δn ≈ **6.9**, independent of speed. So the motor path alone can exceed n 3.4 at VD. C1's claim holds only
  with the angle and altitude triggers off.
- **Speed trigger:** below 14 m/s the wing stalls first: n ≤ (14 / 11.90)² = **1.38** ≤ 3.4
  (`CL-F4-QASSIST-N`). It cannot reach the limit.

### 3.3 Does turning off the altitude and angle triggers remove protection K09-L needs? **No.**
| Trigger | What it would protect | Why K09-L does not need it |
|---|---|---|
| `Q_ASSIST_ALT` 15 | Lift near the ground in FW flight | On a tailsitter the assist motors are the cruise motors: assist adds **no lift**, only motor attitude control. ArduPilot describes the trigger "while the VTOL motors are not running"; on K09-L they always run. K09-L never flies FW below 50 m by plan (transitions and `Q_RTL_ALT` at 50 m) and never lands FW |
| `Q_ASSIST_ANGLE` 30 | Attitude recovery from an upset above 14 m/s | Above 14 m/s the elevons have more authority than at the stall, and they keep working (clamped). A stall or spin drops airspeed below 14 m/s, where the **speed trigger** still fires. The tailsitter's designed recovery is a pilot switch to a Q mode (back-transition, then hover); that is unchanged |

- **Residual:** a stall with the pitot reading high (blocked or iced) would not trigger speed assist. That case is
  already outside the speed trigger at today's settings and is not changed by these two lines. The pilot recovers by
  selecting QHOVER.
- **What turning them off removes:** the clamp bypass of §3.2 (load case at speed) and, before D-161, the rpm case
  B4 found (9 687 > 9 545 rpm at 18 m/s with the MR props). The second reason is gone; the first is enough.
- **Judgement: safe.** No alternative is needed. The settings stay 0 and are easy to change (§1).

### 3.4 Alternative if the team wants the angle / altitude assist back (B4, C1)
- Keep `Q_ASSIST_ANGLE` 30 and `Q_ASSIST_ALT` 15, and cap the copter pitch-rate demand: **`Q_A_RATE_P_MAX`
  120 → 35 °/s**. Both pitch paths would then sit at ≤ 35 °/s, and C1's bound gives n ≤ 3.12 at VD.
- Cost: copter pitch = upper / lower pair in hover. 35 °/s limits hover pitch agility (a 30° lean change takes
  ≥ 0.86 s) and gust recovery; B3 would have to show hover gust rejection and the pitch-over at 35 °/s. Not
  recommended without that work. **F4 recommends 0 / 0.**

## 5. Level speed, `AIRSPEED_MAX` and the VH basis (B4, coordinator)

- **Corrected model.** `CALC-F4-VLEVEL` used to scale B1's 25.65 m/s at the static 9 350 rpm cap by output. With the
  E props `CL-B4-SPIN-MAX` is 1.0 and `propulsion.rpm_max` 13 360, so that scaling gave nonsense (15.4 m/s at 60).
  Now:
  - FW output is **pass-through**, read from source (`tailsitter.cpp` `output()` → `output_motor_mask()`;
    actuator = throttle + roll_factor × rudder_dt × 0.5, PWM linear): `ASM-F4-FW-PASSTHRU` is `CHECKED`.
  - VH check: 0.60 / **0.6112** (B4 `CALC-B4-PROP-AB` `thr_for_vh__11X55E`, the output that reaches 21.8 m/s on B4's
    in-flight model; the reachable static rpm is 11 108) = **0.982 ≤ 1**. At 70: 1.145, 24.9 m/s → fails.
  - Sagged pack: B4's **18.25 m/s** at 3.6 V/cell and output 0.60 ≥ `AIRSPEED_MAX` 18 (+1.4 %).
- **Is the 0.25 m/s margin a safety issue?** No. `AIRSPEED_MAX` is only the TECS ceiling in FBWB / CRUISE / AUTO. If
  it is not reached on a low pack, TECS saturates at `THR_MAX` and trades height for speed within
  `TECS_SINK_MAX` 2.5 m/s. The safety bound is `AIRSPEED_MIN` 17, reachable with +7 %. `AIRSPEED_MAX` is an integer
  (AP_Int16): 17 would remove the TECS speed range, so 18 stays. H2 gate 5 flies the 18 m/s leg early in the pack.
- **Is a `THR_MAX` or `AIRSPEED_MAX` limit needed for VH / VD? Yes, `THR_MAX`.** `AIRSPEED_MAX` does not cap level
  speed in FBWA, where the pilot sets the throttle directly up to `THR_MAX`. **60 is kept**; 61 would leave 0.2 %.
- `CL-F4-FW-RPM-CAP` (the 7 950 rpm static proxy for VH) is now **information**: `CL-F4-VLEVEL-VH` checks VH directly
  and B4's `CL-B4-RPM-MAX-FULL` the in-flight rpm. Value 6 919 rpm static at 0.60 (`EST`). J6 to review.
- **Notch (corrected input).** `CL-F4-NOTCH-TOP` read `propulsion.rpm_max`, a prop limit, not a flown rpm. With
  13 360 it read 1.34. It now reads B4's in-flight envelope maximum `CL-B4-RPM-MAX-FULL` 10 287 rpm. Even so, 3P =
  514 Hz is above the 500 Hz Nyquist, so `INS_HNTCH_HMNCS` 7 → **3** (1P, 2P): 2 × 171.5 / 500 = **0.69**. 3P sits at
  ≥ 292 Hz at hover rpm, > 7 × the 40 Hz gyro low-pass (`EST`). J1 (CR-J1-12 asked for 1–3) to note.

## 6. D-161b: voltage-scheduled spin cap (E1, A1)

**Result: ArduPilot cannot do this on K09-L's flight controller.**
- **No Lua on the board.** `features.txt` of the official 4.7.2 build for CoreWingF405WingV2 lists
  `!AP_SCRIPTING_ENABLED` (l.359; also `!AP_SCRIPTING_BINDING_MOTORS_ENABLED`, `..._VEHICLE_ENABLED`; read
  2026-11-14). The board is an STM32F405 with 1 024 KB flash (`hwdef.dat`). ArduPilot's Lua page asks for "at least
  2 MB of flash and 80 kB of memory" and says scripting is not available on F4 autopilots (common-lua-scripts.html,
  read 2026-11-14). D-33 already recorded "no Lua". So items (1) and (3) (script, `SCR_ENABLE` / `SCR_HEAP_SIZE`,
  pre-arm check) cannot be built; no script was written.
- **No built-in equivalent.** Voltage compensation (`Q_M_BAT_VOLT_MAX/MIN`) scales the demand by 1/V, but
  `thrust_to_actuator()` maps a full thrust demand to exactly `Q_M_SPIN_MAX` at every voltage
  (`AP_Motors_Thrust_Linearization.cpp`: thrust 1 → ratio = v, × 1/v = 1). A fixed cap is the only ceiling.
- **Item (2), hover learning with a moving cap.** Not run: there is no way to move the cap on this board. Note for any
  future board: `Q_M_SPIN_MAX` is not a clip but the top of the scale (actuator = spin_min + (spin_max − spin_min) ×
  f(thrust)), so moving it re-maps every output, and the learnt hover throttle would drift with it.
- **What stays:** `Q_M_SPIN_MAX` 0.84. By E1's numbers this gives 31.2 A winding for one motor at the cap with the
  others at hover (> 25 A), and B4's `CL-B4-SPIN-LIMITER-LOW` (limiter binds at 0.895 on a low pack) fails against
  0.84. **That conflict is not an FSW problem and cannot be solved in FSW.**
- **Alternatives (numbers `EST`; owners decide):**

## D-161c: voltage-scheduled spin cap on the Kakute H743-Wing (option C, provisional)

### 1. The script (`subsystems/flight-software/scripts/k09l_spincap.lua`)
- **Law:** `Q_M_SPIN_MAX` = min(0.93, 11.2 V / V_bus), V_bus = `battery:voltage(0)` low-pass filtered (τ 0.5 s), every
  100 ms (10 Hz). It is set with `param:set` (RAM only, never saved). The saved value in `K09L.param` is **0.746**, so
  the cap is 0.746 at boot until the first good cycle and after any reboot.
- **Faults:** any error inside the update (battery unhealthy, V outside 9–18 V, a Lua error, the `SCR_USER1` = 99 test
  hook) is caught by `pcall`. The script then sets **0.746**, marks its aux pre-arm check failed ("SPINCAP fault") and
  sends one critical message. It recovers by itself when the input is good again. Faults in the first 5 s after boot
  are silent (the battery monitor is not healthy yet); the cap stays 0.746 then.
- **Bindings checked** against `libraries/AP_Scripting/docs/docs.lua`, tag Plane-4.7.2, read 2026-11-14:
  `param:set` ("will not be retained over a reboot"), `param:get`, `battery:voltage`, `battery:healthy`,
  `arming:get_aux_auth_id` / `set_aux_auth_passed` / `set_aux_auth_failed`, `gcs:send_text`, `logger:write`, `millis`.
  All of them ran in SITL (S23).
- **Log:** message `SPNC` (V, Vf, Cap, F) at 10 Hz.
- `fsw_check.py` lints the four script constants (0.93, 11.2, 0.746, 100 ms) against `params.json`
  (`fsw.spin_cap_*`), requires the saved `Q_M_SPIN_MAX` = the fault value and `SCR_ENABLE` 1.

### 5. Residual risks (for E1, A1, J6)
1. **Scripting-engine crash in flight** (out of memory or a panic, which `pcall` cannot catch): the cap freezes at its
   last value. If it froze on a sagged pack (up to 0.93) and the load then fell, the terminal voltage could exceed 11.2 V,
   so one saturated motor could exceed 25 A (`EST`, E1 to size). The next arming is refused. The likelihood is low:
   memory is flat at 23.3 kB in a 100 kB heap. Flight card: "SPINCAP fault or a scripting message → land".
2. **The fault value 0.746 is a compromise.**
   - On a fresh pack with one motor saturated and the others at hover, the bus is above 15.05 V, so 0.746 can pass
     25 A (E1: 25 A at output 0.720).
   - On a low pack it costs thrust: 0.746 × 12.06 V = 9.0 V against 11.2, so T/W ≈ 1.75 × (9.0 / 11.2)² ≈ **1.13**
     (`EST`, square law).
   - The winding-safe value at any state is 11.2 / 16.8 = 0.667, but on a low pack that leaves T/W ≈ 0.89, which
     cannot hover.
   - A1's 0.746 is kept as asked. E1 to confirm, or choose "hold the last good cap" (better T/W, worse winding).
- **One IMU** on the Kakute: EKF lane redundancy is lost (F3). The flight card's EKF failsafe (QLAND) stays.
- `BATT_VOLT_MULT` sets the cap directly: a 1 % voltage error is a 1 % cap error. T-F1-SENSOR-CAL must cover voltage.
- B4's `CL-B4-SPIN-LIMITER-LOW` reads `fsw.spin_max`, which is now the saved 0.746. B4 should compare the limiter's
  binding output with the scheduled cap at its pack state, min(`fsw.spin_cap_max` 0.93, `fsw.spin_cap_vterm_v` 11.2 /
  V_bus). At E1's 3.60 V/cell state that is 0.931 ≥ 0.895.

**SITL verdict: option C works in ArduPilot. F4 does not need to fall back to A or B.** A and B stay valid if the
team prefers a fix without software (E1's §6 numbers).

## Flight-control group, 2026-11-14 (late): flying without the airspeed sensor (COST-TRADE 2.8, lever A1) and pending items

### 1. Verdict: NOT safe. The Matek ASPD-4525 stays ($80.16 comes back; COST-TRADE then reads $58.15 over, A1 must return another lever)
Basis (ArduPilot tag `Plane-4.7.2`, read 2026-11-14, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ArduPilot/ardupilot/Plane-4.7.2/ ...):
- **What the synthetic airspeed is.** `AP_AHRS.cpp _airspeed_EAS()`: with no sensor it is |nav velocity - EKF3 wind| / EAS2TAS (DCM fallback otherwise). `AHRS_WIND_MAX` 0 puts no bound on it. It is only as good as the wind state, and K09-L gives EKF3 no airspeed, sideslip or drag measurement, so wind is observable only by turning (heading change against ground track). In the hover and at the start of the pitch-over the wind state is unobserved, so the error is bounded only by the wind itself: up to **+-5 m/s** (H3 limit 5 m/s steady). EST: 1-3 m/s after several turns, not provable on this airframe before flight.
- **Margin it must protect.** B2: tripped forward-CG stall 12.257 m/s against `stall_max` 12.3 (+0.043); 1.3 Vs = 15.9-16.0 against `AIRSPEED_MIN` 17, so the whole allowance is about **1.1 m/s** (CL-F4-ARSPD-MIN-TRIP margin 7 %). A 5 m/s over-read puts true speed at 12 m/s, which is the stall; a 1.1 m/s over-read already uses the margin.
- **Transition gate and Q-assist.** `VTOL_Assist.cpp should_assist()`: `Q_ASSIST_SPEED` does work on the synthetic estimate (only the `Q_OPTIONS` bit DISABLE_SYNTHETIC_AIRSPEED_ASSIST blocks it), so it is not disabled, but it triggers on the same biased number: 14 m/s estimated can be 9 m/s true. The assist adds no lift on a tailsitter (section 3), so it cannot rescue a stall anyway. Transition-complete is a pitch angle test (`Q_TAILSIT_ANGLE` 70), not a speed test, so the "1.3 Vs reached" gate (B3 band, B2 +0.5 m/s allowance) would be unverifiable.
- **FW control.** TECS, speed scaling (`SCALING_SPEED` 17 / V: at 12 m/s true with 17 estimated the elevon gain is 30 % low) and `AIRSPEED_STALL` bank scaling all read the estimate.
- **Tests.** T-B2-STALL (sets `AIRSPEED_MIN`, `Q_ASSIST_SPEED`) and the gate 5 FW tune need a measured airspeed; a ground-speed stall test works only up/down-wind averaged in calm air.
- **Wind and fence.** The 5 m/s limit is 29 % of 17 m/s, the same size as the error; ground speed on the 300 m legs swings 12-22 m/s. The 250 m fence and 110 m ceiling are geometric and unaffected.

**Parameters: no change** (`ARSPD_TYPE` 1, `ARSPD_USE` 1 stay). **Claims:** CL-B4-PITOT*, CL-D2-PITOT-*, CL-F3-ASPD-*, CL-F4-ARSPD-* stay; COST-TRADE A04 / lever A1 revert; no claim retires. Cheaper sensor instead: COST-TRADE 2.8, $49.99 listing, about $14.3 EST.
