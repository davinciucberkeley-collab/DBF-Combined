### 1.2 `CL-C2-FEA-GCI`: corrected measure, re-run on the current loads

- **Cause.** The governing input was `foam_s1_L4_O_gci` = 0.0778 against 0.05: the 99.5th percentile of foam tension
  in the global model, case L4. Two things were wrong:

     from it), and the number of fine rings grows with the level (`nr` 2, 3, 4). So the 0.5 % of elements that the
     percentile keeps is a different part of the wing at each level. It is not one functional of the stress field, and
     its GCI does not measure discretisation error.
  2. The stored runs dated from 2026-11-10, on the C1 loads of that day.
- **Corrected model.** `wing_fea.py` now also records the 99.5th percentile **by foam volume** (`s1_p995v_pa`,
  `tau_p995v_pa`): the stress exceeded in 0.5 % of the foam volume, a mesh-independent quantity. `fea_report.py` uses
  it for the two foam GCI inputs and for `CL-C2-FEA-FOAM-S1` / `-TAU`. The element-count values stay in the result
  files.
- **Re-run.** `wing_fea_direct.py`, set O, all four cases, levels 1, 2 and 3, on the current loads.
- **Result.**

### 1.3 `CL-C2-FEA-CUTOUT-SENS` and `CL-C2-FEA-FOAM-FILLET-SENS`: the fillet is a real path, so the design changes

The 100 % rows are not a reachable state, so they are re-classed (reason in step 7). **But the fillet is a real load
path:** the drawn half-fin root and its coves are bonded to the wing along the whole chord. So I checked the real path,
found that it fails, and changed the design.

1. **What the fillet really carries.** The fin is fixed to the tube and to the PETG through-core block, which are far
   stiffer than a microballoon cove on foam. The fillet carries what compatibility gives it. C4 computed that split
   (the CR-C2-12 answer: `team/C4/claims.d3.json`, `CALC-C4-D3-STATIC` output `fillet_share_table`): the reactions of
   the fin root, pinned (rigid), split at the hinge, per quarter, for L4 and G2, both senses, at limit. A rigid root
   attracts the most load, so for a foam wing the split is an **upper bound** (`ASM-C2-FILLET-SPLIT`).
2. **New load case `c4split`** (`wing_fea.py`). Each of C4's segment resultants (force and moment, per quarter) goes on
   the station-strip skin of its own surface and on its own side of the break; the block takes the rest of the station
   load, so the strip stays in equilibrium. C4's lower quarter is mirrored z → −z. C4's frame against C2's (load sense,
   sign of y) is not pinned down, so all four combinations are run and the worst is taken.
3. **The drawn design fails on the real path.** L4, cut-out corners (1 mm average, local level 1), ultimate:
   **255 kPa tension vs 150, 438 compression vs 120, 218 shear vs 100** (C4 sense +, y kept). The aft segment loads
   the tab between the elevons, and the tab hands the load to the R 3 mm cut-out corners.
4. **Options tried** (local submodel, L4).
   - *Release aft of the hinge only* (bonded root ends at x 0.2496): corner compression **118.7 vs 120** at level 2,
     up 10 % from level 1, so it fails on refinement. A bonded root that ends at the hinge line ends right at the
     corners.
   - *Bonded ahead of the hinge carrying 100 % of the station load:* corners 353 / 398 / 190 kPa at level 1. No change
     of this size meets the 100 % bound, and it is not a reachable state anyway.
   - **Chosen: release from x 0.22 to the TE**, 30 mm (10 R) ahead of the cut-out corners. C4's share aft of x 0.22 is
     moved, with its moment, onto the bonded root ahead of x 0.22. That puts more load on the bonded part than sending
     it back to the block would, so it is conservative.
5. **Design change** (`ASM-C2-FILLET-BREAK`; D3 builds it, D1 notes it in the wing build).
   - Under each of the four half-fin roots, from x 0.22 to the TE (100 mm), lay two overlapping 25.4 mm strips of PTFE
     film tape on the wing skin before the root bond: about 45 mm wide, centred on the fin plane.
   - The root edge, its 13 mm coves and the fin's 15 mm skin-tape laps sit on the PTFE and bond to the fin only. Epoxy
     and packing-tape adhesive do not bond to PTFE.
   - The coves are laid as drawn, so the aft fillet mass that holds the forward CG limit (D-92) stays.
   - **Tape:** Uline S-14538 PTFE film tape, 1 in × 36 yd, 3.5 mil, silicone adhesive, **$60 per roll**
     (https://www.uline.com/Product/Detail/S-14538/Specialty-Tape/Uline-PTFE-Film-Tape-1-x-36-yds, checked
     2026-11-14). One roll; 0.8 m used. Shipping and tax "—".
   - **Mass: +4.9 g** for the four roots (`EST`: 8 strips × 100 × 25.4 mm × 0.24 kg/m²; 3.5 mil PTFE at about
     2.2 g/cm³ plus the adhesive). It sits at x 0.22–0.32, aft of the CG.
6. **Result with the break at x 0.22**, worst of the four combinations, ultimate:

   | Check | Value | Limit | Margin | Where / combination |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | Cut-out corners, tension (1 mm avg, local L2) | 12.8 kPa | 150 | +1070 % | G2, C4 sense + |
   | Cut-out corners, compression | 77.6 kPa | 120 | +55 % | L4, sense −, y kept |
   | Cut-out corners, shear | 36.4 kPa | 100 | +175 % | L4, sense −, y kept |
   | Block-pocket corners, tension, vm(L/2), tape 50 %, L2 | 107.4 kPa | 150 | +40 % | L4, sense +, y kept |
   | Block-pocket corners, compression | 104.5 kPa | 120 | +15 % | L4, sense −, y mirrored |
   | Block-pocket corners, shear | 62.9 kPa | 100 | +59 % | L4, sense −, y mirrored |
   | Foam tension, 99.5th pct by volume, global L2 | 52.9 kPa | 150 | +184 % | L4, sense −, y mirrored |
   | Foam shear, 99.5th pct by volume, global L2 | 40.8 kPa | 100 | +145 % | L4, sense −, y mirrored |
   | Bead (microballoon) max principal | 5.25 MPa | 10 (`ASM-C2-BEAD`) | +90 % | L4 |

   - The block-pocket corners are on the decided tape model (`c2r5_corner.py` band, D-153) with the split. On the
     block path alone they were 101.9 / 74.6 / 52.0 kPa, so the fillet split costs most in compression (+40 %) and
     still passes. They ran for the two combinations that govern the untaped pocket (sense − y mirrored; sense + y
     kept).
   - The cut-out values are at local level 2 (h 1.1 mm), the level `CL-C2-FEA-CUTOUT-TAU` uses. On the hinge-break
     variant the corner value grew 10 % from level 1 to 2; the margins here are 55 % and more.
7. **Re-class of the two old rows.** `CL-C2-FEA-CUTOUT-SENS` (867 kPa) and `CL-C2-FEA-FOAM-FILLET-SENS` (293 kPa) are
   now records with no limit, titled "not a reachable state". Reason: 100 % of the station loads through the fillet
   needs the block path gone, i.e. a broken block. That is a failure state, covered by `CL-C2-FEA-PETG` and
   T-C4-BLOCK-PROOF. The real path is checked by the new rows, which verify `R-STRUCT-MARGIN`:
   `CL-C2-FEA-SPLIT-S1` / `-TAU`, `CL-C2-FEA-CUTOUT-SPLIT-S1` / `-S3` / `-TAU`,
   `CL-C2-FEA-FOAM-CORNER-S1-SPLIT` / `-S3-SPLIT` / `-TAU-SPLIT`.
