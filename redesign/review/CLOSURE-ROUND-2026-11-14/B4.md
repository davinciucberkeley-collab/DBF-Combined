## 2. `CL-B4-RPM-MAX-FULL`: rpm limit over the flight envelope

### 2.1 Cause
The claim was the static rpm at output 1.0 on a 16.8 V pack: 10 637 rpm against APC's MR limit. APC's page (read
2026-11-14, https://www.apcprop.com/technical-information/rpm-limits) gives "Maximum RPM=105,000/prop diameter (inches)"
for multi-rotor props, which is 9 545 rpm at 11 in. The page states no airspeed condition, so the limit applies in
flight as well as on the stand.

Output 1.0 is never commanded in flight. I checked the ArduPilot source (master, read 2026-11-14,
`ArduPlane/tailsitter.cpp`, `libraries/AP_Motors/AP_MotorsMulticopter.cpp`):
- **Q path** (Q modes, the VTOL→FW pitch-over, Q-assist): the multicopter mixer, capped at `Q_M_SPIN_MAX` 0.84.
  Nothing clamps `Q_M_SPIN_MAX`: "0.9–1.0" is parameter metadata only, and there is no arming check. That settles
  F4's CR-F4-06 concern.
- **FW path** (fixed-wing flight and the FW→VTOL transition): `output_motor_mask()`, where actuator = throttle +
  roll_factor × `rudder_dt` × 0.5, PWM is linear in the actuator, and there is no spin max or linearisation. The
  highest output is `THR_MAX`/100 + 0.25 × `RUDD_DT_GAIN`/100 = 0.70 + 0.0875 = **0.7875**. This also settles
  `ASM-F4-FW-PASSTHRU`: it is pass-through.

The old claim missed one physical effect: **in flight the prop unloads, so rpm at a fixed output rises with
airspeed**. At 0.7875 output and VD 27.2 m/s the FW path reaches **10 134 rpm** (`EST`, B4 motor model with APC PER3
data), so the case is real and needs a fix.

### 2.2 Corrected model (`CALC-B4-RPMFULL`, new outputs)
Both paths use the same pessimistic inputs: a fresh pack (16.8 V OCV, no sag, one motor), a cold winding (lowest R),
and E1's Kv 900. That Kv comes from a fit to the maker's data (`ASM-E1-KV`).
- **Q path:** `Q_M_SPIN_MAX` at an axial inflow V_Q = **14.1 m/s** (`ASM-B4-QPATH`). The forward pitch-over ends at
  Q_TAILSIT_ANGLE 70° at 14.1 m/s (D-149, B3), and Q-assist runs only below `Q_ASSIST_SPEED` 14. Using the airspeed as
  axial inflow is conservative.
- **FW path:** `THR_MAX` + rudder differential at **VD 27.2 m/s** (`requirements.vd_ms`), the top of the flight
  envelope.

| Path / case | Output | Airspeed | rpm | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Static, output 1.0 (`CL-B4-RPM-UNLIMITED`, information) | 1.0 | 0 | 10 637 | not a flight state |
| Q, `Q_M_SPIN_MAX` 0.84 (`CL-B4-RPM-Q`) | 0.84 | 14.1 | **9 449** | ≤ 9 545 PASS |
| Q, if Q-assist could run at AIRSPEED_MAX (`CL-B4-RPM-Q-ARSPMAX`, information) | 0.84 | 18 | 9 687 | would fail |
| FW, `THR_MAX` 70 today (`CL-B4-RPM-FW`) | 0.7875 | 27.2 | 10 134 | FAIL |
| **FW, `THR_MAX` 60 (requested)** | 0.6875 | 27.2 | **9 326** | PASS |
| FW, `THR_VT` 55 back-transition | 0.6375 | ≤ 27.2 | ≤ 8 905 | PASS |

Caps (`EST`): `CL-B4-Q-CAP-VQ` is **0.852** (largest `Q_M_SPIN_MAX` for 9 545 rpm at 14.1 m/s). `CL-B4-FW-CAP-VD` is
**0.714** (largest FW per-motor output for 9 545 rpm at VD).

### 2.3 Why `Q_M_SPIN_MAX` stays 0.84 and Q-assist changes instead
Lowering `Q_M_SPIN_MAX` would hurt hover T/W with sag. On a 3.6 V/cell pack with a hot winding, the 70.2 A limiter
binds at output **0.835** (`CL-B4-SPIN-LIMITER-LOW`, new claim, limit ≤ `P:fsw.spin_max`). At 0.84 the limiter still
governs, so B1's `CL-B1-HOVER-TW-SAG` 1.647 and E1's fresh T/W are unchanged. On a fresh pack, 0.84 draws 88.9 A, well
past the limiter.

If Q-assist stays able to run at 18 m/s, the cap would need to be ≤ 0.823. The cap must also stay ≥ 0.835 to protect
the sag T/W. Both cannot hold, so the Q-path airspeed bound is the variable to change. `Q_ASSIST_ALT` 15 and
`Q_ASSIST_ANGLE` (default 30) are the two triggers that run the multicopter mixer at any airspeed. Setting both to 0
leaves the speed trigger (< 14 m/s). F4 owns that safety trade, CR-B4-31.

Margin is +1.0 % on the Q path, +2.3 % on the FW path. Stand pass line for `T-F4-STAND-RPM-CAP`: the static rpm at 0.84
on a fresh pack must be ≤ 9 271 × 9 545 / 9 449 = **9 365**. Above that, Kv is higher than E1's fit and F4 lowers
`Q_M_SPIN_MAX` along `CL-B4-Q-CAP-VQ`. The test confirms the design; the claim does not depend on it.

## 3. `CL-B4-ELEVON-HOVER-ALT`: both spin-map senses keep the elevons dry

### 3.1 Cause and corrected model
At 9° cant the mirrored map leans each hover wake toward the wing. The R4 claim was a 2-D z-gap that put elevon
material directly under the wake centre-line. The wake centre crosses the TE at y 0.344 (mirrored) or y 0.296 (design),
next to the fin root at y 0.32. The panels actually stop at y 0.30 and 0.34, which leaves a 40 mm gap there. The new
`CALC-B4-WAKE` outputs compute a **3-D clearance**:
- the perpendicular distance from every point of each deflected panel (±25°, real span) to the leaning wake axis,
- minus the wake radius plus the mixing layer (`ASM-B4-MIX` 0.08), the same wake model as before,
- for both props of a side.

The 3-D model alone does not pass: the clearance without chamfers is **−9.5 mm** (`CL-B4-ELEVON-HOVER-ALT-NOCH`).
The deflected TE corners next to the fin root sit inside the leaning wake.

### 3.2 Options checked (mirrored sense, worst prop)
| Option | Mirrored clearance | Cost | Taken |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move the outboard panel's inboard end 0.34 → 0.41 and the inboard panel's outboard end 0.30 → 0.28 (straight) | +6.6 / +7.4 mm | Area 17.2 → 13.8 % of S, below B3's 15 % floor | no |
| Cant back to ≤ 7.2° (W2 break-even) | 0 | Fails B3 `CL-B3-HOVER-YAW-WTIP-20` (needs 8.73°) | no |
| Radial tilt component of ~4° added to the cant (`EST`, hand estimate) | ~0 | cos loss −0.24 % breaks E1's T/W 2.001 window | no |
| Raise `stations.z_m` about 10 mm (`EST`, wake edge moves about 1:1 with z_m) | ~+1 mm | Fin, box, loads and every clearance move | no |
| Elevon travel ±25 → ±17° | 0 | B3 trim use 0.42 of travel becomes about 0.62, above the 0.5 limit | no |
| **TE-corner chamfers at the fin root: inboard panel 20 mm, outboard panel 70 mm set-back at the TE, zero at the hinge line** | **+7.4 / +6.6 mm** | Area kept 0.900 (17.2 → **15.5 %** of S) | **yes** |

Chamfer sweep, outboard panel, set-back at the TE (mm) → clearance (mm): 0 → −9.5, 40 → −4.7, 60 → +2.2, **70 → +6.6**,
80 → +11.5. Inboard panel: 0 → −1.1, 10 → +2.9, **20 → +7.4**.

### 3.3 Geometry change (for D1, A1; copy D6, B2, B3, A5)
Cut each elevon panel's trailing corner next to the fin root on a straight line. The cut runs from the hinge-line end,
which stays where it is (y 0.30 inboard panel, y 0.34 outboard panel), to the TE at:
- **y 0.28** on the inboard panel (20 mm set-back),
- **y 0.41** on the outboard panel (70 mm set-back),

on both sides of the aircraft. The hinge line, horns, link slot through the lower fin half and the servo are unchanged.
New params key `wing.elevon.te_chamfer_m` = [0.02, 0.07]. B4 reads it from `ASM-B4-ELEVON-CHAMFER` until A1 adds the
key.

Effect on other B4 claims:

  (`CL-B4-ELEVON-HOVER-2D`).
- `CL-B4-ELEVON-CRUISE`, `-WING-GAP`/`-ALT`, `-ESC-WASH`, the pitot, yaw, download, gap and tip claims do not read
  the panel planform. They are unchanged.
