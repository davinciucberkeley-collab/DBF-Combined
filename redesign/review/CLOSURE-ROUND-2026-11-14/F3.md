# F3 avionics hardware: closure round 2026-11-14, option C (H7 flight controller with Lua)

Role: F3 (avionics hardware, `redesign/TEAM.md`). Question from A1: E1's voltage-scheduled spin cap
`Q_M_SPIN_MAX` = min(0.93, 11.2 V / V_bus) (`E1.md` "D-161 spin cap" §5) needs Lua. F4 found that the CoreWing F405
Wing V2 has no Lua (`F4.md` §6, CR-F4-42). A1 is leaning towards option C, an H7 wing flight controller that runs
Lua. This report checks the candidate boards and recommends one.

## Verdict

- **Option C can be built.** The board is the **Holybro Kakute H743-Wing** (target `KakuteH7-Wing`). It is the only
  candidate that meets all four of these: still made, in US stock today, bidirectional DShot and Lua in the standard
  Plane 4.7.2 build, and a 6 V servo BEC rated at or above the 5.6 A need.
- **Cost: +$39.15** with tax against line A01 ($115.76 vs $76.61; Holybro direct at $105.00, stock and shipping
  —). It is **+$79.33** if it is bought from the one US stock confirmed today (ReadyMadeRC, $141.44). The budget is
  already $389.38 over (`I1.md`), so option C makes it worse. I1's request to swap to the SpeedyBee F405 Wing (−$23.70)
  cannot be combined with option C (see §1).
- **Mass: −9.1 g `EST`** against the masses booked today. The boards alone are −12 g: CoreWing FC 12 g + power board
  28 g `EST` out, Kakute 28 g in. If the CoreWing power board really weighs its 11.4 g listing, the change is **+7.5 g**
  instead. A3 should book −9.1 g and carry the +7.5 g as the pessimistic end.
- **CG: +0.18 mm aft, −0.30 mm lateral (to the left)** `EST`. The predicted CG of 0.0496 m stays inside the 0.0393–0.0511
  m range.
- **Fit:** the board is 45 × 30 × 13.5 mm and goes in the power-board seat on the deck in the left channel. The seat
  `KO-PWR-BOARD` is 56 × 34 mm. The top of the board is at z 0.0571 m, 5.5 mm under the keep-out top of 0.0626 m. The
  right-channel FC tray (52 × 32 mm) is no longer needed.
- **What the board costs in hardware:**
  - The servo BEC margin drops from 43 % to 7 % (6 A against 5.6 A).
  - There is one IMU instead of two.
  - Pack current now flows through the flight-controller board, so the gel mount must be resized (§3).
- **F3 does not rank C against A or B.** F4 recommends A (motor with a ≥ 33 A winding rating) or B (ESC that limits
  current), because both keep the fix out of software. A1 decides. If C is chosen, the board below is the one to buy.

### 2.1 Mounting: the board takes the power board's place (not the FC tray)

The Kakute's current sense, servo BEC and battery pads are on the same single board as the IMU. The CoreWing could be
split so that only the 12 g FC sat on gel cubes. That split is not possible here. The pack current has to pass through
the board, so the board goes where the power board was: on the deck of `PWR-006` in the left channel. This is the
"case U, whole stack on the deck" layout that D-74 already provided for. The current path stays as it is: AS150U → board
battery pads → ESC pads → bus board `PWR-004`.

## 3. Vibration isolation with the heavier board (corrected model, `EST`)

F3's `CALC-F3-VIBE` model (`team/F3/avionics.py`): k = n E a² / h, ζ = 0.10, E = 30 kPa.
Hover 1P is taken from today's `CL-B4-HOVER-RPM`, 5 844 rpm (97.4 Hz). The claim still runs on 5 429 rpm, which is
drift; it is re-run when the params change.

| Mount | Board mass | Cubes | f_n Hz | T at 1P (limit ≤ 0.3) | f_n lateral (limit ≥ 15) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Today: CoreWing FC on gel | 12 g | 4 × 5 mm | 35.6 | 0.175 | 20.5 |
| Kakute, cubes resized for the same f_n | 28 g | 4 × **7.6 mm** side, 5 mm high | 35.4 | 0.173 | 20.4 |
| + battery and ESC leads, 230 N/m `EST` | 28 g | same | 38.2 | 0.204 | 22.1 |
| + leads, 460 N/m `EST` | 28 g | same | 40.9 | 0.235 | 23.6 |
| Lead stiffness that reaches the limit | 28 g | same | 45.5 | 0.30 at ≈ 900 N/m | — |

- **Lead stiffness `EST`:** fine-strand 10 AWG silicone wire, about 1 050 × 0.08 mm strands, so EI ≈ 2.5e-4 N m² (copper
  only). Strand friction and the jacket are allowed for as ×5 to ×10. Each lead is treated as a 40 mm cantilever loop
  (3EI/L³). Four leads come to 230–460 N/m.
- **Design rule:** every lead soldered to the board (battery +/−, ESC +/−) leaves through an S-loop of at least 40 mm
  of fine-strand silicone wire. Each lead is tied to the deck, not to the board.
- **Test:** `T-F3-VIBE` (tethered hover, VIBE < 30 m/s²) stays as confirmation. The design passes at the stiff end
  (460 N/m) today.
