- **`FENCE_MARGIN` 10 (`K09L.param` l.408) does not lower the altitude breach.** Source:
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ArduPilot/ardupilot/ArduPilot-4.7/libraries/AC_Fence/AC_Fence.cpp, read
  2026-11-14 (no exact 4.7.2 tag was found).
  - `check_fence_alt_max` breaches on `_curr_alt_u_m >= _alt_max_m`.
  - The margin sets only `_safe_relhome_alt_max_m = _alt_max_m - _margin_m`, which drives `record_margin_breach`. That
    is a notification, raised only with `FENCE_OPTIONS` bit 2.
  - Pre-arm needs `FENCE_ALT_MAX − alt_min > 2 × FENCE_MARGIN`: 110 > 20 passes.
- **Margin to the 110 m breach.** The top of the transition is 99.5 m from a 50 m start and 104.5 m from 55 m, which
  leaves 10.5 m and 5.5 m. The 400 ft ceiling (122 m, `../H3/FLIGHT-CARD.md` l.20) is 22.4 m and 17.4 m away.
- **Recommendation.** Aim for 50 m and treat 55 m as the most. The baro error is "—" until the twin log, so the 5.5 m
  at the 55 m edge is honest but small.
- F4 should confirm the breach behaviour in SITL (S9). Raising `FENCE_ALT_MAX` toward 115 m would stay under 122 m, but
  that is F4's call.
