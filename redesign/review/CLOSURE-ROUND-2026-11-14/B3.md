## 1. R-TRANSITION: the entry condition

### 1.1 Cause

D-149 entered the transition at "≥ 1.4 W". That fails in two ways at the pessimistic pack state.

- **The upper end has no room.** A2 §2.3 computes the sagged T/W at the gate mass as 4 × 1.309 × 0.867 / 2.6 = 1.7456.
  1.4 W is 0.802 of that, against the 0.80 control reserve (`ASM-B1-CTRL-RESERVE`).
- **The lower end is close behind.** From a steady hover, the time-domain pitch-over needs 1.306 W for a trim use of
  0.60. It needs 1.328 W to also hold the 2 m/s gust reserve (≤ 1.0) and the handover (≥ 12.94 m/s).
  - `team/B3/results/closure1114.json`, `window.rate12_w00`.
  - So the window from a steady hover is **1.328–1.397 W**, ±2.5 %.
- **D-149 named no realisation tolerance.**
  - The throttle maps to thrust through the learnt hover throttle θh: static T/W = dl_true × throttle_in / θh.
  - dl_true lies between 1.025 (B4's SU2 download, `params.json` note on `propulsion.download_factor`) and the key 1.06.
  - The thrust-curve fit adds ±2 % `EST`.
  - A stick position adds ≈ ±0.04 W per ±2 % of stick (throttle_curve slope 0.86 at θh 0.49).
  - Together these do not fit in ±2.5 %.

### 1.2 Firmware law: read from source, no longer a SITL condition

ArduPilot 4.7.2 source, read 2026-11-14. The links are stored as `SRC-B3-AP-TAILSIT`, `SRC-B3-AP-ATCMULTI` and
`SRC-B3-AP-QSTAB`.

- **Throttle hold.** `Tailsitter_Transition::update`, state `ANGLE_WAIT_FW`, calls
  `set_throttle_out(MAX(get_throttle_hover(), get_throttle_in()), true, 0)`.
  `AC_AttitudeControl_Multi::set_throttle_out` starts with `_throttle_in = throttle_in;`, so the value is written back
  every loop. The entry value is therefore held to `Q_TAILSIT_ANGLE`, and the stick is ignored.
- **Entry state.** `VTOL_update` calls `restart()` every VTOL loop. `ANGLE_WAIT_FW` therefore begins at the mode change,
  from the last QSTABILIZE `throttle_in`, which is `hold_stabilize(get_pilot_throttle())`.
- **No other writer.** In `quadplane.cpp` and `tailsitter.cpp` the only other writers of `_throttle_in` are
  `hold_stabilize` (Q modes) and the FW→VTOL block (back-transition only).
- **Angle boost.** The call passes `apply_angle_boost = true`. The tailsitter defaults table sets
  `Q_A_ANGLE_BOOST` 0, so no 1/cos(tilt) boost applies. If boost were on, the throttle would rise to saturation in the
  pitch-over and the 0.80 fraction would fail. F4 should state it explicitly.
- **`Q_TRANSITION_MS`.** It only defaults an unconfigured `Q_TAILSIT_RAT_FW` (ANGLE / (MS / 2000)). The tailsitter
  default of 2000 would give 70 °/s.
- **Hover learning.** `update_throttle_hover` returns while the Z controller is off, so θh does not move in QSTABILIZE.

### 1.3 Design change: entry ratio and pre-climb

`team/B3/closure1114.py` sweeps entry thrust, pitch rate, `Q_TAILSIT_ANGLE`, CG, prop slope, wn and mass. In each
case it reports the lowest T0 at which use ≤ 0.60, gust ≤ 1.0 and handover ≥ 12.94 all pass.

| Pre-climb vertical speed at the mode change | Low end of window (W) | High end (0.80 × 1.7456) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (steady hover) | 1.3278 | 1.3968 |
| 3 m/s | 1.2949 | 1.3968 |
| **4 m/s** | **1.2827** | 1.3968 |
| 5 m/s | 1.2775 | 1.3968 |

- **Why a pre-climb helps.** Climbing at the mode change lowers the angle of attack early in the pitch-over, where the
  post-stall nose-down wing moment peaks.
- **Levers tried and rejected.**
  - `Q_TAILSIT_RAT_FW` 8–16 °/s moves the use threshold by ≤ 0.006 W.
  - `Q_TAILSIT_ANGLE` 75 adds 1 m/s at the handover. But the longer VTOL phase pushes `GUST2` to 1.004–1.031 at the
    high-thrust edge.
  - A fixed `Q_M_THST_HOVER` floor would make the entry independent of the pilot. It would also bias the hover
    altitude feed-forward and the throttle gain scaling (`Q_TAILSIT_GSCMSK` 1) by about 1.29×. It is not needed.

**Design of record** (`ASM-B3-TRANS-T0` [1.285, 2.0 s], `ASM-B3-TRANS-REALISE` [1.025, 2 %, 0.8]):

| Item | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| `throttle_in` at entry | **1.285 × learnt θh** (0.630 at θh 0.49) | Centres the band in the window |
| How it is set | Transmitter throttle override on the transition switch (exact channel value), not a stick position | Removes the stick error |
| Pre-climb | **2.0 s** in QSTABILIZE at that throttle, then FBWA | Credit w0 = 0.8 × (T0 − dl) g t = **4.17 m/s** at the low edge |

are < 1.0 of the 30 % cap (`CL-B3-TRANS-PITCH-USE-HOVERTHR`). An unplanned hover-throttle entry uses up the gust and

### Verdict

- **The answer to A1's question is that D-121 is not only a flight-test choice. It stands in for a term the NP model
  lacks.**
  - AVL is inviscid. On B2's XFOIL S5010 polars, the viscous section aerodynamic centre sits ahead of c/4:
    dcm/dcl = +0.0126 at 1.3 Vs (Re 3.4e5, free transition, Ncrit 9).
  - That moves the NP **4.0 mm forward** (−dcm/dcl × c, unswept wing).
  - With a free-transition wing, the A3 predicted CG 0.04962 m has SM **4.2 %**, below 5 %. That applies to every
    flight, not only the first ones, until the NP is measured.
- **Honest design fix: a turbulator strip on the upper surface of both wing panels.**
  - It makes the tripped polar the design state. dcm/dcl is then +0.0011 (XFOIL, xtr 0.02) to +0.0032 (B2's SU2 SA),
    so the NP shift is at most 1.0 mm.
