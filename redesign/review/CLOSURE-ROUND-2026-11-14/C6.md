## 1. Failing claim, cause, fix

### `CL-C6-DOG-HOLD-RF`: the dog alone holds the cant twist if the clamp is lost (lower bound)

**Cant check.** The 0.909 in `proofs/out/graph.json` is already at 9°. `CALC-C6-DOG` reads the C4 design twist
`CL-C4-D3-MOUNT-TWIST` = 0.347 N·m. That value comes from C4's 9° / 12.0 mm run (`team/C4/claims.d3.json`, inputs
cant 9.0, x_prop −0.01704, x_face 0.0198). The same number appears in `review/DESIGN-ROUND-2026-11-14/C4.md` §1.5, and
it was 0.258 at 7°. Only the claim title still said 7°.

**Cause (corrected model).** The dog is an ASA block that hangs from the lower face of the mount collar
(`subsystems/fins/fusion/fins.py`, `build_mount` "clocking dog"). The collar is printed with its axis vertical, so the
root of the dog is a layer plane. The tangential force on the notch wall, F = T_twist / r_m = 0.347 / 0.0120 = 28.9 N,
acts e = gap + key_d/2 below that root:

- gap = 0.5 mm (`station_collar.gap_m`)
- key_d/2 = 1.25 mm

So the root carries shear **and** bending, and the bending puts tension across the layers. The old model checked shear
alone, at the top of the notch.

| Quantity (9°, design) | Old model, 3.0 / 2.5 mm | Corrected model, 3.0 / 2.5 mm | Proposal, 7.0 / 2.0 mm | Allowable (lower bound) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Root shear τ = F / (w · 1.6 mm) | 6.03 MPa | 6.03 MPa | 2.58 MPa | 0.26 × 36.5 / √3 = 5.48 MPa |
| Root bending σ = 6 F e / (1.6 mm · w²) | not checked | 21.1 MPa | 3.32 MPa | 0.26 × 36.5 = 9.49 MPa |
| RF_hold = 1 / (τ/τ_il + σ/σ_il) | 0.909 (τ only) | **0.301** | **1.217** | ≥ 1.0 |
| Bearing on the PETG notch wall F / (key_d · 1.6 mm) | — | 7.24 MPa | 9.05 MPa (RF 2.60) | 23.5 MPa, `allowables.petg_inplane_pa` |

Sources for the table:

- Allowables: `ASM-C6-ASA-YIELD` 36.5 MPa (Push Plastic ASA TDS) and `ASM-C6-ASA-INTERLAYER` 0.26 = 11 / 42 MPa
  (Prusament ASA TDS, D-107). Both are UNVERIFIED until T-C6-DOG-SHEAR.
- The interaction is linear, which is the conservative choice.
- Radial depth 1.6 mm = min(station-collar ring wall 1.6, collar wall 2.0).

**Fix.** Make the dog wider. Width is the only lever that works on both terms: shear falls as 1/w and bending as 1/w².
A shallower engagement also shortens the lever. The width and depth sweep below is the same formula with T = 0.347 N·m
at 9° and 0.393 N·m at the 10° contingency (`C4.md` §1.5).

| key_w \ key_d | 1.5 mm | 2.0 mm | 2.5 mm |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.0 mm | 0.37 / 0.33 | 0.33 / 0.29 | 0.30 / 0.27 |
| 5.0 mm | 0.81 / 0.72 | 0.74 / 0.66 | 0.68 / 0.61 |
| 6.0 mm | 1.06 / 0.93 | 0.97 / 0.86 | 0.90 / 0.80 |
| **7.0 mm** | 1.31 / 1.16 | **1.22 / 1.07** | 1.14 / 1.00 |
| 8.0 mm | 1.57 / 1.39 | 1.47 / 1.30 | 1.38 / 1.22 |

I chose 7.0 / 2.0 mm, the smallest width that passes at both 9° and 10°. 1.5 mm engagement was not chosen: it raises
the notch-wall bearing to 12 MPa, and fewer than 8 layers of engagement makes seating less certain. 7.0 mm is also
about the widest dog that stays inside the collar's outer radius:

- The box corners at y = ±3.5 mm reach r = 13.27 mm. The collar is 13.2 mm, so the corners stand 0.07 mm proud of the
  collar outer face.
- The inner corners are at r = 11.73 mm, outside the 11.2 mm bore, so the dog does not touch the tube.
- The dog sits at −x. The brace ear spans x ±8 mm about the tube axis and the tail is at +x, so both stay clear of the
  dog at x −11.2 to −12.8 mm.

**Mass (EST):**

- The dog grows from 3 × 1.6 × 3.0 = 14.4 mm³ to 7 × 1.6 × 2.5 = 28.0 mm³. That is +13.6 mm³ of ASA at 1.07 g/cm³

  1.27 g/cm³, or −0.014 g per collar and −0.058 g for four.
- Net: **+0.001 g ≈ 0.0 g**. CG and cost do not change.

- Foam bonds (`CL-C6-POD-*`, `CL-C6-FIN-ROOT-RF`) use the same microsphere mix class; allowables from `T-C6-XPS-PULL` unchanged.
