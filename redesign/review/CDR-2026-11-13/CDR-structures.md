## Verdict

**Go-with-conditions for the mid-December order. No-go for cutting flight half-fins or printing flight collars,
shoes and mounts until conditions 1–4 close.**

The order itself is low-risk for structures: the airframe materials are cheap (about $125 per airframe, EST,
`review/STRUCTURE-WING.md` SW-11) and no open structural item changes the bill of materials by more than a tube
SKU, a pad material and some cloth or strip. The design is not at CDR maturity in one area, the half-fin under the
D-65 sideways-landing load (39.3 N limit per foot, `team/C1/SUMMARY.md:21`). Every model the team has run on that
load says the paper face of the fin fails, the strip tail fails, and the fixes on the table are either not drawn or
not funded in mass. The proof graph hides part of this (CDR-ST-03).
