# K09-L critical design review: electrical, avionics and flight software

Reviewer: J6-electrical (CDR board), 2026-11-13, branch `redesign/v1` at `39d52a2`.
Scope: power distribution, harness, avionics, flight software and ArduPilot parameters, RC link, thermal
(`subsystems/{power,harness,avionics,flight-software}/`, `team/{E3,F1,F2,F3,F4,F5}/`).
Method: read the SPEC, SUMMARY, wire list, claims and parameter files; re-ran `python -I redesign/team/F4/fsw_check.py`
(print only; lint OK, no negative claim); read the generated `claims.json` files instead of re-running any script that
writes; checked ratings against the ArduPilot pages and maker or distributor data (URLs at the end, all checked
2026-11-13). No file other than this one was edited. Nothing in scope is bench-tested, so every "passes" below means
"passes on paper". My own arithmetic is labelled `REVIEWER EST`.

## Verdict

**Go with conditions** for the 15 December order.

The electrical architecture is sound and the gauges, connectors and BECs are sized with margin: 10 AWG mains at
0.5 °C above their joints, 16 AWG ESC leads at 9.2 °C on the 8.4 A hover share, AS150U 70 A against 37.8 A, servo
BEC 8 A against 5.6 A, FC BEC 4 A against 0.87 A, pack at 15 C against a 120 C label. The motor map, `Q_OPTIONS`,
serial ports, bdshot outputs and battery-monitor defaults in `K09L.param` agree with the ArduPilot pages I read. No
finding needs a different architecture.

It is not a clean go because three things the December order depends on are still unknown, and all three can be
settled with the early cart (~20 November, `team/I1/ORDER-PLAN.md`) before 15 December:
