# CDR fix round, 2026-11-13: build-sequence physics (assembly check)

These change-log rows are merged into `params/CHANGE-LOG.md` together with the S1–S4 and integrator rows.

The owner asked that every build step pass a real 3-D check, with no exemption by declaration
(`showcase/animation/assembly_check.py`). With every exemption removed, five CAD conflicts remained, and no motion
could fit the parts as drawn. They are fixed here in the CAD itself. Distances are in the aircraft frame (x aft,
y right, z up), in mm unless marked. EST where marked.
