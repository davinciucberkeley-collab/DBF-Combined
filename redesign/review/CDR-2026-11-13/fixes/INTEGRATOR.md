| **D-135** | **Power board on a foam pad above the frame screws; screws back to head on the deck (CDR-17, CDR-EL-03)** | `subsystems/power/fusion/power.py` (board z, foam pad body, frame-screw seat, D-89c keep-out removed), `subsystems/power/params.json` `board_pad_m` [0.048, 0.016, 0.0033] (new), `make_power.py` and `team/C6/fastener_parts.py` (PWR-103 0.5 mm washer), `team/F2/harness.py` (`board_in` / `board_out` / `pdb_link` ports +2.3 mm, W-MAIN-1 and -2 pad points, W-PMOD start, `LAP_FLAT.pad` 0.0491 → 0.0514) | D-89b and D-89c drove M3 frame screws "down through the board's mounting holes". The CoreWing PDB has Ø2 mm holes on 30.5 × 26.5 mm ([M] p. 13, `power/SPEC.md`), so no M3 screw passes them. The two aft screws also sat under the 10 AWG joints, and thread in the insert fell to 3.4 mm against the 4.5 mm minimum. Now the screw heads sit on the deck over a 0.5 mm washer, giving 18 − (0.5 + 2 + 10) = 5.5 mm of thread (`CL-PWR-ENGAGE`). The board sits on its spec's case S mount: a 3.3 mm foam pad (3 × PWR-106 1.1 mm tape) along its centreline, clear of the heads (head edge 9.7 mm from the axis, top 2.3 mm over the deck), plus one tie. That leaves 1.0 mm between the heads and the board. No fastener passes the board, and the board lifts out with its leads attached (`power/BUILD.md` "Service"). The main leads follow the board up 2.3 mm. `collide.py` PASS. |
| **D-136** | **Spar slot drawn in the core; cap strip `WNG-008` (build-film physics check)** | `subsystems/wing/fusion/wing.py` (`cap_loop`, "spar slot" cuts on WNG-001, WNG-008 bodies, E-WNG-18), `team/D1/wing_data.py` (WNG-008 part, mass taken out of WNG-001, E-WNG-18) | The build film showed the spar going up through solid foam at step 2.5. The assembly check passed it only because `assembly_plan.OVERRIDES` exempted WNG-002 and the plugs from the core by declaration: the slot that BUILD-GUIDE 2.2 cuts from below was never drawn. Now WNG-001 has that slot: x 0.0688–0.0912 (x_s ± bore radius 11.2 mm), from below the lower skin up to the spar axis z 0.0066. It runs wherever the spar is in foam; the block and anchor pockets are already full depth. `WNG-008` is the slot strip re-bonded under the spar as the cap (E-WNG-01 already described this). It stops at the lip beads (`bead_depth`) and round the pod-tie channels (bore + `tie_d`). Its foam mass moves out of WNG-001, so the total is unchanged. `collide.py` PASS (1 177 bodies, 220 parts); `wing_selfcheck` PASS. The exemption is removed by the build-sequence check rework. |
| **D-137** | **Mass re-balanced after S1 and S4 (CDR-12)** | `params/params.json` `mass.allocation_g.fins` 466 → 435, `.landing` 60 → 71, `mass.growth_reserve_g` 194 → 214, `requirements.build_overrun_kg` 0.064 → 0.084; `team/A3/mass_model.csv` (the three `FIN-A3-CDR-*` adders removed); `params/mass_budget.json` regenerated (A1 `frame_claims.py`) | S4 booked 31 g of structures contingency as fins adders. A3 now gives predicted 2 502 g, gate margin +98 g against the 84 g overrun, and pack accepted up to 563.6 g (the 551 g pack passes again). |
| **D-138** | **Foot side-load cap confirmed at 24.5 N (B1)** | `params/params.json` `requirements.foot_side_load_cap_n` 24.5, `pad_mu_max` 0.40 (S1's keys) | The board's 23 N came from a linear estimate.
