# A3 mass properties: design round 2026-11-14, mass-reduction search

**Verdict.** No package of design changes alone closes the round. The wave-1 fixes take `CL-A3-GATE-MARGIN`
from +98.4 g to **+31.0 g (11×5.5MR props)** or **+9.3 g (11×5.5E props)** against the 84 g build-overrun line.
That is **53.0 g or 74.7 g short** (predicted, `EST`). This is more than the 45–70 g in the brief, because C4 booked
the tube's growth from the wrong maturity (§1.2).

The design-only savings I can defend recover 21.5 g. That leaves the round **31.5 g short (MR) or 55.5 g short (E)**.

The cheapest close is two $0 checks on parts in the early electronics order:
- weigh the CoreWing power board: −21.6 g predicted if it is the listed 11.4 g;
- measure the X3108S factory phase leads: −16.6 g if they reach the ESC without extensions.

With both, the **MR-prop branch closes at +6.7 g free** (gate margin 90.7 g ≥ 84 g). The **E-prop branch is still
17.2 g short**. It closes (+10.1 g) only if two structural levers are added, and both sit next to C2's open block-corner
fail. Neither branch closes with comfortable margin.

`CL-A3-CG-X-STATIONS` passes in every package: 1.013 → **0.899 (MR)** or **0.791 (E)**. It depends on C5's 7 mm nose
move. Without the move the MR package reads **0.972**.

`CL-A3-ALLOC-WORST` (mechanisms 73.3 against 73 g) is not fixed by any saving. A1 resets the allocations as part of
the round; the numbers are in §3.

Scope: analysis only. The scratch script is `team/A3/round1114/package.py`; its output is
`team/A3/round1114/out/package.json`. It loads `rollup/mass_items.json` and `params.json`, adds delta items, and calls
A3's own `mass_rollup()` and `cg_cases()` from `mass_rollup.py`. So every number below uses the claims' equations.
The baseline reproduces 98.4 g and 1.0125. Nothing shared was edited, `mass_model.csv` is unchanged, no git, and no
FE or SU2 was run. `EST` = estimate; "—" = unknown. Grams are **predicted** (with `ASM-A3-MGA` growth) unless marked
basic.
