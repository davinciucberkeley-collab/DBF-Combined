# C5 landing / impact: design round 2026-11-14 (wave 1)

**Verdict.** Both C5 fails can close by design changes, and no limit is loosened.

- **`CL-C5-PACK-STACK-MARGIN`** (R-PACK-RETENTION) is a real fail, and it is worse than reported. On the block section as drawn it is **−6.0 mm**, not −3.3 mm.
  - notes-P's 4 mm nose move is not enough. It leaves −2.3 mm on the drawn section, or −1.1 mm with C3's step block.
  - **Recommended:** move the nose 7 mm forward and use C3's step block. The margin becomes **+1.9 mm** (+0.7 mm if the block stays as drawn).
  - Effect: mass **+1.6 g** (`EST`); CG **0.13 mm forward**; the most forward pack centroid stays at **−0.0382**, which meets C3's condition.
- **`CL-C5-SIM3D-V-BROADSIDE`** (R-WIND) cannot reach 5 m/s with any foot or pad lever I can justify.
  - Best option (no hardware): **land belly into the wind.**
  - With the wind on the belly (−z) face, the claim's lull limit is **5.3 m/s** (5.7 m/s at ±30° heading error).
  - With the wind on the pod (+z) face it stays **4.5 m/s**.
  - The only hardware fix that works in any heading is an outboard toe on the two +z feet of **≥ 25 mm**. It gives 5.1–5.2 m/s but costs about +7 g and moves the CG **0.9 mm aft**. Not recommended.

Every simulation runs on the live inputs (A3 basic 2.378 kg), unchanged physics (`team/C5/sim/landing_sim3d.py`
`simulate()`), and in-script overrides only. No shared file was edited.

Scripts: `team/C5/dr1114/crash_stack.py`, `broadside_levers.py`, `confirm.py`, `toe_envelope.py`. Records:
`team/C5/dr1114/out/*.json`, `*_log.txt`.

## 1. Fail 1: `CL-C5-PACK-STACK-MARGIN` −3.3 mm vs ≥ 0 (R-PACK-RETENTION)

### 1.1 Cause, re-checked
The model is `CALC-C5-CRASH` (`team/C5/landing_calc.py`):

- stack margin = `ASM-A3-NOSE-STACK` − (8.8 mm + 1.5 mm spreader + L_B);
- L_B = KE / (F · eff);
- F = min(100 g · m, σ · A);
- eff = 0.55 − 0.05/2 = 0.525.

| Check | Result | Source |
|---|---|---|
| notes-P reproduction, A = bay W × H = 52 × 56.9 mm = 2.959e-3 m², F = 509 N | L_B(590 g) 27.60 mm, margin **−3.30 mm** | `crash_stack.json` `as_is` |
| Per pack, A3 station −0.036 vs `x_fwd_max` | 546 g +0.66, 551 g +0.42, **590 g −1.40 mm** | same; `team/A3/rollup/rollup.json` `cg.station_cases` |
| **Drawn section** (D-143: block top 3 mm under the hatch, C3) A = 0.052 × (0.0594 − 0.003) − 2.4e-4 = 2.693e-3 m², F = 463 N | L_B(590 g) **30.33 mm**, margin **−6.03 mm** | `crash_stack.json` `sections`; `C3.md` §crush block |

- notes-P's finding is confirmed: A3 flies the 590 g pack 1.4 mm ahead of its block-B limit.
- C5's own model overstates the force by 10 %, because it uses bay W × H instead of the drawn section. This is a model correction. It goes in §4 and does not loosen anything.
- Block A is the 1 mm epoxy line (D-47) and gets no credit (C3 model corrected, A1 message 2026-11-14).

Notes on the table:
- **C3 step:** block top back to 0.5 mm clear, with a 3 (z) × 3 (x) mm step on the fore top edge for the hatch tongue (C3's proposal).
  - The step lowers the force over its first 3 mm of block. That costs 3 × (1 − 459/486) = 0.17 mm of extra length, which is included in the table.
- **Plateau:** σ = 172 kPa (`ASM-D5-XPS-PLATEAU`, `UNVERIFIED`). The FOAMULAR 250 sheet gives 25 psi as a *minimum* (ASTM D1621; https://www2.owenscorning.com/comminsul/documents/foamular250.pdf, checked 2026-11-14). That makes a low plateau less likely than a high one.
  - With the step block, a high plateau is bounded at +10 % (546 g pack at 100 g).
  - The 7 mm move covers −6 % on the low side.
  - 8 mm would buy −9 % for another 1 mm of box slack. Not chosen: T-D5-COUPON decides it before the pod is cut.
- **Rejected options:**
  - F-400 block: 816 N over the section, 152 g on the 546 g pack (notes-P, re-derived with the same formula).
  - Per-pack block cut sitting on 100 g: zero margin.
  - Dropping the stop: loses the D-69 T-stop.
  - These are the same reasons notes-P gives, re-checked against the formula and not re-run.

## 2. Fail 2: `CL-C5-SIM3D-V-BROADSIDE` 4.5 vs 5.0 m/s (R-WIND)

### 2.1 Cause
The claim is the lull minimum of the broadside wind cases.
- Touchdown tilt = hover lean(V) + 10° error, toward the **+z (pod-side) pad line**.
- Drift 0.3 m/s the same way, rotor lift 2/3 W ramped out from 0.3 to 0.6 s, no attitude control.
- Script references: `landing_sim3d.py` §3; `landing_sim_claims.py` line 89.

Geometry and behaviour:
- The +z lever is 0.219 − 0.018 = 0.201 m against a CG height of 0.319 m. That gives a static tip angle of 32°.
- Dynamically the aircraft tips from about 24–25°: drift toward the pad line, plus the edge-pivoting foot.
- The −z lever is 0.237 m, so leaning that way is safer. The current claim never checks it.
- The record `sim/out/sim3d.json` is stale (2.409 kg). On live inputs (2.378 kg) the claim value is unchanged at **4.5 m/s**.

**Foot geometry.** I simulated the toe by moving both +z pad corners outboard (pad size kept). The hard-landing envelope check (`toe_envelope.json`: gate mass, 1.2 m/s, 10° in 24 directions, μ 0.40) does not get worse with the toe:

|---|---|---|---|
| none | 25.2 mm | 5.95 | 24.11 N |
| +25 mm | 23.3 mm | 5.95 | 24.13 N (cap 24.5) |

The model does **not** carry the eccentric pad load into the piston and shoe. Today the pivot sits 8.9 mm off the piston axis; with the toe it would sit 34–39 mm off, roughly 4× the cocking moment. The shoe FEA would have to be redone.

**Toe mass (`EST`):** a PETG outrigger 25–30 × 19 × 3 mm plus a 1.6 mm neoprene pad, two feet, 5.6–6.7 g.
- Densities: PETG 1270, neoprene 1300 kg/m³ (`subsystems/landing/params.json`).
- At x 0.368 that moves the CG 0.75–0.90 mm aft. That is more than the whole aft CG margin of the 546 g pack.
- The envelope height rises from 0.438 to 0.463–0.468 m; height slack is 77 mm (A1 note).
- PETG cost is about $0.03 (SUNLU 4 kg $39.99, `config-study/cards/K01/cost.csv`, 2026-11-01).

**Pad face.** μ < 0.40 only helps if the feet stick and pivot instead of sliding, and it costs the steady case.
- UHMW tape for reference: JVCC UHMW-PE-15, 15 mil, ½ in × 5 ft, $4.91 (https://findtape.com/JVCC-UHMW-PE-15-UHMW-Polyethylene-Film-Tape/p749, checked 2026-11-14).
- No friction value is published for that tape on the rubber mat: "—".

### 2.3 Recommended fix
**Procedure: final descent and touchdown with the belly (−z) face into the wind, never the pod face.**
- On a heading-locked case (commanded heading ±30°), the claim's lull limit is **5.3 m/s**.
- In steady wind at 0.5 m/s sink it is 5.0 m/s, which is zero margin.
- The pod-face-to-wind case stays at 4.5 m/s. It becomes an information row with a card rule: "abort and re-orient if the pod faces the wind".

Effect: 0 g, 0 mm CG, $0. It replaces rule S2 (wingtip into the wind).

**Interaction with B3's fail.** Wingtip-into-wind is the case behind B3's `CL-B3-HOVER-YAW-WTIP-20` fail (1.32 vs 1.5). B3 lists belly into wind at 5 m/s as passing (ratio 2.83, `team/B3/S-AND-C.md` §9.2). The yaw stability of a belly-to-wind hover in a 5 m/s lull is B3's call, not mine.

**If A1 requires a heading-independent pass:** the +z toe of ≥ 25 mm (z_outer +z 0.219 → 0.244) is the only lever that gets there. Its costs (+5.6 g, CG 0.75 mm aft, new shoe FEA) make it the second choice.

**Not a lever:** the drift reduction to 0.15 m/s. Its basis is an assumption, and only the twin's landing logs (T-C5-DRIFT-LOG) can lower it.
