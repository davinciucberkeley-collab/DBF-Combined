## 4. Answers to D1 (via A1), requests, and open items

**D1 question 1: the reading of rule 2. Confirmed.**

- In |y| 0.27–0.37, for x ≤ 0.24, these are not allowed: a strip end, a butt joint, or a splice.
- The lengthwise lap edges of the 48 mm spanwise strips that run through the zone are allowed. The layup is
  `team/C2/sizing.json` (10 % overlap, 4.8 mm).
- **Why.** What failed in options 1–2 (`C2.md` §1.4) was a skin end that hands the station torque back into the foam
  near the block. A lap that runs through the zone has no end there. It adds a second layer over the 4.8 mm overlap,
  which is of the order of the PSA shear-lag length (2–5 mm `EST`).
- **Not modelled.** The FEA has a uniform layer with no laps, so the effect of a lap edge on the corner is "—".
- **Request to H1.** Make the taped coupon with one lap running through the block band, as on the wing.

**D1 question 2: the servo-pocket opening. Yes, it is in the model, and it now has its own claims.**

- In the local model the opening is a void with no skin over it. Probe: the material is `None` at
  x 0.175–0.205, y 0.26–0.285, from the lower skin up.
- The servo-pocket tape doubler (`sizing.json` "one extra chordwise band … over each servo pocket edge") is not
  modelled. Leaving it out is conservative.
- **Governing point.** The aft-outboard corner, at (0.2078, 0.2875, −0.0056) m. At 50 % credit:
  28.8 / 91.9 / 44.3 kPa.

  | Case | s3 at that corner |
  |---|---|
  | 25 % credit | 88.6 |
  | `-CUTS` | 91.8 |
  | G 4.31 GPa | 72.4 |
  | untaped | 75.5 |

  All are below 120.
- **Open.** The local cut plane sits at y 0.27, and results are kept 6 mm clear of it. The two **inboard corners**
  (|y| 0.2527) are therefore only in the coarse global model. Their local stress is "—".

**Open items (requests to A1):**

   `c2r5_measure.py`, `c2r5_reduce.py` and `fea_claims.py`. Until then the tape model has two levels (1.05 % change)
   and the 3-level GCI is on the untaped geometry.
2. **Inboard servo-opening corners.** These need a global solve with local planes at about 0.235 / 0.335 m, then a local
