# C2 wing structures: design round 2026-11-14, wave 1

**Verdict.** The block-pocket corner fail (`CL-C2-FEA-FOAM-CORNER-*`) has two parts:

- **A measure that could never converge.** The current measure is an element peak or a 1 mm average at a
  bimaterial corner. That point is a true stress singularity. Its peak grows with every mesh refinement.
- **A model that leaves out structure the wing has.** The as-drawn BOPP tape skin and its two-layer block doubler
  are not in the FEA.

Changes and results:

- **Measure.** It is replaced by a critical-distance measure from the literature. This is a volume average over a
  ball of radius L/2 = 1.78 mm, with the ball centre placed where it gives the maximum.
  - L comes from the XPS fracture toughness and strength.
  - The measure converges: the three-level GCI is ≤ 0.5 %.
  - **It does not save the current design.** It still fails, at 214 / 144 / 101 kPa ultimate against
    150 / 120 / 100.
- **Model correction.** The as-drawn tape skin and doubler are added at the 50 % effectiveness the beam design
  basis already uses. The corner then passes, converged, at **102 / 75 / 52 kPa** (margins +47 % / +61 % / +92 %).
  - It still passes at 25 % tape effectiveness: 113 / 83 / 55 kPa at L1.
  - It costs 0 g and $0.
  - No allowable changes.
- **Recommendation.** Take this measure and the corrected model, with a coupon test that decides the tape credit.
- **Fallback, if the coupon fails.** A ±45 glass D-cell from the root to 25 mm outboard of each block. It passes
  by 2.7× with no tape credit, at +16.5 g EST and about $43.
- **Rejected: the local glass band from notes-W.** It moves the problem to the band ends: 252–299 kPa at L1,
  worse than today.

Paths are relative to `redesign/`. Every run was made on 2026-11-14. `EST` marks an estimate and "—" an unknown.
All stresses are at ultimate (1.5 × limit) for the governing case `block/L4-CORNER-SIDE-Y/MC-C`. The allowables
are `wing_struct.M` (unchanged): tension 150, compression 120, shear 100 kPa.

### 1.2 Why the peak cannot converge: the corner is a singularity
The peaks sit at two places, both just under the lower skin on the inboard pocket face:

- the toe of the 3 mm microballoon bead, at (0.0296, 0.2989, −0.0069) m;
- the PETG block's aft edge, at (0.0958, 0.2989, −0.0083) m.

At each, the foam is bonded to a material 110–150 times stiffer and meets a free surface. `team/C2/fea/c2r5_wedge.py`
solves the Williams eigenproblem for a foam wedge (ν 0.25, plane strain) with a rigid face and a free face. The
displacement formulas are from Barber, *Elasticity*, Table 8.1. It is checked two ways: a crack gives λ = 0.500,
and the 180° rigid–free case gives Im λ = 0.1103 = ln κ / 2π, as theory states.

| Corner | λ | Stress field |
|---|---|---|
| block face meets the free surface (90° foam wedge) | 0.745 | σ ~ r^−0.255 |
| bead toe (135° foam wedge) | 0.698 ± 0.04 i | σ ~ r^−0.30 |

A singular corner has no finite peak, so the element peak and any average over a few elements go up as h goes down:

| Measure | L1 (h 1.6) | L2 (1.1) | L3 (0.75) | GCI (Celik) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| element peak s1 | 312 | 332 | 389 | no asymptotic range |
| 1 mm sphere at the peak, s1 (the D-68 claim measure) | 249 | 284 | 251 | 2.84 |

The GCIs come from `team/C2/fea/c2r5_gci.py`.

### 1.3 The right measure: theory of critical distances, volume form
Quasi-brittle foams fail at a notch when the stress over a material length L reaches the plain strength. That
length is the critical distance. Taylor's point method reads the stress at L/2; the line method averages it over 2L.
TCD is applied to notched polymer foams (Negru et al. 2015). In cellular solids, defects smaller than about four
cells do not matter.

**L for this foam, using the conservative end of each input:**

L = (1/π)(K_Ic / σ₀)² = (1/π)(0.0547 MPa√m / 0.517 MPa)² = **3.56 mm**, so L/2 = **1.78 mm**.

- K_Ic = 0.0547 MPa√m, measured on XPS of E 10.7 MPa. Our model uses E 10 MPa.
- σ₀ = 517 kPa (75 psi). This is the FOAMULAR 250 minimum flexural strength, the highest strength on record. A
  higher σ₀ gives a shorter, more conservative L. A tensile σ₀ of 300 kPa would give L = 10.6 mm.
- L/2 is 6–18 XPS cells (cell size 0.1–0.3 mm, `ASM-C2-FOAM-AVG-R`). That is above the 4-cell threshold, so the
  continuum stress is meaningful there.

**Measure used: vm(L/2).** This is the foam-only volume average over a ball of radius 1.78 mm, maximised over the
ball centre; the 3 000 highest-stressed foam elements are tried as centres.

- For σ ~ ρ^−s, a ball average of radius R equals the point value at ρ = R(3/(3−s))^(−1/s), which is 0.70 R for
  s = 0.30.
- So vm(L/2) reads the point-method stress at about 0.35 L. That is more conservative than both the PM (0.5 L)
  and the line method (about 0.6 L for these exponents).
- It needs no ray direction. For a smooth field it returns the field value, so it gives no credit where there is
  no gradient.
- Code: `team/C2/fea/c2r5_measure.py`.

A sphere-shell point method was tried first and dropped: around nearby hot spots it picked up the peak itself.

**The allowable is not changed.** The 150 kPa is already the 250–500 kPa strength halved, so the measure is
compared with a knocked-down value.

**Base D-68 design with vm(L/2):**

| | L1 | L2 | L3 | GCI fine | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| s1 | 195.7 | 212.4 | **213.9** | 0.001 | 150 |
| s3 | 147.1 | 144.7 | **143.7** | 0.005 | 120 |
| tau | 90.8 | 99.0 | **100.7** | 0.005 | 100 |

The measure converges, and the design **still fails all three**. The measure does not close the fail on its own.

**Measure caveats:**

- The literature basis is mode-I cracks in homogeneous foam. Leguillon's coupled criterion (2002) is the rigorous
  form for bimaterial corners, but it needs a G_c for the XPS / bead interface, and that value is "—".
- vm(L/2) at 0.35 L sits on the conservative side of the TCD forms, which covers part of this gap.

**What the runs show**

- **Why option 0 fails.** The L4 station torque is introduced at the block. The C1 cut at y 0.30 is
  M_y −5.63 N m limit, and 0.08 N m at 0.34 (`team/C1/data/wing_spanwise.csv`). With nothing on the surface, the
  stiff block twists against bare foam.
- **Why options 1–2 fail.** Any skin stiff enough to take the torque off the block corners carries it to its own
  ends and hands it back to the 22 N m² tube through the foam. Band GJ is about 120 N m² EST (4A²Gt/s,
  A 3.17e-3 m², s 0.247 m). That hand-off is worse than the original corner, even at 1/7 of the stiffness. The
  option-2 ends sit 6–12 mm from the cut planes, so their magnitude is uncertain, but the trend is clear.
- **Option 3.** The collar softens the modulus step but keeps a corner. It fails by 1 % on s1 and 13 % on s3. It
  also needs a machined PVC frame let into the core.
- **Option 5 (recommended).** The tape is everywhere and stiffens the surface over the block with no strong end.
  The 2-layer doubler band that D1 already draws (`skin.block_doubler_x_m` 0.12, 60 mm) is the right amount.
  More layers (5b) add a doubler-end peak. Tape stress at the doubler is 3.75 MPa layer stress (~19 MPa in the
  film EST), against the 60 MPa tape allowable.
- **Option 6 (fallback).** It carries the station torque in a closed D-cell to the root, so there is no hand-off
  near the block. Laminate shear at ultimate is q = T/2A = 8.4 / (2 × 3.17e-3) = 1 323 N/m, which is 12.7 MPa in
  1 ply of 3.12 oz and 28.8 MPa in Style 1080. Both are far below woven E-glass ±45 strength (> 100 MPa EST).
  - Global tip w in L4 drops from 2.80 to 1.03 mm (`global_block_gband.pkl`). This changes twist, frequency and
    spar-stress claims owned by C4 / B2 / B3 (§3).
  - The root region (pod recess, y < 0.07) is not checked locally.

**Other cases.** The other block cases were well below the allowables in the base model (L3 averages
44.7 / 50.8 kPa limit for F1 / G2). G2 on option 6 gives 18 / 22 / 13 kPa ult.

**Glass and resin data used (all EST):**

- Woven E-glass/epoxy ply at V_f 0.45: E 20.4 GPa, G12 2.6 GPa, ν12 0.114 (NTNU TMM4175 notebook).
- Rotated to ±45: G_xy 9.2 GPa, E 8.5 GPa. Knocked down × 0.8 for wet lay-up at V_f 0.40.

- the corrected model, which includes the tape skin and the 2-layer block doubler that are already drawn;

**What the claims move to:** 102 / 75 / 52 kPa ult against 150 / 120 / 100, converged at L2.

**Build and CAD rules for D1.** The geometry is unchanged. These become requirements, not choices:

1. **Block doubler.** Two extra BOPP layers over each block. The band is 60 mm spanwise, centred on |y| 0.320
   (y 0.290–0.350), wrapped round the LE from upper x 0.120 to lower x 0.120 m. This is the existing
   `skin.block_doubler_x_m` 0.12 and `servo_pocket.doubler_w_m` 0.06.
   - Slit only round the tube boss and the half-fin root, with **≤ 2 mm clearance**.
   - The band must cover the pocket faces, y 0.30185 / 0.33815, and the bead toes 3 mm beyond them.
   - Mark the band "structural (C2-R5)" in `subsystems/wing/params.json` notes and in the BUILD-GUIDE.
2. **Skin.** The base skin layer runs unbroken over the block span: no tape seam or overlap edge between
   y 0.27 and 0.37 m on either surface, for x ≤ 0.24.
3. **Field repair.** A damaged doubler is replaced in kind (2 layers) before flight. This goes on the H3 flight card.

**Coupon test that decides it (add to `T-C2-XPS-COUPON`, owner H1).**

- **Specimen.** An XPS slab of 25 mm wing thickness × 150 × 150 mm with a printed PETG block 40 × 36 mm, bonded
  and beaded as D-68, and taped skin + 2 doubler layers as in rule 1.
- **Loading.** Twist the block about the spar axis. The coupon torque that reproduces the wing's L4 corner strain is "—":
  C2 sizes it with `c2r5_corner.py` on the coupon geometry before the test.
- **Pass.** No foam crack at 1.5 × the scaled limit.
- **Control.** A second coupon without tape. If it does not crack either, the vm(L/2) measure is shown to be
  conservative.
- **Fail.** If the taped coupon cracks below 1.5×, go to the fallback.

**Fallback, already sized: option 6, root-continuous ±45 glass D-cell.** If the coupon fails, or the team would
rather not rely on the PSA tape:

- **Layup.** One ply of Style 1080 (1.4 oz) cut on the bias at ±45. It passes at L1 by ×2.1 (option 6lt).
- **Extent.** Wrapped from upper x 0.120 round the LE to lower x 0.120 m. Spanwise it runs continuous across the
  centreline, |y| 0 → 0.363 m (block outer face 0.338 + 0.025).
  - It goes over the pod-recess floor and walls; D2 / C3 confirm the pod bond on glass.
  - A 2 mm cut-out round each tube boss.
  - Ends cut square, 10 mm ply-drop taper EST.
- **Process.** Laid with laminating epoxy after the spar and block bonds and before the tape skin and the pod bond.
- **Mass** (laminate at V_f 0.40 + 15 % excess resin, EST, on 0.1795 m² = 0.2472 m arc × 0.7263 m):
  **+16.5 g** in Style 1080 or **+36.7 g** in Style 116. Areal mass 92 / 205 g/m².
