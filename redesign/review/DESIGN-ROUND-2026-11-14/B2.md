# B2 wing aero: `CL-B2R3-VTR-GATE-FWD` (design round 2026-11-14, wave 1)

**Verdict: the wing reproduces 15.70 m/s, and no wing-side change closes the fail with honest margin.** A direct AVL run
at the forward limit gives 1.3 Vs = **15.700 m/s** (claim 15.703). That is 0.10 m/s, or 0.6 %, over the 15.6 key.

**The cause is up-elevon trim at the forward CG, not Re and not a model artefact.** At x 0.0394 the trimmed stall needs
−15.8° of elevon. At the target CG it needs −10.8°. Up-elevon lowers the critical strip's section clmax from 1.256 to
1.021, and the extra 5° takes the trimmed CLmax from 0.791 to 0.775.

**The planning baseline gives 16.82 m/s.** Board decision B6 makes the tripped section the baseline, and the
aircraft flies `AIRSPEED_MIN` 17 on it. On that basis 1.3 Vs is 16.82 m/s, which already exceeds the 15.6 key by
1.2 m/s, so 15.6 is no longer the transition-end speed. Changing the key is B1's corridor decision. The full Vs /
1.3 Vs table over `cg.x_range_m` is in §2.

**Recommendation (B2):** make no change to the wing. The best wing lever is +2° linear wash-in. It passes on paper by
0.3 % (15.55 m/s), which is inside the 1.7 % XFOIL clmax spread, and it uses 0.38° of the remaining pitch-elevon
trim (§3). It is not worth a twisted core.

Script: `team/B2/sim/vtr_gate.py` → `team/B2/sim/out/vtr_gate.json`. It writes no shared file and no params, and the
proof graph was not run. Every number below is from that JSON unless another source is named. The output is
`MODELLED` (AVL 3.52 + XFOIL 6.99), not tested.
