## 1a. Wave-2 notes on earlier decisions

- **D-146 / CG range.** With the prop plane at −0.01704 (`CL-D3-X-PROP`) the aft CG limit of record is 0.05119 m (B3-W2.md); `cg.x_range_m` aft end 0.0513 → 0.0511. The range narrows; nothing is loosened.
- **D-149.** B3's time-domain model with the signed prop normal force gives `CL-B3-TRANS-PITCH-USE` 0.348 ≤ 0.60. `ASM-B3-TRANS-T0` stays PROPOSED until F4's SITL S4c logs the frozen throttle ≥ 0.65. The hover-thrust pitch-over rows (`-NOHUB-CAP30`, `-HMAX-CAP30`) now carry no limit because D-149 no longer flies that condition; **J6 to confirm this is a change of flight condition, not a loosened limit.**
- **D-152.** B3 finds the belly-into-wind heading statically stable at 5 m/s (net restoring 0.07–0.23 N·m at 30°, `EST`), but ArduPilot's nose-in weathervane acts only on roll lean beyond `Q_WVANE_ANG_MIN` 1°, so it corrects only beyond ±7.7–15.6° and slowly. The ±30° band is therefore set by the pilot: the H3 card sets the landing heading from the windsock by stick, AHRS level trim ≤ 0.5° before the twin flights, and the hanging yaw test plus C5's twin touchdown logs (M2) decide. `Q_WVANE_OPTIONS` stays 0 (bit 0 off). `CL-B3-HOVER-YAW-WTIP-*` stays in force.
- **D-147 (C4-W2).** `CL-C4-SIM-SEP` 0.145 → **0.244** (≥ 0.20), lower than the 0.322 predicted because B4's 17 m/s cruise rpm (6 347) raises 2P to 211.6 Hz. At the knock-down tube properties it is 0.189 (fails), so the GT-16 measurement on a delivered tube decides R-VIBRATION: pass lines E ≥ 65.7 GPa, G ≥ 8.67 GPa; measure before the fins are printed and re-run `sim_fin.py` with the measured values. Cruise above ≈ 6 582 rpm (`EST`) re-opens the claim.
- **D-155 (A3-W3, booked as drawn).** Fins 476.1 g (mounts +4.0 g drawn vs 2.4 booked), pod 147.3 g, plug 0 g: the round is 2.8 g heavier than §1 predicted. Allocations fins 477, pod 148, reserve 174 (sum 2 426 + 174). **Case (a) now: `CL-A3-GATE-MARGIN` 56.5 g (fails by 27.5 against 84), `CL-A3-PACK-ACCEPT` 522.5 g (fails by 33.5 against 556). Case (b) with S02 / S03 measured as listed: 94.7 g (+10.7 free), 560.7 g (+4.7).** CG passes in every case (`CL-A3-CG-X-PRED-RANGE` 0.789 / 0.909); the D-146 stack moves CG −0.79 mm. The D-cell fallback is 9.1 g short even in case (b). After S02 / S03: power 609, harness 128, reserve 205.

## 2. Fails that this round does not close (for the student team)

1. **R-MISSION as flown.** The requirement's own budget passes: 2 min hover + 5 min cruise to the 20 % line at 17 m/s on the
   pessimistic pack is 5.16 min (`CL-E2-CRUISE-MIN-PESS-V17`). But with the failsafe set to protect landing energy and the
   reserve, `BATT_LOW` fires after **3.61 min** of cruise on a pack at its 92 % acceptance floor. No LiPo that fits the bay
   and the gate closes it (best 4.72 min); the Li-ion pack fails thrust. Options for the team: tighten pack acceptance
   (0.97 floor: 4.28 min), accept a shorter cruise leg on worn packs, or raise the mass gate for a larger pack (7.5 Ah ≈ +85 g).
2. **Mass gate, if S02 / S03 do not come in.** See `A3.md` "Final package" for the shortfall. Raising the gate or the
   overrun line is the team's call.
3. Budget and schedule (R-BUDGET, R-RESERVE, R-BUILD-HOURS), unchanged from the CDR. D-147 adds about $152 and D-153's
   fallback about $44.
