## 3. Weathervane stability, belly into the wind (D-152)

**Firmware** (`AC_WeatherVane.cpp`, tag Plane-4.7.2, checked 2026-11-14):
- In NOSE_IN mode (`Q_WVANE_ENABLE` 1 means belly into the wind, because the tailsitter's copter nose is the belly), the
  yaw-rate demand is `GAIN` × (|roll| − `ANG_MIN`). It reads only the roll lean (pitch is used only with `OPTIONS` bit 0)
  and passes through a 0.98 / 0.02 low-pass.
- `K09L.param` sets `GAIN` 1. It does not set `ANG_MIN`, so the default 1.0° applies.
- On Plane, `HGT_MIN`, `SPD_MAX` and `VELZ_MAX` default to 0 (checks off). So the weathervane stays active down to
  touchdown.
- It switches off while there is any pilot yaw input.

**Model** (`CALC-B3-HOVER-BELLY`, `EST`; equation stored in the node):
- The broadside wing is restoring: the Kirchhoff CP shifts toward the upwind tip (the same form as `M_fin` in
  `CALC-B3-HOVER-WTIP`).
- The upwind half-fins are destabilising, because their CP lies upwind of the CG.
  - Lift slope: Helmbold low-aspect-ratio slope, 1.87 /rad.
  - Dynamic pressure on them: kq = 0.49 from the stagnation flow ahead of the plate. kq = 1 is kept as a bound.
  - Area: the params area of 0.0578 m² per half-fin, larger than the as-built 0.0455 m² (`verify_asdesigned.py`
    header). This is conservative.
- The downwind half-fins sit in the wake and are not counted. Their moment would be restoring, so leaving them out is
  conservative.
- The prop H-forces cancel in the symmetric pairs.

| 5 m/s | ψ = 10° | 20° | 30° | 8 m/s gust, 30° |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wing, restoring N·m | 0.141 | 0.270 | 0.376 | 0.962 |
| Fins, destabilising N·m (kq 0.49) | 0.050 | 0.100 | 0.150 | 0.385 |
| **Net, restoring +** (kq 0.49 / kq 1) | 0.091 / 0.039 | 0.169 / 0.066 | **0.226 / 0.070** | 0.577 / 0.180 |
| Roll lean to hold position (fins only make spanwise force) | 0.64° | 1.28° | 1.92° | 4.90° |

**Answer: yes, the heading is stable (`EST`).**
- Belly into the wind is a statically stable yaw attitude. Stiffness is 0.53 N·m/rad (0.23 at kq 1).
  `CL-B3-HOVER-BELLY-YAW-STIFF` ≥ 0 and `-YAW-NET-30` 0.070 ≥ 0 (kq 1).
- The aerodynamics push toward the commanded heading, so yaw authority is not the limit. M_ctrl at 9° is 0.654 N·m, more
  than any moment in the table.
- This is the opposite of the wingtip case, which is unstable toward broadside.

**What does not close: the ±30° heading band of D-152.**
- The weathervane senses only the roll lean, and belly into the wind the lean is small. It acts only beyond ±15.6°
  (`CL-B3-HOVER-BELLY-WVANE-DEADBAND`; ±7.7° at kq 1; ±6.1° in the 8 m/s gust).
- Inside that band the heading stays where the attitude controller holds it.
- At 30° the demand is only 0.9°/s.
- A 1° roll-trim error shifts the band to **31.3° on one side** (`-DEADBAND-TRIM1`), past C5's ±30°.
- The weathervane alone therefore does not guarantee ±30°. The level (AHRS) trim and the pilot's heading set from the
  windsock do.
- With a 0.5° trim error the band is about 23° (linear scaling of 15.6 × 1.5, `EST`).

**What decides it.**
1. Hanging yaw test (motors off, aircraft free to yaw, measured 5 m/s): it settles belly to the wind (static stiffness).
2. H1 AHRS level trim ≤ 0.5°.
3. Twin hover logs (C5 M2): heading error at touchdown with the weathervane on.
4. F4 SITL can check the controller logic only. The K09-L yaw aero needs stage B.

**`CL-B3-HOVER-YAW-WTIP-*` does not retire.** `Q_WVANE_TAKEOFF` 3 still puts a wingtip into the wind, and hover happens
at any heading (A1.md D-152). The claim now passes at 9°.
