# C4 fin / motor-tube structures: design round 2026-11-14, wave 1

**Verdict.** `CL-C4-SIM-SEP` (R-VIBRATION) passes with one change: the fin motor tube becomes the Rock West
35046-S filament-wound tube (0.875 × 0.750 in, ±15 / hoop / ±45 / ±15). In the 3-D model, three meshes, the
separation goes **0.145 → 0.358** at today's 7° / 7.5 mm mount. With B3's 9° cant and E1's 12 mm `face_ahead` it is
**0.322**, and at the 10° / 14 mm contingency it is **0.304**. The limit is ≥ 0.20. Every fin, mount and
collar margin that passes now still passes.

The fix costs mass the aircraft does not have. The tubes add **+37.3 g** basic (+35.7 g predicted with A3's growth,
`EST`). A3's free margin above the 84 g build-overrun line is only **14.4 g** (98.4 − 84,
`CL-A3-GATE-MARGIN`). So the tube puts `CL-A3-GATE-MARGIN` about **21 g short** (`EST`). notes-F said "the A3
margin is +98 g". That is the margin before the 84 g line is subtracted. A1 has to find about 21 g elsewhere, or
accept the tube and re-open the overrun line (§3). Nothing lighter passes:

- The 22 × 19 roll-wrapped tube (+26 g) passes only at today's mount. It fails at 9° / 12 mm.
- The mount, arm and brace levers are each under +2 %.
- Taking the brace off drops the mode to 178 Hz.

B3's longer overhang alone makes the existing fail worse. On today's tube the separation goes 0.145 →
**0.075 (−5.9 % in f1; 0.044 at 10° / 14 mm)** at 9° / 12 mm (3-D). The shell model goes 0.214 → 0.161, which now fails too. E1's 11 × 5.5EP prop
is worse again: the +6 g per prop gives **0.071** on today's tube. Both requests make the stiffer tube necessary,
not optional.

Everything here is analysis on scratch overrides in `team/C4/round1114/`. No shared file was edited and no git was
run. `EST` marks a hand estimate. "—" means unknown.

Notes on the levers:

- The shorter arm cannot go further. notes-F's −5.4 mm came from the prop-gap ratio, but the binding limit is E1's
  25 mm blade-to-tube clearance, which needs ≥ 6.5 mm (`fin_detail.mount.face_ahead_m` note, D-61). B3's cant moves
  `face_ahead` the other way (§1.5).
- Mount stiffening and the brace are each under 2 %. They are not a fix alone.
- The rpm side, raising the mode above 2P cruise, is not in C4's hands. A cruise rpm ≤ 5 718 would pass on today's
  tube (notes-F, `EST`). E1 and B1 are moving rpm **up** (EP prop, cant), not down (§1.5). So no request is made.
- A notch filter is mitigation, not a pass. It is not counted.

**Mode shape changes with the RW tube.** The first station mode is now **fore-aft** (271 Hz). The lateral-torsion
mode moves to 375 Hz (lower) and 388 Hz (upper), at L3. Both are clear of every forcing. The next forcing above them,
2P max at 9 319 / 30 = 310.6 Hz, is not in the rule. Even so, 375 Hz sits 21 % above it (`EST`, record).

## 3. Requests to other roles

- **R1 → A1 / A3 (mass, blocking).** The tube needs about 21 g of the gate margin above the 84 g overrun line
  (§2.4).
  - Within C4 nothing offsets it. The brace is load-bearing in the modes (178 Hz without it), and the fin laminate
    was not re-optimised in this round.
  - Choices for A1: take 21 g from another allocation; or accept 63 g of build overrun against 84; or accept the
    tube and weigh the delivered tubes first. The page density, 0.105 lb/ft, is a listing, and the sanding takes off
    about 4 % of the area.
  - The 22 × 19 tube saves 11 g but fails at B3's 9° / 12 mm. It is not a fallback once the cant moves.
- **R2 → E1 (prop).** On today's tube the 11 × 5.5EP prop (+6 g each, rpm up) drops `CL-C4-SIM-SEP` to **0.071**.
  On the RW tube it gives 0.258 at 9° / 12 mm. If E1 recommends the EP, it is acceptable only together
  with this tube.
- **R3 → C1 (loads).** Re-run the mount and fin load cases with 9° cant and `face_ahead` 12 mm (and 14 mm). The
  rocking moment rises about 5 % (`CL-C4-D3-MOUNT-ROCK` 1.99 → 2.09 N·m). C4's `CL-C4-D3-MS-*` claims read C1 and
  must be re-run after.
- **R4 → H1 (GT-16 card).** With the RW tube the shell-model pass lines fall to E ≥ 37.5 GPa, G ≥ 3.96 GPa at 9° /
  12 mm (`CL-C4-D3-TUBE-E-REQ`, `-G-REQ`). Keep testing against the page values with a −30 % knock-down. Measure the
  twist over 0.30 m on the drop.
- **R5 → I1 / S4 (BOM).** Replace the FIN-002 line: SKU 35046-S, 48 in, about $152 `EST`, 2–4 weeks.
