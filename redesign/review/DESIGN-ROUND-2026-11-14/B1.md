# B1 aero performance: design round 2026-11-14 (wave 1)

Role: B1 only. Fails analysed: **R-MISSION**, the cruise-power and speed side (`CL-E2-CRUISE-TO-LOW-PESS-V17`), and
**R-TRANSITION**, the speed side (`CL-B2R3-VTR-GATE-FWD` against the stale `performance.transition_min_ms`). No
params, subsystem or other-role file was edited, and nothing on the do-not-run list was run. New evidence is in
`team/B1/round-2026-11-14/`:

- `design_round.py` reuses the `performance.py` helpers and the input values saved in `claims.verify.json`, at the
  2.6 kg gate. It writes `design_round_results.json`.
- `xf_fin_edges.py` runs XFOIL on the fin-board edges. It writes `xf_fin_edges_results.json` and `xfoil/`.

All paths below are relative to `redesign/`.

## Verdict

- **R-TRANSITION closes with a design-speed change.** Set `performance.transition_min_ms` and
  `performance.cruise_ms` to 17 m/s, which is the speed the aircraft already flies (D-122, `K09L.param`). At the gate
  and the forward CG the corridor holds:
  - 1.3 Vs is 15.70 m/s on the clean stall: +1.30 m/s (+8.3 %).
  - 1.3 Vs is 16.82 m/s on the tripped stall: +0.18 m/s (+1.1 %).
  - The steady corridor uses at most 0.50 of available thrust (limit 0.80).
- **R-MISSION cannot be closed by a cruise speed the requirements allow.** E2's corrected count needs ≤ 315 W at the
  pack.
  - With the 11×5.5MR props, the pack power falls to 315 W only at **15.2 m/s**. That is below 1.3 Vs at the forward
    CG (15.70 m/s).
  - The best aero option (fin trailing-edge bevel) plus the 11×5.5E props reaches 361 W at 17 m/s. That still fails.
  - At 16 m/s the same pair gives 313.5 W, under the 315 W line by only 0.5 %. 16 m/s is also outside the B6 tripped
    baseline, and its margin above 1.3 Vs (0.30 m/s) is smaller than the airspeed error (0.45 m/s).
  - **A speed choice does not avoid E2's Li-ion pack.** The aero and prop savings are real (−11 % at 17 m/s) and are
    recommended either way.
- **The 406 W is reconciled** to within 0.1 %: B1's pessimistic 366.4 W × E1/B1 drive-chain ratio 1.055 × E2's
  circuit factor 1.05 = 405.8 W.

**Reconciling E2's 406.0 W.** `CALC-E2-BUDGET-V17` computes:

- k = (E1 286.2 + 6) / B1 241.8 at 16 m/s = 1.2088;
- pessimistic = B1's 16 m/s pessimistic 314.8 W at 2.50 kg × k × circuit 1.05 × mass scale → 406.0 W.

Built bottom-up, the same quantity is B1's direct 17 m/s gate pessimistic 366.4 W (no cant, as in E2) × 1.0547 ×
1.05 = **405.8 W**. E2's scaling `EST` (16 m/s pessimistic-to-base ratio carried to 17 m/s) is therefore correct
within 0.06 %. The B1 claim E2 asks for at 17 m/s is 366.4 W (368.1 W with cant), and it changes nothing.

**Model question for A1 (not a change).** The 406 W stacks three penalties:

1. B1's pessimistic η_prop × 0.85, for APC/BEMT optimism (`ASM-B1-PESS`).
2. E1's kP 1.24, an APC power-coefficient calibration against the maker table, on a chain that B1 already
   calibrates with `CL-B1-KCAL` 1.13.
3. The circuit factor.

Items 1 and 2 cover partly the same thing, prop optimism. Without the E1 factor, the pessimistic figure at 17 m/s is
366.4 × 1.05 = 384.7 W. That is still over 315 W, so the overlap does not change any conclusion. D-13 (carry the
conservative model) stands until the `T-B1-CRUISE-POWER` legs.

### 1.6 Fin trailing-edge bevel (aero option F1)

- **The fins carry most of the drag.** Fins and tubes are 0.0327 of CD0 0.0580, 56 % (`CALC-B1V-AERO`
  `cd0_parts`). The bare 9.6 mm board has square edges, and SU2 gives it cd 0.0364 on the 0.275 m chord
  (`b1sim.cfd.fin_b_cd`).
- **XFOIL check** (`xf_fin_edges_results.json`): 9.6 mm board, t/c 3.49 %, semicircular LE, transition at 2 % c,
  Re 3.2e5, α 0.

  | TE | cd | cdp |
  |---|---|---|
  | Blunt 9.6 mm base | 0.01783 | 0.00752 |
  | Straight bevel over the last 55 mm to 1 mm | 0.01341 | 0.00317 |
  | Bevel over 30 mm | 0.01363 | — |

  Δcd at the TE is **−0.0044**. It is applied to both the tube section and the bare board over the full fin height
  (2 × 0.21 m), × IF 1.15 → ΔCD0 −0.00319.
- **Round LE (F2).** It applies only to the 0.076 m per fin of bare board above the tube (`fins.half_height_m` 0.21,
  `fins.tube.length_m` 0.344). The Δcd of −0.0186 is a cross-tool difference, SU2 square board vs XFOIL round LE
  (`EST`), so F2 is shown but not recommended until SU2 checks it.
- **Limits.**
  - XFOIL's blunt-TE base drag is approximate.
  - The bevel case should be re-run in SU2 with `cfd_sections.py` (B1, wave 2) before the drag credit is booked.
  - **C4 must clear the bevel for stiffness first.** R-VIBRATION already fails (`CL-C4-SIM-SEP` 0.145 vs 0.20).
