- **Every B1 claim now runs at 17 m/s and 9° cant**, read from params. No B1 claim fails.
- **Cruise at the 2.6 kg gate is 278.2 W, or 368.2 W pessimistic** (`CL-B1V-CRUISE-P-GATE`, new `-P-GATE-PESS`).
  - These are within 0.1 % of wave 1's 278.1 / 368.1 W at 7° cant. The extra 2° of cant (+0.4 %) is offset by a
    0.5 % lower CD0 from the AVL/XFOIL re-run at 17 m/s.

- **R-MISSION is unchanged: still open on E2's count.** E2's `CALC-E2-BUDGET-V17` now gets a direct 17 m/s B1 level.
  By hand its pessimistic power is about 409 W (`EST`): 365.1 × 1.050 × 1.05 × 1.016, where 1.050 = (E1 286.2 + 6) /
  278.2, 1.05 is E2's circuit factor, and 1.016 is E2's mass scale from wave 1. E2 owns the figure.
- **One side effect for B2 and B3.** The AVL re-run uses A3's current CG, 0.0512 m (it was 0.0536). That moves
  `CL-B1V-CLMAX-SIM` from 0.862 to 0.838.
  - The governing CLmax is still B2's 0.783 (`CL-B1V-CLMAX`), so Vs does not move.
