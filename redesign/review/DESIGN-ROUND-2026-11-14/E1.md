## 1. `CL-E1-ILIM-I`: 30.0 A winding against 25 A at the 100 A limiter

### 1.1 Why a battery-current limit cannot hold a winding current on both bounds
- Winding current is set by prop torque alone: I_m = Q/Kt + I0 (`propulsion.motor_point`), and static Q is fixed by
  thrust.
- So "≤ 25 A in the winding" means "≤ a fixed static thrust per motor", whatever the bus voltage or the resistance
  bound. For the 11×5.5MR that thrust is **1 335 gf** (7 345 rpm), which is **T/W 2.039** at 7° cant.
- T/W 2.0 fresh needs 1 310 gf per motor, which is 24.56 A in the winding.
- The requirement therefore leaves a **2 % thrust band** with this prop. How much battery current buys that band
  depends on the bound: the second bound loses 0.208 Ω in the station, against 0.086 Ω on the first.

- `Q_M_BAT_CURR_MAX` **73** with the 11×5.5E / EP (11×5.5MR: **70**, valid only once `T-F1-SENSOR-CAL` shows the

- The value becomes **95** (MR: 93) if `T-E1-STAND` reads the second bound. The test is the existing one: 11×4.7 at

- **Limiter dynamics.** ArduPilot reduces max throttle "in 1 to 5 seconds" once the current exceeds the limit, and

  `T-E1-STAND` should log phase current through the ten punches it already includes.
- **Not needed:** a motor change. The E/EP prop opens the window at +24 g. A larger motor would cost more mass than
  that (—, not sized).
