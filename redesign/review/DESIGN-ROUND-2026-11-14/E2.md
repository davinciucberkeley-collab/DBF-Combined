# E2 battery / energy engineer: design round 2026-11-14, wave 1

**Verdict.** `CL-E2-CRUISE-TO-LOW-PESS-V17` fails worse than 4.68 min. The model credits the pessimistic
pack (92 % capacity) with its full 6 500 mAh in the `BATT_LOW` count. Corrected, the claim reads **3.61 min against
5.0 (−28 %)**. No 4S LiPo that fits the pod bay and the pack acceptance line closes it, even with the props changed.
The one option found that passes with margin is a **Li-ion 4S2P 21700 9 Ah pack (Molicel P45B class)**, at
**5.6–6.0 min `EST` (+12 … +20 %)**. Its cost is about +24 g and four checks by other roles (section 3). The pack
pass line of record is **the live `CL-A3-PACK-ACCEPT`, 564.4 g today, so ≤ 564 g**. The 558 g in the E2 text is
out of date.

Scope: analysis only. No shared file was edited. Evidence script: `team/E2/levers_2026-11-14.py`. It imports
`energy_claims.py` and calls its `budget()` with in-script overrides; it writes nothing. Paths are relative to
`redesign/`. `EST` marks estimates and "—" marks unknowns.

## 1. The fail, its cause, and the options

### 1.1 Baseline, reproduced
The script reproduces `CALC-E2-BUDGET-V17` exactly: 406.0 W, 29.28 A, `low_mah` 2 700, **4.676 min**, and 5.161 min to
the 20 % line (`team/E2/claims.json`).

to_low = (C_counter − LOW − Ah_before) / I_cruise × 60, with:
- Ah_before = 1.518 Ah (ground, climb, 2 min hover, 20 s forward transition, pessimistic);
- LOW = ceil(0.2 × 6.5 + need, 100 mAh);
- need = back-transition + descent + 60 s return cruise + 30 s landing hover = 1 383 mAh.

### 1.2 Finding 1: the counter base is wrong in the pessimistic case (model correction, makes it worse)
`failsafe_rows()` (`energy_claims.py` l.360–368) uses C_counter = nameplate 6.5 Ah. The pessimistic budget, however, gives
the pack 0.92 × 6.5 = **5.98 Ah** (`requirements.pack_capacity_min_frac`, R-PACK-MASS acceptance floor). The two
cannot both hold.

| How F4 sets `BATT_CAPACITY` | `BATT_LOW` fires after | Cruise to `BATT_LOW` | True charge at touchdown after a far-point QRTL |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 500 (as `K09L.param` l.273 now; the repo model) | 3.80 Ah used | 4.68 min | **797 mAh = 12.3 %**: the 20 % reserve is spent; `BATT_CRT_MAH` fires at 0.78 Ah true |
| measured capacity, 5 980 on the floor pack (as `team/E2/ENERGY.md` §4 tells F4) | 3.28 Ah used | **3.61 min** | 1 317 mAh = 20.3 % (reserve kept) |

So 4.68 min holds only by spending the reserve. The honest figure is **3.61 min**. The same correction moves
the 16 m/s claims, which pass today:
- `CL-E2-CRUISE-TO-LOW-PESS`: 5.97 → **4.67 min** (`EST`, −0.52 Ah / 23.93 A);
- `CL-E2-CRUISE-TO-LOW-PESS-240`: 5.45 → **4.21 min**.

R-MISSION therefore fails at 16 m/s as well, on the pessimistic pack.

### 1.3 Finding 2: which way `BATT_LOW_MAH` helps
- **A larger `BATT_LOW_MAH` shortens the cruise before it fires.** 2 600 → 2 700 costs 0.1 Ah / 29.28 A = **−0.20 min**.
  The V17 claim already uses 2 700. Setting 2 600 would read 4.88 min (or 3.82 min corrected), but 2 600 does not
  cover the 1 383 mAh landing need plus the 1 300 mAh reserve, which is 2 683 mAh. That would loosen the reserve,
  so it is rejected.
- **notes-A is right on safety.** If `AIRSPEED_CRUISE` is 17, `BATT_LOW_MAH` must be **2 700** for the 6.5 Ah pack.
  This costs the claim 0.2 min; it does not help it.
- Rounding up to 10 mAh instead of 100 (2 690) still covers the need and gains only 0.02 min, so it is not worth a change.
- **The voltage backup does not bind for LiPo.** `BATT_LOW_VOLT` 14.0 V is 3.50 V/cell, sag-compensated (`BATT_FS_VOLTSRC` 1),
  which is well under the 3.73 V rest voltage at 20 % (`ASM-E2-OCV-20`).

### 1.4 The reserve rule (requirement text)
`team/A2/requirements.json` R-MISSION:
- value: "hover >= {hover_time_min}, cruise >= {cruise_time_min}, reserve {energy_reserve_frac}";
- how: "2 min at hover power + 5 min at cruise power <= 0.8 x usable x pack energy … landing with >= 20 % remaining".

The text supports a reserve of 20 % of *usable* capacity. E2 models 20 % of *nameplate*, which is stricter. Taking the
text's reading gives LOW 2 600 and 3.82 min corrected (+0.2 min). It does not close the fail, and the RULES say not
to use a limit change to close one, so the nameplate basis stays. The 60 s return leg also stays: `FENCE_RADIUS`
250 m is about 15 s at 17 m/s, but the leg covers a 5 m/s headwind, the turn and the QRTL approach. SITL can
measure QRTL time from the far fence corner (`T-F4-SITL`); only that test can shorten the leg.

The pessimistic case keeps the 0.92 floor, cold, gate mass and 1.10 hover factor.

## 2. Recommended fix

Three parts. The first two are needed whatever is chosen.

**(a) Correct the model, then re-run.** In `team/E2/energy_claims.py`, `failsafe_rows()` takes `cap_frac` and the case.
The pessimistic `to_low` counts from 0.92 × C, so `BATT_CAPACITY` is the measured capacity (as `ENERGY.md` §4 already
says). Also add a claim: true charge at touchdown after a far-point QRTL ≥ reserve. E2 makes this edit in its own
script in wave 2, or now if A1 allows. Effect:
- `-V17`: 4.68 → 3.61 min;
- 16 m/s `CL-E2-CRUISE-TO-LOW-PESS`: 5.97 → 4.67 min;
- `-240`: 5.45 → 4.21 min.
