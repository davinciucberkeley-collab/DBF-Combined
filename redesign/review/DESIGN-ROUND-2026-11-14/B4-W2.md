## 3. `CALC-B4-SIM-INC` at 9°: both speeds (for B3)

Fixed hover rpm 5 985.5 (was 5 971.6; T_h / cos 9°), 11×5.5E geometry, NACA 4412, M = 12.

| Claim | 7°, 15.6 m/s (before) | **9°, 15.6 m/s** (`-V156`) | **9°, 17 m/s** (main claim now) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CL-B4-SIM-DH-DI` N/rad | 0.6329 | **0.6336** | **0.7251** |
| dH/di ÷ q (per Pa) | — | 0.004251 | 0.004097 (−3.6 %) |
| `CL-B4-SIM-H-MAX` N | 0.4296 | 0.4305 | 0.4768 |
| `CL-B4-SIM-HT-MAX` | 0.1297 | 0.1284 | 0.2599 |
| `CL-B4-SIM-H-HOVERT` N | 0.8832 | 0.8786 | 1.778 |
| `CL-B4-SIM-T-I45` | 0.5270 | 0.5286 | 0.4695 |
| `CL-B4-SIM-T-I90` | 1.0544 | 1.0541 | 1.0643 |

Reading the table:
- **The cant has no material effect** on these numbers (≤ 0.5 % at equal speed), as B3 expected.
- The speed does. At 17 m/s and hover rpm, the axial prop is near zero thrust: T at i = 0 is 5.5 % of static, and at i = 15° it is 19 %.
  - H/T therefore doubles: 0.13 → 0.26 at i = 15°.
  - `-H-HOVERT` = H/T × T_hover extrapolates from that small thrust (1.78 N). I would not use it as more than an
    optimistic bound at 17 m/s.
- On a per-q basis dH/di is 3.6 % lower at 17 m/s. Under B3's rule (the smaller nose-up term is the conservative one),
  the 17 m/s slope is the conservative choice.

**`CL-B4-ELEVON-HOVER-ALT`.** The claim asks: if the motor spin map were mirrored (every rotation reversed, cant
swapped), would the elevons stay out of the hover wash? At 9° the answer is no. The elevon tip enters the mixing layer by
8.6 mm. The cant break-even is about 7.2° (8°: −3.9 mm). The design sense (`ASM-B4-WAKE-SENSE` k = +1, D-52) keeps a
68 mm gap.
- A mirrored map also reverses the cant yaw (D-52), so the aircraft would not be flyable as configured anyway.
- `CR-B4-16`(b) already requires an inclinometer check on each mount and a hanging yaw test before the first hover, and
  that test detects this build.
- I do not loosen or retire the limit. The claim fails, and the hanging yaw test is what keeps that build from flying.
