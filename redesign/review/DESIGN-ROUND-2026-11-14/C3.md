# C3 pod structures: design round 2026-11-14, wave 1

**Verdict.** Both C3 fails on R-PACK-RETENTION were a stale model input. They were not a design fault. The model now
reads the forward-stop face from the pod geometry. Both claims pass with no limit changed:

- `CL-C3-BLOCK-A-SHARE`: 0.431 → **0.039** (≤ 0.10).
- `CL-C3-STOP-SECTION-PENALTY`: 6.17 → **0.56 mm** (≤ 1 mm).

The hatch tab and the block-top change do not break any C3 claim. One new finding goes to C5 and A1: the crush block
as drawn after D-143 is 9 % smaller in section than the area C5 uses. That decides whether the notes-P nose move is
enough (§4).

## 1. The fail and its cause

**Cause, confirmed against the CAD.**
- Before this round, `team/C3/pod_structures.py` `CALC-C3-NOSE-PATH` took the forward-stop aft face from
  `ASM-C3-D47-NOSE`, hard-coded at -0.1212 m. That is the D-47 face.
- D-62 moved the pod nose to `pod.x_le_m` -0.140 (`params/params.json`).
- The pod CAD draws the stop face as a sum of params (`subsystems/pod/fusion/pod.py` lines 127–130):
  - `x_fs = x_le + board t + nose_block_l_m + fwd_stop_m[0]`
  - = -0.140 + 0.0048 + 0.001 + 0.003 = **-0.1312**
  - The same value appears in `subsystems/pod/params.json` `nose_block_l_m` note, and in D2's `CL-D2-FWD-STOP-X`
    (`team/D2/claims.json`).
- With -0.1212 the model found L_A = (-0.1212 - 0.003) - (-0.1352) = 11.0 mm of "block A".
- The pod has no such block. Block A is a 1 mm epoxy line: `nose_block_l_m` 0.001, D-47 (XPS block deleted),
  CR-C3-08.

**notes-P re-checked.**
- L_A, the share and the penalty are as notes-P states.
- `team/C3/SUMMARY.md` shows 0.041. That figure came from the older pack range. The current 546/550/590 g packs give
  0.039.

**Correction made (C3-R5).** The edit is in `team/C3/pod_structures.py` only:
- `ASM-C3-D47-NOSE` is retired.
- `CALC-C3-NOSE-PATH` now takes `x_fs` = `CL-D2-FWD-STOP-X` and t_fs = `ASM-D2-FWD-STOP`[1]. Both come from
  `subsystems/pod/params.json` through D2. The block A length and the nose stack therefore follow any nose move.
- The 3 mm stop thickness is now read from params. It was C3's recommendation value.
- Limits are unchanged.

**Why the claims now pass physically.** The model credits the 1 mm epoxy line as if it were crushable XPS. That makes
0.039 and 0.56 mm upper bounds. C5 already gives the epoxy line no crush credit (`team/C5/landing_calc.py`, line 902),
so the two models agree.

**Re-run** `python -I redesign/team/C3/pod_structures.py`, 2026-11-14, values from `team/C3/claims.json` and
`team/C3/out/results.json`:

| `CL-C3-NOSE-BLOCK-A` | 11.0 mm | 1.0 mm | — |
| `CL-C3-NOSE-STACK` | 18.8 mm | 8.8 mm (matches D-47 / D-62) | — |

Every other C3 claim is unchanged.

**Options evaluated.**
- (a) Model correction, chosen. It is a corrected model with a stated reason, as RULES allows.
- (b) Full-section forward stop, CR-C5-13: +6.3 g (`CL-C3-POD-MASS-FULL-STOP` 151.4 g against 145.1 g). Not needed
  now that the penalty is 0.56 mm.
- (c) Raising the limits. Not allowed.

## 2. Other pod claims against the current pod

**Hatch tab, 8 × 60 mm (D-144).**
- `ASM-C3-HATCH` already reads `latch_tab_m` [0.008, 0.06] and hatch length 0.066 - (-0.140 + 0.0048 + 0.006) =
  0.1952 m.
- `CL-C3-HATCH-TAB` = 2.225 N/cm² against 2.5 (+11 %), at F_design 21.4 N.
- The allowable is still the `ASM-C3-HL` estimate. `T-D2-STRAP-PULL` and the spring-scale test on the hatch stay
  open.
- Passes.

**Crush block top, 3 mm under the hatch (D-143, `block_top_clear_m` 0.003, `pod.py` line 424).**
- No C3 strength claim reads the block top.
- C3 sizes the forces the block puts on the stop and nose wall from bay W × bay H = 2.959e-3 m² (`CL-D2-BAY-H`
  0.0569). That gives 509 N, which is conservative for the pod structure.
- The section as drawn is smaller (tray top to zh - 3 mm, less the 30 × 8 mm vent groove):
  - 0.052 × (0.0594 - 0.003) - 2.4e-4 = **2.693e-3 m²**, so F = 172 kPa × A = **463 N**.
  - D2's `CL-D2-BLOCK-SECTION` (2.849e-3) still runs the block to the hatch underside. It misses the D-143 3 mm.
- The block top at z 0.0772 still covers the pack fore face (pack top 0.0723) by 4.9 mm, so the spreader bears on the
  whole pack face.
- Above the 25 mm stop, the upper 31 mm of the block faces a 4 mm gap (stop 3 mm + bond line 1 mm) to the nose wall.
  The stop-section penalty claim covers this, at 0.56 mm.
- Passes for C3. It is a finding for C5 (§3).

**Pod-structures view.**
- The move is acceptable to C3. No pod claim fails, and the load path does not change: the nose wall, the T and the
  flange under the floor all move together.
- The tab position (x 0.066–0.074) and the D-143 grip clearance are unchanged.
- The cost is a longer overhang lever. It takes the thinnest pod margins down a few points (overhang crash +10 → +7 %,
  FEA fail-safe ≈ +3 %).
- **Hold the most forward pack centroid at -0.0382, not -0.0404.** If C5's block length is not raised with the move,
  the forward limit goes to -0.0404 and the overhang crash margin falls to +4 %.

**Caution for A1.**
- notes-P's +0.4 mm stack margin uses the 509 N section.
- At the drawn 463 N the 590 g pack needs 30.3 mm, so the 4 mm move leaves about **-2.3 mm** (38.3 stack vs 8.8 +
  30.3 + 1.5).
- The move closes `CL-C5-PACK-STACK-MARGIN` only if the block section is restored, for example with the §3 front
  notch (486 N, 28.9 mm). Even then the move is about 0.8 mm short (8.8 + 28.9 + 1.5 = 39.2 vs 38.3), so it needs
  about 5 mm of forward room, not 4.
- C5 owns this number.
