## 2. Props: APC 9x4.5MR (E1)

Pick: **APC 9x4.5MR (2 CW) + 9x4.5MRP (2 CCW) 4-pack with adapter rings, $9.40, in stock, 11.06 g each** (https://www.readymaderc.com/products/details/86771-apc-multirotor-9x4-5-2-blade-4-pack-with-adapter-rings, `V` 2026-11-14). Data: APC PER3 static file https://www.apcprop.com/files/PER3_9x45MR.dat (read 2026-11-14): CT 0.1155, CP 0.0443 at 7,000 rpm, 5.264 N and 53.8 W shaft. Same 4.5 in pitch as the 9545, 0.5 in less diameter. Vendor labels 2 standard + 2 reverse; the blade marking is checked before mounting. Hub fit on the MN2212 adapter is `—`.

Model (`prop_a.py`): Kv 780, Rm 0.173 ohm (T-Motor page, via SIZING-A), **I0 0.5 A `EST`**, pack R 30 mohm `EST`, sag = 3.73 V/cell rest. APC static data is not a measurement on this motor, and it predicts **+24 % thrust over the maker's own 9545B row (1,138 vs 918 gf)**: that looks too good, so the board's R3 0.93 derate is applied next to the table value and the bench decides.

|---|---|---|
| T/W fresh, 4 motors, 16.8 V rest (bus 15.2 V) | 2.94 | **2.74** (>= 2.0 PASS) |

`Q_M_SPIN_MAX 0.90` is now required, not just recommended: uncapped full duty equals the maker rating. Hover thrust was 326 gf only at 1.226 kg; at the gate it is 398 gf (382 at 1.44 kg). Yaw reaction torque at hover is about 59.7 N mm against the bench pass line of 55 (thin; `EST`). Cruise: same 4.5 in pitch, so the 161 W `EST` carries; a smaller disk may lower hover efficiency, folded into the table above, and cruise is untested.

Bench pass lines updated in `bench/bench_analysis.py` (single motor on the stand, 16.8 V, throttle taken as duty `EST`): fresh thrust 100 % >= 773 gf (gate) and >= 1,201 gf (0.93 x 1,292 model); sag >= 618 gf; hover 398 gf at <= 56.3 W; current at 100 % predicted 14.6 A (over 13 A by design, cap is the fix), at 90 % <= 13 A (predicted 11.2 A bus); rpm within 5 % of 10,792 at 100 %; CT band 0.10-0.125; D 9.0 in.
