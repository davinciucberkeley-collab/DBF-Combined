# Car fit (R-CAR-FIT) — measure two cars before freezing

Status `UNVERIFIED`: no car measured yet. The box (and the 1.15 m span) freezes only when two rows below pass.
Box to fit: **1.25 × 0.55 × 0.54 m** (CR-D7-01); A1's current 1.25 × 0.55 × 0.50 m is the fallback row.

## How to measure (tape measure, 10 min per car)

| Where | Measure | Pass if |
|---|---|---|
| Back seat, across | door-to-door width at seat-cushion height, doors closed | ≥ 1.27 m |
| Back seat, door | clear opening: width at the bottom, height at the narrowest point | both ≥ 0.57 m (box goes in end first) |
| Back seat, height | seat cushion to headliner at the centre seat | ≥ 0.56 m |
| Hatch / trunk | floor length (seats as used), width between wheel arches, opening height | one horizontal pair ≥ 1.27 × 0.57 m and height ≥ 0.56 m |
| Trunk lid opening | width × height of the opening | the 0.55 × 0.54 end passes, then the box slides in |

20 mm allowance on every dimension for handles and straps.
