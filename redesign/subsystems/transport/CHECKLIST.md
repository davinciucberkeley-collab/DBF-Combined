# Field set-up checklist (laminated card in the pouch)

One person. The only tool is the 10 mm prop spanner in the pouch (D-40). Target: closed box to arm check ≤ 5 min
(`CL-D7-SETUP-MIN` 4.4 min `EST`). Lift the aircraft only by the centre section, hands under the wing at the cradle
stations (y ±0.20). Never lift it by a tube, fin, brace, motor or foot.

## Set up

| # | Step | s | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Box on level ground, lid up. Undo 2 cam straps, lift the lid (pillars come with it), lay the ground mat | 20 | mat flat, upwind of the aircraft |
| 2 | Lift the aircraft out by the centre section; stand it nose-up on its four feet on the mat | 15 | all four feet down, no rock |
| 3 | Take the props, pitot boom and pouch from the tray | 15 | 4 flight props, colours match the motors |
| 4 | Fit 4 props: colour on the prop = colour on the motor (LH nut on the CW motors); 10 mm spanner, 1.5 N·m (firm wrist turn) | 140 | each prop turns freely, nut tight, spanner back in the pouch |
| 5 | Plug in the pitot boom (keyed), push the two tubes onto their barbed couplers | 20 | tubes not kinked, tip cover off |
| 6 | Take a pack from the LiPo bag, seat it at the trim mark, strap it | 40 | pack does not slide |
| 7 | Close the pack plug (AS150U); FC boots | 15 | tones, LEDs |
| — | **Start the arm check** (flight card, H2/H3) | — | — |

## Pack down (reverse)

1. Unplug the pack first and put it in the LiPo bag. Packs never go in the box.
2. Props off, each into its slot in the tray. Pitot boom off, tip cover on, into its slot (tip end against the PE plug).
3. Put the spanner back in the pouch, then the pouch in its pocket.
4. Set the aircraft upright on the cradles with the wing seated in both saddles and nothing touching the braces or tubes.
5. Lid on, close the 2 cam straps, carry the box upright. One person uses the side handle; two people use the end handles.
