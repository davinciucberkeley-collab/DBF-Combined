# Field set-up checklist (laminated card in the pouch)

Round 3, 2026-11-09. One person. The only tool is the 10 mm prop spanner in the pouch (D-40). Target: closed box to arm
check ≤ 5 min (`CL-D7-SETUP-MIN` 4.75 min `EST`, `T-D7-SETUP`). Lift the aircraft only by the centre section, with your
hands under the wing at the cradle stations (y ±0.20) or by the pod carry handle. Never lift it by a tube, fin, brace,
motor or foot. The joints and their checks are in `FIELD-JOINTS.md`.

## At the car (not timed)

| # | Step | Check |
|---|---|---|
| A | Carrier A takes the box (side handle) and the tote. Carrier B takes the battery can and the folded mat. Alone: the box on the first trip, the rest on the second | box carried flat, lid up |
| B | Set the box down flat, never on its end, and keep it out of the sun until set-up | — |

## Set up

| # | Step | s | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Undo the 2 cam straps and lift the lid (the pillars come with it). Unfold the mat on level ground, rubber up, upwind of the pilot | 30 | mat level within 5° (C5 S1) |
| 2 | Lift the aircraft out by the centre section and stand it nose-up on its four feet on the mat | 20 | all four feet down, no rock; four replace-mark grooves visible (C5 S7) |
| 3 | Take the props, pitot boom and pouch from the tray | 15 | 4 flight props whose colours match the motors |
| 4 | Fit the 4 props, colour on the prop to colour on the motor (LH nut on the CW motors). 10 mm spanner, 1.5 N·m (firm wrist turn) | 140 | each prop turns freely and its nut is tight; spanner back in the pouch |
| 5 | Plug in the pitot boom (keyed) and push the two tubes onto their barbed couplers | 20 | tubes not kinked; tip cover off and in the pouch |
| 6 | TX on | 5 | model K09L selected |
| 7 | Take a pack from the battery can, seat it at the trim mark and strap it | 40 | pack does not slide when the aircraft is tipped nose-down by hand |
| 8 | Close the pack plug (AS150U), then the hatch. The FC boots | 15 | tones, LEDs, hatch flush |
| — | **Start the arm check** (flight card, H2/H3) | — | — |

## Pack down (reverse)

1. Unplug the pack first and put it in its LiPo bag in the battery can. Packs never go in the box.
2. Take the props off and put each in its tray slot. Take the pitot boom off, fit the tip cover and put it in its slot
   (tip end against the PE plug).
3. Put the spanner back in the pouch and the pouch in its pocket.
4. Check the feet. Swap any insert past its replace mark now (3 min, `FIELD-JOINTS.md`).
5. Set the aircraft on the cradles with the wing seated in both saddles, the pod nose toward the nose pad and nothing
   touching the braces or tubes.
6. Put the lid on, close the 2 cam straps and fold the mat. Carry the box flat.
