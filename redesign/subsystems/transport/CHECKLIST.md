# Field set-up checklist (laminated card in the pouch)

One person, no tools except where marked. Target: closed box to arm check ≤ 5 min (`CL-D7-SETUP-MIN` 4.4 min `EST`).
Lift the aircraft only by the centre section, hands under the wing at the cradle stations; never by a tube, fin,
motor or foot.

## Set up

| # | Step | s | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Box on level ground, lid up. Undo 2 cam straps, lift the lid (pillars come with it), lay the ground mat | 20 | mat flat, upwind of the aircraft |
| 2 | Lift the aircraft out by the centre section; stand it nose-up on its four feet on the mat | 15 | all four feet down, no rock |
| 3 | Take the props, pitot boom and pouch from the tray | 15 | 4 flight props, marks match motors |
| 4 | Fit 4 props: colour on the prop = colour on the motor; nut on, tighten (wrench only if CR-D7-05 keeps a nut) | 140 | each prop turns freely, nut tight |
| 5 | Plug in the pitot boom, push the two tubes home | 20 | tubes not kinked |
| 6 | Pack from the LiPo bag: seat it, strap it | 40 | pack at the trim mark |
| 7 | Close the pack plug; FC boots | 15 | tones, LEDs |
| — | **Start the arm check** (flight card, H2/H3) | — | — |

## Pack down (reverse)

Unplug the pack first. Props off into their slots, pitot boom into its pocket, pack into the LiPo bag (never in the
box). Aircraft upright onto the cradles, lid on, 2 cam straps closed, box upright.
