# Landing feet (GER)

Status: `MODELLED` (D5, revision D5-R1, 2026-11-09). Answers CR-A1-20 / CHANGE-LOG D-11. Calc and claims:
`team/D5/feet_calc.py`; CAD: `fusion/landing.py`; parts and edges generated by `team/D5/make_interfaces.py`.

## Purpose

Four crushable feet on the fin trailing-edge tips. Each foot stands the aircraft, takes a 1.5 m/s hard landing at
n ≤ 6 without rebound, keeps the wing TE off the ground, and is repaired in the field by swapping one insert, no tools.

Each foot is a telescoping crush unit (stack along +x, which points at the ground in hover):

| Part | What | Life |
|---|---|---|
| `GER-001` shoe | PETG U-channel + sleeve bonded over the fin TE tip; back wall takes the crush load | permanent |
| `GER-002` piston | PETG cup sliding in the sleeve; two snap hooks in 35 mm windows; flange = stop at 32 mm | insert |
| `GER-003` cartridge | XPS F-250, 11 × 18 mm, two 38.5 mm segments, crush axis = board thickness | insert, consumable |
| `GER-004` pad | 1/16 in neoprene with adhesive back on the flange; outboard edge at z ±0.219 m | insert |

## Open issues

- Plateau 172 kPa is the datasheet 10 % strength: the coupon test sets the cartridge section (z height is the knob).
- Two-feet-first landing alone would need 54 mm; C5 checks it with the rotation (CR-D5-05).
- Worst CG corner tip-over is 30.05° (+0.2 %): any further forward/up CG shift fails R-TIPOVER (CR-D5-05).
- Splay grows the props-off envelope to 0.438 m (CR-D5-01); `DX-FOOT-*` still at z 0.190 (CR-D5-02).
- Mass margin 0.9 g: walls are 2 perimeters; do not add a third.
