# K09-L datum sheet

Status: `PROPOSED` (A5, 2026-11-09, params revision A1-R3, geometry R3). Every designer builds from these names, never from
typed numbers. The table in section 3 is generated by `python -I redesign/fusion/k09kit.py --datums-md` from
`params/params.json` and, for the R3 points the designers own, from their subsystem files: `DP-MOTOR-FACE`,
`DX-COLLAR-*`, `DX-ANCHOR-*` from `subsystems/fins/params.json#fin_detail` (D3), `DX-FOOT-*` from
`subsystems/landing/params.json#landing.pad` (D5). `check.py` warns when the table is stale against any of them.
A datum moves only when A1 changes `params.json` or the owning designer changes those keys (and A5 regenerates).

## 1. Frame

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | wing root leading edge, on the chord line, in the plane of symmetry | `params.json#frame.origin` |
| +x | aft, LE → TE (cruise drag direction) | `params.json#frame.x` |
| +y | right wing tip, seen from behind in cruise | `params.json#frame.y` |
| +z | up in cruise (toward the pod) | `params.json#frame.z` |
| Handedness · units | right-handed · metres in files and code; `fusionkit` hands Fusion cm | `fusion/README.md` |
| Fusion view | Preferences > General > Design > Default modeling orientation = **Z up** | — |
| Symmetry | `DP-SYM` (y = 0) left/right; `DP-CHORD` (z = 0) upper/lower motor pairs | `params.json#frame.symmetry_plane` |
| Part position | centre of its bounding box; motors by shaft axis at the mount face | `params.json#frame.station_convention` |

## 2. Attitudes

| Attitude | Transform from the frame | Ground | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cruise (model attitude) | none | — | all CAD, `interfaces.json` `cg_xyz_m`, analyses |
| Transport | none: the aircraft stays in the frame, props off, pitot boom out; the box is modelled around it, centre `DX-BOX` (`k09kit.box_frame()`) | box floor | `transport` module (flight: false), `check.py` transport fit |
| Hover / standing | rotate +90° about y (body +x → world −z, body +z → world +x), lift 0.368 m (TE 0.320 + foot 0.048, D-30) | `DP-GROUND` (x = TE + foot) on world z = 0 | renders, viewer, tip-over, ground keep-out |

`k09kit.hover_pose(params)` and `k09kit.to_hover(p, params)` give the transform. Never model a part in the hover
attitude. There is no tilt joint on K09-L: motors are fixed, the aircraft pitches as a whole.

## 3. Datums

Names: `DP-` plane (normal = the axis column), `DA-` axis (point + direction), `DX-` point. Motor stations
`UR UL LR LL`: U/L = +z/−z, R/L = +y/−y; counter-rotating pairs are diagonal (UR+LL, UL+LR). In code: `ctx.D('DP-SPAR')`
→ 0.080; `ctx.D('DX-HUB-UR')` → (−0.0082, 0.320, 0.190). In Fusion: component **00 Datums (DATUMS.md)**, grounded,
never edited by hand.

## 5. Rules

1. Read positions with `ctx.D(name)` or `ctx.value(key, unit)`; a literal that duplicates a datum fails review.
2. Build the right / upper-right part, then mirror: `k9.pair()` (through `DP-SYM`), `k9.quad()` (also `DP-CHORD`).
3. The elevon hinge is on the upper skin (`params.json#wing.elevon.hinge`): use `k9.hinge_point()`.
4. Nothing but the feet may reach `DP-GROUND`; nothing but a prop and its motor may enter a prop keep-out. The
   landing and propulsion modules register these; `check.py` enforces them.
5. Ground items (`transport`) are modelled in this frame at their transport position, around the aircraft;
   never move the aircraft to fit the box.
6. A new datum (a designer needs a shared station) is a change request to A5 with the `params.json` key it comes
   from; A5 adds it to `k09kit.datum_specs()` and regenerates this sheet.
