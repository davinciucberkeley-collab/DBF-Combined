## Risks the team must design out (detail: REFERENCES.md §2)

1. **FW yaw.** All four student Box Kites yawed uncommanded in FBWA; Plane has no FW yaw stabilisation by default. K09-L's fin centroid is 0.04 m aft of the CG with 40 % of fin area ahead of it, and four tractor props sit 0.21 m ahead (Ribner normal force). B3: Cn_β with margin; F4: name the FW yaw actuator.
2. **Tube resonance.** Bare-tube f₁ ≈ 181 Hz lands 6 % from the hover 2P; tube modulus unknown (50–140 GPa). C4: fin bond as the stiffener; H1: tap test, bend test.
3. **Trim vs static margin.** MH 45 (+0.0145) trims 2.7 % SM with neutral elevons at the cruise CL; the 5 % in `params.json` needs elevon up-trim, which costs CLmax and worsens CR-A1-05. B2/B3 choose.
4. **Disconnect.** XT60 fails the 120 A momentary case (60 A / 1 min). F1: AS150U or an output cap.
5. **Cruise prop efficiency.** APC data puts the 11×4.5MR at η 0.30–0.45 at 1 N / 15 m/s (REVIEW used 0.47): endurance 12–19 min is optimistic. B4/B1 re-run.
6. **No in-class precedent** for a logged elevon quad-tailsitter round trip (EVIDENCE §0 reconfirmed); the twin, the FW-first tune and the airspeed sensor remain the three conditions.
