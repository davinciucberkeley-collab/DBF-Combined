## Frame decisions

| Decision | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | x aft from the wing root LE, y right, z up (cruise); metres; symmetry XZ | matches `fusionkit` and `interfaces/SCHEMA.md`; CG reads as % chord |
| Subsystems (11) | `wing` D1 · `fins` D3 · `pod` D2 · `landing` D5 · `propulsion` E1 · `power` F1 · `avionics` F3 · `harness` F2 · `mechanisms` D6 · `transport` D7 · `flight-software` F4 | one owner per part; the fin + tube + mount is the boom problem, so D3/C4 keep it |
| Inactive | D4 (tail) | no tail; fins belong to `fins` |
| Spar | x = 0.080 m (26.7 % chord), CF 22 × 20 × 1 000 | inside the 30 mm section near maximum thickness |
| CG target | x = 0.060 m (20 % chord), range 0.045–0.075; z 0 (−0.02 … +0.03) | ~5 % static margin before AVL; B3 fixes |
| Motor stations | x −0.10 (mount), prop plane −0.15, y ±0.32, z ±0.19; ids `UR UL LR LL` | card geometry; F4 maps to ArduPilot motor numbers |
| Elevons | two panels per side (y 0.07–0.30, 0.34–0.56), 22 % chord, 17 % of area, one servo per side | tie-break asks 15–25 %; the fin root splits the TE |
| Mass | allocations 2 132 g + growth 350 g (A1) = 2 482 g sized; gate 2 600 g | skeptic-corrected build-up + elevons |
