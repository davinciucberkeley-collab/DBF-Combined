Main findings:
- Four of the documented student builds never completed a transition (FAMU tilt-tri, Purdue tailsitter, Santa Clara, UCI).
- Southampton dropped a tailsitter for a quadplane because the quadplane handled wind better.
- Both 2023 VFS-competition tailsitters crashed in wind, and one study puts landing tip-over at about 8.4 m/s.
- The 2023 winner (8-rotor lift+cruise) won by being rebuildable within hours.
- Forum reports blame tiltrotor failures on tilt-servo gears, wiring/parameter errors and transitions without an airspeed sensor (unverified).
- Costs: $782 (FAMU), $2,000 (Santa Clara), under $1,000 goal (Mozzie). No source gave build hours.

Implication: a modular separate-lift quadplane of about 2–3 kg, airspeed-gated transition, spares in budget.
