# Proposed requirements for detailed design — K09 quad tailsitter with elevons

Status: PROPOSED (study lead, 2026-11-09), pending the team lead's decisions in `README.md` §5.1. This is the set the
18-agent detailed design should work to if K09 is adopted. Values are skeptic-corrected (`cards/K09/REVIEW.md`) unless
marked. Tags: `ESTIMATE` = engineering guess, `ASSUMPTION` = team can overturn, `UNVERIFIED` = not checked at source.
Source column: C/D numbers refer to `requirements/REQUIREMENTS.md`; "TL" = team lead 2026-11-09; "REVIEW" = the K09
skeptic review; "CARD" = `cards/K09/CARD.md`.

| ID | Requirement | Value | Source / status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | Configuration | Quad tailsitter: rectangular flying wing, reflexed section, two fins at ±0.32 m each carrying a vertical CF tube with a motor at each end (4 motors in X, 2 above / 2 below the wing); stands on the fin tips; **two elevons**. No pusher, no tilt, no field structural joint. | README §4.1; CARD; REVIEW §8 |
| **P2** | Budget | Total spend ≤ **$2,000** incl. 10.25 % tax, shipping, packs, charger, radio, test gear, spares, consumables, transport box. Planned crash reserve ≥ **$150** at order time (current corrected plan ≈ $1,665 spend, ≈ $335 reserve). Outside the cap: FAA $5, AMA, Part 107 exam if ever needed. | TL hard; C1; REVIEW §6 |
| **P3** | Mission | Vertical take-off → hover ≥ 2 min total → transition at ≥ 1.3 Vs → cruise ≥ **5 min** → back-transition → vertical landing; 20 % energy reserve; no payload; 0.25 kg growth allowance in sizing; VLOS inside one RFS sub-field. The 10 min cruise is a stretch target, not a requirement. | TL (demonstrator); §3 M-A numbers `ASSUMPTION` |
| **P4** | Mass gate | Sized MTOW **2.48 kg** (2.43 corrected + 0.05 elevons); **hard as-built gate 2.6 kg** (+5 %). Per-subsystem allocations written before any CAD; every part has a status (`PROPOSED` / `MODELLED` / `CHECKED` / `VERIFIED`); no floating parts. | D14; REVIEW §2; advisor lessons |
| **P5** | Hover thrust-to-weight | ≥ **2.0** at the 2.6 kg gate on the maker's 14.8 V table (corrected plan 2.55); never below 1.6 with sag. | D11; REVIEW §3 |
| **P6** | Geometry envelope | Span ≤ **1.15 m**, chord 0.30 m, fin height 0.40 m, so the aircraft is one piece. May grow only if the measured car box (P9) allows. K11 biplane planform (0.80 m span) is the permitted alternative if the foam-board twin shows it needed. | CARD; SCREENING §4 |
| **P7** | Speeds | Stall ≤ **12 m/s** (CLmax 0.9 `ESTIMATE`, corrected Vs ≈ 11.2 m/s); transition ≥ 1.3 Vs (≈ 14.6 m/s); cruise ≈ 15 m/s. | D11; REVIEW §9 |
| **P8** | Wind | Hover and fin landing controllable in **5 m/s steady**; flight card limit **5 m/s**, morning flights at RFS. 8 m/s gust is a survive-and-land case (lean ≈ 36°, `Q_A_ANGLE_MAX` raised accordingly), not an operating case. | D11 `ESTIMATE`; REVIEW §5; flight-risk judge |
| **P9** | Transport | One foam-lined box ≈ **1.25 × 0.55 × 0.45 m external**, ≤ 6 kg, cradles under the spar (fins and motor tubes exposed); one person; field-ready in ≤ 5 min: 4 props, pack, AS150U/XT60 loop, arm check. Second box: charger, ammo can, tools. **M-item: measure two team members' cars before freezing P6/P9.** | TL hard; C3 (previous numbers `ASSUMPTION`); REVIEW §7 |
| **P10** | Build processes | Hot-wire XPS (F-250, 2 in) from printed templates; packing-tape skin; one 22 × 20 mm CF spar epoxied in a slot; foam-board fins (laser if Jacobs allows, knife otherwise); PETG FDM: motor mounts, tube-to-spar saddles, fin-tube clips/spacers, crushable feet, pod tray, elevon horns. No composites, no machining. | C4; CARD; REVIEW §2 |
| **P11** | Build hours and schedule | ≤ **90 team-hours** to first hover (plan 70–85) and ≤ 3 weeks from first cut; plus 30–40 h of flight-test sessions. Milestones: foam-board twin flown before the order; orders placed mid-Dec 2026 (CF tubes first, ≈ 2-week lead); first XPS hover by week 4 of build; first transition by week 7; complete by 2027-04-30 with one crash-repair loop (≥ 1.5 weeks calendar each, RFS 7-business-day request) inside the plan. | TL hard; REVIEW §8; build judge |
| **P12** | Crash repair | A crashed wing core re-cut and skinned in ≤ 1 day from stock on hand (one XPS sheet ≈ 6 cores); spare set in the BOM: 1 motor, 4 ESCs, 8 props, 1 CF tube, printed feet; the reserve (P2) covers one bad crash (≈ $309 incl. tax). | STUDY-BRIEF; cost.csv |
| **P13** | Propulsion stations | 4 × SunnySky X3108S KV900 + LANRC BLHeli_S 45 A + APC 11×4.5MR (CW/CCW pairs) on **4S**; hover ≈ 24 A total, full throttle ≈ 30 A per motor; thrust-stand one station before any flight (table is for 11×4.7, `UNVERIFIED` for 11×4.5MR). | CARD; REVIEW §3 |
| **P14** | Energy | 4S 6500 mAh LiPo (96 Wh), 80 % usable, 20 % mission reserve; ≥ 3 packs; weigh a pack on arrival (listings 503–680 g conflict). Corrected endurance: hover-only ≈ 13 min; cruise after 2 min hover ≈ 19 min (pessimistic 12). The 4S 4000 fallback misses 10 min in the pessimistic case. | CARD; REVIEW §2, §4 |
| **P15** | Flight controller and firmware | ArduPilot **Plane**, tailsitter: `Q_ENABLE 1`, `Q_TAILSIT_ENABLE 1` (elevons + motors; `Q_TAILSIT_MOTMX` all four), `Q_FRAME_CLASS 1`, `Q_FRAME_TYPE 1`, `Q_OPTIONS` bit 18 (arm only in VTOL mode). Motor-only `Q_TAILSIT_ENABLE 2` is an experiment, not the baseline. Board: a **stable** Plane target with tailsitter support confirmed on firmware.ardupilot.org before ordering (CoreWing F405 Wing V2 is beta/vendor-hex history; Matek/SpeedyBee fallback). Logging to SD or flash mandatory (campus policy). | D7; CARD; REVIEW §8; flight-risk judge finding 7 |
| **P16** | Controls content | Elevons: ≈ 0.22 × 0.05 m each, 2 micro servos, tape hinge, ≈ +0.05 kg, ≈ +$30. Airspeed sensor optional ($60) and recommended once transitions begin (gain scheduling). | REVIEW §8 |
| **P17** | Arming, kill, failsafes | Hardware/RC arm switch, full `ARMING_CHECK`, RC motor emergency stop ≤ 1 s; props off for all bench electrics. RC loss → QLAND/QRTL; battery low → return, critical → land; `Q_TRANS_FAIL` → QRTL; geofence = RFS sub-field + 400 ft ceiling; all tested on the bench before first flight; parameter names re-checked against the installed version. | D8, D10 |
| **P18** | Power disconnect and wiring | Physical anti-spark disconnect rated ≥ 1.25 × the peak flight current (hover 24 A; all-motors-full ≈ 120 A momentary: AS150U or XT60 class, decide and size wire for ≤ 10 °C rise `ESTIMATE`); four ESC leads into the pod; wiring treated as an installation (support, chafe, strain relief); the pack plug is the only field disconnect. | D5 (voided numbers), D9; advisor lesson 3 |
| **P19** | Thermal | Motors, ESCs and pack within rating for the full mission; report the field-ambient ceiling as an output (C0's 25 °C does not apply). | D13 |
| **P20** | Legal and site | < 25 kg; FAA registration ($5) and number on the airframe; § 44809 educational path, TRUST per pilot, AMA; fly only at RFS (FRIA: no RID module; keep the $40 module as a conditional line); UC Drone Program registration, PI sponsor, request ≥ 7 business days ahead, spotters, 6 a.m.–sunset, yellow sub-fields 1–4; check the FAA UAS facility map for RFS once (`UNVERIFIED` Class G/E). | D1–D6 |
| **P21** | Battery handling | Storage charge in a LiPo bag in the trunk; never charged unattended or in a car; sand bucket / extinguisher at hand; US-stock packs (ground shipping). | D12 |
| **P22** | Pilot readiness | ≥ 10 h SITL with the team's parameters and the foam-board twin (or a cheap quad) before the first XPS hover. | D15 |
| **P23** | Staged tests (gates in order) | (1) Foam-board twin on the real motors/FC/pack, ≈ $40, 2 days: tethered QHOVER within 1 m in calm air and a 5 m/s fan, logged lean vs 16° predicted; then QLOITER ≥ 30 m, transition ≥ 15 m/s, 2 laps, back-transition; pass = currents within ±15 % of 24 A hover / ≈ 13 A cruise. (2) One station on the thrust stand (T/W ≥ 2.0 at 2.6 kg). (3) XPS aircraft tethered hover; weigh-in against 2.6 kg. (4) Transition campaign at RFS. Fail at (1) on wind → test K11 planform, then fall back to K01. | CARD; REVIEW; README §4.3 |
| **P24** | Design method | One inventory drives BOM, Fusion model (`redesign/params/params.json` scripts), renders, build manual and viewer; joint-stack table for every fastened interface (fin-tube clips, tube-to-spar saddles, motor mounts, feet, pod tray); mass and CG reported against P4 by every subsystem; checks a student can rerun in seconds. Status words only: `PROPOSED`, `MODELLED`, `CHECKED`, `VERIFIED`. | Advisor lessons 1–7; mentor guidance; D13 |
| **P25** | Open items to close before the December order | Fin-tube joint design (not flat as carded); box cradles; stable FC build; pack stock and mass; one spare CF tube; car measurement (P9); Jacobs foam-board laser policy; Maker Pass coverage; RFS March–April wind records; static margin of the reflexed wing with elevons (CG target set before motor placement). | README §4.3, §5.1; build judge §6 |
