**A. Scaled quad tailsitter** (card K09; K09-L architecture at about 1.2 kg). 0.90 x 0.245 m foam wing, 2 fins, 4 x SunnySky X2212 KV980 (`EST` $30, 60 g), 9x4.7 props, 4 x 45 A ESC (LANRC 4-pack $30, `U`), 4S 2200 mAh (CNHL 2000 mAh 4S 100C $37.99, pyrodrone.com snippet, `U`). Mass 1,228 g, T/W 3.26, hover 163 W, 13 Wh mission against a 32.6 Wh pack, so a smaller pack could be checked later. ArduPilot `Q_TAILSIT_ENABLE=1`, 4-motor X (https://ardupilot.org/plane/docs/guide-tailsitter.html, snippet 2026-11-14). Technical risk Medium: hover of a flat wing in wind, pitch-over transition; thrust margin is large. Schedule Medium (scratch wing, 6-8 weeks `EST`).

**B. Small quadplane 4+1** (cards K01, K19; K01 scaled to 1.4 m). 4 x X2212 lift + 1 pusher, 2 CF booms, 4S 3000 mAh (CNHL 3000 mAh 4S 70C, 356 g listing, price `—`, `EST` $42). Mass 1,877 g. ArduPilot `Q_ENABLE=1`, speed-scheduled transition (https://ardupilot.org/plane/docs/quadplane-overview.html, snippet), the best-documented hobby VTOL (Mini Talon conversions, 300+ forum posts per MORPH.md B03). Technical risk Low-Medium: no pitch-over; risks are parked-prop drag, boom flex, pusher in transition. Schedule Low-Medium.

**C. Dual-motor tailsitter** (fixed motors, elevons; the tilting bicopter K16 was dropped, builder gave up). Cheapest and lightest but any motor-out is fatal and hover roll authority is differential thrust only. Risk High (K10 rejected on this basis); `Q_FRAME_CLASS` value `—`. Not recommended.

**D1 HEEWING T1-S VTOL PNP + F405 + GPS** (card B08). $393.49 RaceDayQuads (snippet 2026-11-09; NextFPV $360, DefianceRC $409.99), T1 conversion kit as spares $159.99 (fit to T1-S `U`). Listing flight weight 600-950 g, so T/W 1.64 at 895 g with a 4S 2200 is at the 1.6 limit. Official ArduPilot target HEEWING-F405 (ardupilot.org/plane/docs/common-heewingf405.html, per B08 review); V2 board logs to flash, no compass; forum reports of transition aborts. Ships assembled, EPP can be modified, but the team designs almost nothing: a capstone-scope question, not a cost one.

**D2 ZOHD Altus VTOL PNP.** $299.99, in stock, read at readymaderc.com/products/details/86192 today (`V`): airframe, 3 motors, 3 ESC, servos; no FC, RX or battery; flying weight 1,100-1,300 g. No maker ArduPilot parameter file found (B08). Credible commercial fallback.

**D3 HEEWING T2 Cruza 1.2 m PNP ($339.99 BuddyRC `U`) + VTOL conversion ($226 NextFPV `U`).** 3 motors, 2 tilt servos, maker flight weight 2,000-3,500 g, 6S. Margin only +$66 once its crash bill is counted: fails the $100 margin.

Screened, not costed: K19 flying-wing quadplane (B's cost class, harder structure); K06 scratch tilt-tri (earlier study: marginal T/W); U01 wing-assisted quad (no transition); K12 / K08 / K24 tilt variants (earlier study: mechanisms and failures documented).

## 4. Recommendation

1. **B, small quadplane 4+1**: lowest-risk concept that clears $100 margin (+$375; +$175 with candidate parts +30 %).
2. **A, scaled quad tailsitter**: the carry-over option (+$539, highest reuse of K09-L work), taken only if the bench test passes. A and B share motors, ESCs, 4S packs and the whole floor, so the first purchase is common; decide A vs B after the thrust test and the team's appetite for pitch-over tuning.
3. Fallback if both stall: D2 Altus (+$451), accepting low design content.

Next design steps: (a) bench-test X2212 KV980 + 9/10 in props on 4S (replaces the `EST` thrust, sets hover current and pack C-rate); (b) re-run `restudy_cost.py` with vendor-page prices (replace every `U`) and a real spares list; (c) check ArduPilot tailsitter / QuadPlane parameters, frame class and logging on the SpeedyBee target; (d) size wing area and 1.3 Vs transition on the racetrack; (e) CAD interference and assembly checks, landing and SITL sims on the chosen layout; (f) order floor items first, airframe parts by mid-December.
