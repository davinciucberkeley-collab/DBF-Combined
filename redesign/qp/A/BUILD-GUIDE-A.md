# Concept A build guide (scaled quad tailsitter)

Status `PROPOSED`, nothing built yet. Written for a team of four students at Jacobs Hall. It follows the 45 steps of `qp/A/cad/a_assembly_plan.py` in the order of the build film ("How it's built" in the Explorer), stage by stage. Each step says what you need, how to make the part, how to fit it, what to measure before you go on, the safety points and a time (`EST`). Every number is taken from a repo file (CAD parameters in `qp/A/cad/amodel/*.py`, `BOM-A.csv`, `MASS-GATE.md`, `review/QP-5/ARMS.md`, `review/QP-5/HARNESS-ENCLOSED.md`, `review/QP-4/FIXES2.md`) or is marked `EST`; `-` means the repo does not have it. This file is generated by `python -I redesign/qp/A/make_build_guide_a.py` from `qp/A/cad/a_build_steps.py` (the same script drives the cards of the film), so edit the script, not this file.

**Rule of the build: the CAD is the drawing.** Every part is made to the CAD shape and size; weigh it against the CAD mass in this guide (parts weigh within +-10-15 %, `EST`) and write the number in the build log. A part that does not fit its neighbour at the dry-fit is a CAD question: stop and ask, do not file it to fit.

**Safety, for the whole build:**

- LiPo: charge, store and carry the pack in the TBS LiPo bag (BOM W03); keep it in the ammo can (W04) when not in use; never charge unattended.

- Epoxy and CA: nitrile gloves, ventilation. No solvent glue and no ordinary CA on XPS (it melts the foam): epoxy or foam-safe CA only, test a drop on an offcut.
- Carbon dust: sand cut ends wet or with a mask and a vacuum; carbon dust is conductive, keep it off the electronics.
- Soldering only in the Jacobs Electronics Lab (studio 110) with extraction (SCHEDULE-A); heat-shrink with the lab heat gun.

### A1. Wing core on the bench

*Step 1 of 45. Plan caption: Wing core on the bench*

**Do this:** Hot-wire the S5010 core (245 x 900 mm), cut every pocket and groove, tape both skins.

**Time (EST):** 10 h 00 min hands-on.

**Parts (CAD id, BOM-A line, CAD mass):**

| CAD id | Part | Made / bought | BOM line | CAD mass (g) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `WNG-001` x1 | Wing core, XPS 25 kg/m3 + tape skin + glue + hinge tape | made | R01 Local | 100.1 |

Mass placed by this step 100.1 g; running total of placed parts 100.1 g (CAD; the harness lump HRN-001 and the hardware lump HRD-001 are not placed parts).

**You need:**

- XPS foam block (BOM R01); packing tape for the skin (R01 line, "tape, glue"); template material: 3 mm ply or MDF from the Jacobs laser cutter (EST)
- Hot-wire cutter and power supply: NOT in BOM-A and not a Jacobs tool (SCHEDULE-A B1: workspace W). Flagged, see Open items.
- Calipers, steel rule, 0.1 g scale (BOM G02)

**Tools:** hot-wire cutter, S5010 templates (laser), router/knife, 48 mm tape.

**Make it:**

- Template: the Selig S5010 coordinates are in `redesign/subsystems/wing/s5010.dat` (published, unscaled, 9.82 % t/c, D-193); scale to chord 0.245 m. Lower the airfoil datum 5.0 mm (D-194) so the 12 mm spar bore sits at mid-thickness: foam above / below the bore 6.3 / 5.6 mm. Cut two templates (root, tip: one section, so the same template both ends) on the Jacobs laser cutter. The template file is not generated for concept A: `-`.
- Practice first: hot-wire one scrap core (EST 1 h) to find the wire current and feed speed. Cut the blank 900 mm span x 245 mm chord in one pass per surface; sand the leading edge until no ridge can be felt.
- The core ends at the elevon hinge line: the cove foot is at x 179.25 mm, the hinge at x 183.75 mm (CAD `cove_x`, `XH`), so the elevon (stage 5) is the 61.25 mm aft strip, cut from the same blank in the same pass (EST).
- Shop cuts (mark from the CAD; every dimension is in `cad/amodel/wing.py` `build_wing`): spar bore 12 mm along y at x 60 mm, z 0 (the 12 mm tube is bonded in this bore); bridge slot open below x 0-56 mm, |y| <= 6.5 mm; pack tray slot x -10 to 22.5 mm, |y| <= 57 mm, open above; saddle slot x 29.5-71 mm, |y| <= 16.5 mm, open below; strut slots (0.5 mm clear); strut clamp slots y 270.5-309.5 mm; rib saddle slots |y| 349.5-410.5 mm; servo pockets x 124.5-165.5 mm, y 177.5-222.5 mm, open to the upper skin; elevon-tip damper notches at the elevon tip; balance-strip windows y 231-339 mm; phase-lead raceway grooves x 0-22.5 mm, |y| 14-24 mm and the open ditch x 22.5-32 mm; servo-lead drop slot at x 83 mm and a 5 x 3.2 mm groove 3 mm under the upper skin (D-198).
- Tape skin: both surfaces, the doublers and a tip-edge layer (CAD part WNG-001 lumps the foam, skin, glue and hinge tape: 100.1 g). Tape hinge and fillets are not drawn.

**Fit it:**

- Lay the finished core lower surface up on a flat bench, leading edge toward you. Nothing is fitted yet. Never lift a slotted core by its ends: lift with two hands under the root (K09-L SW-07 practice).

**Checkpoint (do not go on until it passes):**

- [ ] Template gauge at three stations: thickness within 0.3 mm of the S5010 (EST tolerance); log the real trailing-edge thickness.
- [ ] Dry-fit the 12 mm spar tube through the bore before taping: it must slide without force. The CAD bore equals the tube OD (12.0 mm); a real foam bore needs a small oversize for the epoxy (EST 0.2 mm): cut a test bore in a scrap first.
- [ ] Weigh the taped core: CAD 100.1 g (MASS-GATE wing group 191 g EST, D-185). Write the number down; every later step is compared with the CAD mass.

**Safety:**

- Hot wire fumes: extraction on, window open.

## Stage 2: Cage unit: motor leads threaded, motors, props

Film clip: `assets/animation/build/a-stage-02.mp4` (Explorer, "How it's built", stage 2). Steps B0, B1. Hands-on time EST 4 h 00 min.

### B0. Cage frame and motor leads (bench)

*Step 6 of 45. Plan caption: THREAD (bench, before the motors go on the cups): feed the three 20 AWG leads of each motor (400 mm stock + extension, EST) from the cup side through the 4.8 mm lead hole, the collar and the arm bore, out of the 4.8 mm hub socket floor hole; free ends hang out of the hub; then screw the motors on (B1)*

**Do this:** Make and bond the cage frame; once cured, feed each motor's 3 leads through cup, arm and hub.

**Time (EST):** 2 h 30 min hands-on.  **Cure / wait:** 24 h epoxy cure (K09-L practice) of the frame bonds before the leads are threaded.

**Parts:** none placed (marker step: it lays wires or threads leads). Wires used: see the checks below and `review/QP-5/HARNESS-ENCLOSED.md`.

**You need:**

- 4 x 12x10 arm tubes + 1 bridge (3 tubes of BOM R08 cover spar, arms, bridge); 2 x 6x4.5 struts (R10); PETG (R03); 20 AWG silicone wire (W07) for the extensions; heat-shrink (W05); fish wire or a long thread

**Tools:** tube cutter, printer (PETG), fish wire / thread, 20 AWG wire.

**Make it:**

- Arms: cut 4 x 245 mm from the 12x10 tube (CAD arm 12x10 x 0.245, 13.5 g each, from CAD `R_M - R_IN`). Bridge: cut 154 mm (CAD, 8.5 g). Cut both bridge and arms square; sand the ends.
- Struts: cut 2 x 286.2 mm from the 6x4.5 tube (CAD length from `STRUT_S` to `STRUT_E`; 5.7 g each at that length, the CAD part mass is 6.5 g EST). The BOM R10 text says 0.66 m total: 2 x 286.2 = 572 mm fits.
- Dry-fit first (no glue): arm into the hub socket (25 mm deep) and into the cup sleeve (25 mm); bridge into the hub sleeve and the saddle; struts into hub and clamps. Anything that needs force is wrong: re-sand.
- Epoxy the 4 arms into the hub sockets and the cups (each motor axis is canted 17 deg (D-189): check the cant against the CAD drawing with a protractor on the cup plate); epoxy the bridge into the hub and the bridge saddle CGE-006; epoxy the struts into the hub and the two clamps CGE-007-R/L.
- Print (PETG, 0.2 mm, machine time EST at 14-24 g/h): hub CGE-001 (17.5 g: 2.0 mm walls over the outer 12 mm of each arm socket, 1.2 mm bridge sleeve, 0.8 mm webs, D-196) about 1 h; 4 motor cups CGE-003 (4.3 g each, plate flat on the bed with the shaft axis up, from ARMS.md) about 15 min each; strut clamps CGE-007-R/L (1.5 g), bridge saddle CGE-006 (2.0 g), tray CGE-008 (3.8 g). File names: <part id>.stl (export pending: `-`). Orientation of the hub: `-` not in the repo; EST sleeve axis vertical, aft face (tray pad) on the bed, supports under the socket bosses.
- Phase-lead extensions: each MN2212 has 400 mm of 20 AWG lead (T-Motor page) but the route to the ESC pads is 0.456 m (UR, UL) or 0.470 m (LR, LL) (wire_inventory.json), so the stock lead is SHORT: splice an extension of about 0.10-0.12 m onto each of the 12 strands (BOM W07; 30 mm splice overlap, heat-shrink; the CAD lumps the splices in HRN-001). Put the splices outside the arm bore (EST).

**Fit it:**

- After the 24 h cure: THREAD, on the bench, before the motors go on the cups: push the three leads of each motor from the cup side through the 4.8 mm lead hole, the collar and the arm bore (264 mm inside the arm), out of the 4.8 mm hole in the hub socket floor. Keep the bend radius at the collar and at the hub exit at 4 mm or more (CAD bundle bend, HARNESS-ENCLOSED D-198). Leave the free ends hanging out of the hub; leave the motor on the leads (unscrewed) until B1.
- Use a fish wire pulled through the arm and hub: tape the three lead ends to its tip. Pull slowly; the bundle fills 9.7 % of the arm bore (HARNESS-ENCLOSED s2).

**Checkpoint (do not go on until it passes):**

- [ ] Pull test: the bundle slides freely both ways with two fingers. A tight bundle in the 4 mm bend is a stop: re-lay the bundle, do not force it.
- [ ] Measure the free length out of the hub: the route minus the 0.264 m inside the arm is 0.19 m (UR, UL) or 0.21 m (LR, LL) still to run to the ESC pads (CALC from wire_inventory.json); each bundle must reach it with the extension.

## 14. After the last step

1. **Weigh-in.** Weigh the aircraft with the pack. The gate is **1.47 kg** (`MASS-GATE.md` s8, D-190); the CAD basic mass is 1262.6 g (placed parts 1218.2 g plus the harness lump 18.4 g and the hardware lump 26.1 g) and the design target is 1.46 kg predicted including growth and overrun (`params-A.json`). If the weight is above the gate the aircraft does not fly until the C3 window check is re-run at the as-weighed mass (`MASS-GATE.md` s6).
2. **CG.** Balance the aircraft on a knife edge in the cruise attitude: the band is **19-26 mm aft of the wing leading edge**, hard aft limit 28.5 mm, lateral within 5 mm (`params-A.json`, A-P4). Move the pack in the tray slots (6 mm forward, 10 mm aft of nominal, about 2.2 mm of CG per 10 mm, `common.py` `TRIM`): no ballast first.
3. **Controls.** Each elevon +-20 deg (servo limit), stops at +-21 deg; both surfaces move the right way for the stick (EST: check with the TX, props off).
4. **Power-up, props OFF.** First power-up with the props removed. Check each motor direction: UR and LL clockwise, UL and LR counter-clockwise, seen from the thrust side (BOM P02, MR and MRP); if one is wrong swap two phase leads (EST). Then fit the props, props-on tests only on the thrust stand (`bench_analysis.py`, `SCHEDULE-A.md` B3).
5. **Tap test.** Tap each arm: first arm mode 32-50 Hz, four arms within 8 % of each other (`ARMS.md` s5). Below 32 Hz stop: the socket or the bond is soft.
6. **Flight card and range check.** Not covered by this guide (`team/H3/FLIGHT-CARD.md` is the K09-L card: `-` for concept A).
