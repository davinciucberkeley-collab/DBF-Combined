**Why pitch fails (decomposition, base group = nominal cell, 6061, 50 A pad, FE inertia, `t089_sens_stdout.txt`):** pitch minimum at nominal modes: iter2 plant + iter2 FE 4.19 / 4.25 / 5.88; iter2 plant + T-085 FE 1.91 / 1.77 / 1.64; X2216 plant + iter2 FE 3.13 / 3.17 / 3.76; X2216 plant + T-085 FE 1.22 / 1.13 / 1.03. Both changes erode it (the plant by the 1.56 x thrust, the structure by the new first symmetric mode). **The limiting closed-loop pole is at the first symmetric wing-flap / cage mode, 26.8 / 27.8 / 29.7 Hz (kd / nom / hi; pitch gain 0.225-0.246 of the rigid gain), not at the 94-109 Hz mode** (limiting pole 27-33 Hz over the modes x 1.0-1.2, `t089_sens_stdout.txt`). The 100.1 / 102.9 / 108.7 Hz mode does couple strongly to pitch (gain -1.28 / -1.32 / -1.37 of rigid, `t089_modegain_stdout.txt`, as does a 42-46 Hz mode, -0.41 to -0.47), but the loop is attenuated by the motor lag (40 ms), the gyro LPF (60 Hz) and the delay by then, so it does not reach the gain-margin boundary first; with a slower motor or ESC lag than the 40 ms EST it could. Yaw is limited by a 2.7 Hz pole (rigid-body; 2.19 min). A 113 Hz notch is useless for it (pitch 1.0, roll 2.26, yaw 2.09-2.13 with it).
**Illustrative sensitivity (not a design change, not credited; CAD-inertia basis, `t089_sens.py`):** scaling every pitch rate gain by 0.7 / 0.5 / 0.4 gives GM 1.49 / 2.09 / 2.60 over all groups and modes -20..+20 %; rigid crossover falls from 1.9 Hz to 1.4 / 1.05 / 0.86 Hz (PM 56 -> 58 / 58 / 57 deg). So roughly half the D-191 pitch gains would restore 2.0 on this model at the cost of a slower pitch loop (crossover 1.05 Hz); roll and yaw need no change (roll 2.44, yaw 2.13 at gain x 1.0).
Ladder (docs/engineering-decisions.md): no rung applied here (analysis only). Candidate rungs, in order: 1 subtract: none; 2 modify: pitch P / D at about half or a mass-stiffness change moving the 27-30 Hz mode up (T-085 levers C1 / C5 raise the cage modes); 3 rearrange: FC off the spar centre is not credited (the node choice does not remove the pole); 4 mechanism: a mode isolator or a tuned notch at 27-30 Hz in the pitch loop (not modelled); 5 add material: stiffen the bridge / spar. The tap test T-A-TAP (TP-10b) gives the built frequency and damping before a retune.

- **Mechanism:** the governing mode (91.5-108.7 Hz over all groups, 94.6-108.7 Hz in the credible bounds; cx 0.74-0.85, cy 1.12-1.35) has its 1P resonance at 60 f = 5,490-6,522 rpm. The lowest steady operating rpm is 6,789 (hover derate 1.0, 1.8796 kg, `hover_1p_rpm_lo`), so it is not met at a steady state, but its resonance sits at or just beyond the 6,000 rpm sweep edge: at zeta 0.5 % the governing mode alone peaks at 151-180 m/s2 (`gov_peak`), 76-91 at 1 %. The sweep worst is at the lower flank (rpm < 6,700) in 57 of the 144 rows; the rest peak at 11,400 rpm (the old spar-only worst). 2P of the mode needs 3,000 rpm: outside.
- **Margin vs the limit 30 m/s2 (unchanged, `req.vibe_max_ms2`, A-P10(b)):** sweep worst -524 % (187.2 vs 30). Operating window 6,789-8,028 rpm: worst 32.9 (hi, 0.5 %; -10 %); at 1 % damping kd and nom pass (19.3 / 21.8, +36 % / +27 %) and hi is 32.4 (-8 %).
- **Motors:** worst 193-257 m/s2 summed (53-87 per axis bound). No motor vibration limit is sourced ("—"); the X2216 / ESC data sheets give none and A-P10(b) is an FC limit. The motor value is a bound on the arm-tip acceleration at 1P resonance, reported for the TP-10b / T-A-TETHER-VIBE record.
- **Model limits:** beam FE only (no shell, no solid; the hub joint, the pad and the wing cell are the T-085 bounded inputs, EST); steady-state response at fixed rpm (the real spool-up passes through 5,676-6,522 rpm in well under a second, so the transient peak is lower than the steady value, not computed, "—"); zeta is a guess, not a measurement (tap test T-A-TAP measures it); all modes added in absolute value and the four rotors in the worst phase (conservative; the real prop unbalance after balancing may be below 1.0 g mm, "—"); the FC filter chain (INS_GYRO_FILTER 60, harmonic notch INS_HNTCH 105 Hz) is not applied to the accelerometer VIBE reading here. These errors are conservative except zeta 0.5-1 % (if the real damping is below 0.5 % the response is higher).

## 4. What the two results mean together
The GM failure is limited by the 27-30 Hz symmetric mode (the 95-109 Hz mode also couples strongly to pitch but is attenuated by the loop lag), the vibration failure by the 95-109 Hz mode; both are properties of the T-085 beam model (wing flap with the cage bouncing on the bridge and struts), and D-236 already assigns the 95-109 Hz mode to the built-airframe tap test. The same test (T-A-TAP, TP-10b, $0, before the first tethered hover) measures both frequencies and the damping; the first tethered hover has the VIBE check (A-P10(d)) and the rate-gain scale (the Increase-gain test in `explain_a.py` CL-A-VIB-GM, "real"). The pitch gains in A.param are NOT changed here (D-191 stays; a retune is a design decision for the owner after the tap test).
