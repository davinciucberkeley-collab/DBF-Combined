## 2. Deviations from the decisions file (each one is mine, with the reason)

1. **Lane position.** The decisions put the I2C drop at y about -23.5 mm. The -y lower-left phase bundle (strand outer edge 22.94 mm) and the cover wall inner face (25.9 mm) leave 2.96 mm; a 2.0 mm bundle centred at y -24.42 leaves 0.48 mm to each. I used -24.42 mm for both leads. Drop slot y -26.1..-22.7 mm is still inside the left plate window (|y| 21..40).
2. **Heights.** The servo-lead ribbon drops through the window at x 83, y -24 and would meet any lead at plate level. I2C runs at z 22 mm (over the ribbon top at 19.7 mm) and drops at x 95; UART runs at z 25 mm to x 98 (3 mm east of the I2C drop column) and then down to 19.5 mm. Centre-line gaps (envelope): UART to I2C 1.03 mm, both to the phase bundle 0.5 mm (my own paper/Python check, then the collision gate).
3. **Antenna length.** The 65 mm is the 3-D length including the z step, not the plan length: the straight run was solved so that the polyline is 65.00 mm. Tips: x 169.4 mm, y -92.2 and +0.4 mm: 14.4 mm ahead of the hinge (XH 183.75), 9.4 mm more than the 5 mm required margin.
4. **Plate-end step.** The plate end (x 127.03) is a 5.74 mm step down to the skin (zu 8.3 mm). A real coax cannot wrap that corner at 4 mm radius. The step (cosine, 8.5 mm long in x) starts where the plan turn ends (x 128.3 mm) so the combined bend radius stays 4.0 mm (minimum of the sampled antenna path 4.00 mm). Consequence: about 11 mm of each antenna (x 127.3 to 135.4 mm) is not within 0.2 mm of a surface; the maximum gap to the plate or skin is 5.7 mm. The acceptance line "within 0.2 mm of the skin along its whole length" is therefore met everywhere except this bridge. In the build it is taped across the step. Coax minimum bend radius: not published ("-").
5. **Hood roof height and slots** are design values (EST) as the decision; antenna slot 4.0 x 3.5 mm.
