## 2. Where the 14 N came from, and whether it is sound (task item 1)
- K09-L: `requirements.foot_side_load_cap_n` = **24.5 N** (D-105, D-138 in `redesign/params/CHANGE-LOG.md` l.764, l.824): it is a **design value set equal to K09-L's own 6-DOF result** (24.28 N at mu 0.40, `CL-C5-SIM3D-SIDE-12`, `redesign/review/CLOSURE-ROUND-2026-11-14/C5.md` l.191), "the integrator accepts 24.5 N". The structure was then designed to it (shoe FEA side capacity 24.96 N, `CL-C5-FEA-SIDE-CAPACITY-D5R6`, same file l.277). It was never a capacity of a part; for K09-L it was a demand. The same file l.141-142: "side load stays 24.1 N ... at every mass. It is set by the plateau and mu, not by mass."
- A: `structures_run.md` s5 (and `structures_a.py` l.220) scaled 24.5 N by mass (24.5 x 1.5 / 2.6 = 14.1 N). **Not sound:** (a) K09-L itself states the load does not scale with mass; (b) the 24.5 N belonged to K09-L's cartridge shoe, which A does not have (A's foot is a PETG shoe over a bonded EVA/PE pad, rib tube behind it); (c) a scaled demand is not a capacity. The 14 N was therefore compared with nothing real.
- What fails in A: nothing yet; the question is which A part has the least capacity. Computed in section 3.

## 4. Inputs sourced (task item 2)
- Touchdown lateral speed. ArduPilot does **not** enforce a lateral touchdown limit: `redesign/qp/A/A.param` sets only vertical rates (`Q_LAND_FINAL_SPD,0.3` l.118, `Q_LAND_FINAL_ALT,6` l.119, `Q_WP_SPD_DN,1.2` l.116 above the final 6 m, `Q_PILOT_SPD_DN,1.0`); there is no Q_ parameter that caps horizontal speed at touchdown. So 0.5 m/s drift is an operational limit and a SITL result, not a controller limit. Evidence: 29 stored SITL touchdown records (`redesign/qp/A/sitl/runs/*/sum.json`, field `landing`): lateral speed max **0.52 m/s** (Z5_gb45 0.50, Lb_5e 0.45, Lb_3b 0.40), sink at touch max 0.29 m/s, peak sink in the last second max **0.34 m/s**, none tipped over. The sim's 0.5 m/s drift matches the SITL maximum (0.52: 4 % above); the sim's 1.2 m/s sink is the hard-landing design case and is 3.5 x the controlled rate, so the 18.7 N combines a hard sink with the SITL drift maximum and a 10 deg attitude error.
- Attitude error 10 deg (K09-L `landing_attitude_err_deg`, carried). SITL lean at touch: belly-on to 3 m/s 10.3-11.3 deg (Ta_h3, Tb_h3); edge-on to 5 m/s 5.0-12.1 deg; so 10 deg is consistent with the flight-card wind rule. **Exception:** Z5_b0 (belly-on at 5 m/s, which the card forbids: belly-on only to 3 m/s) touched at **29.1 deg**.
- Pad friction. mu 0.40 is K09-L's `pad_mu_max` (D-105: pad face = UHMW-PE tape over neoprene, "the test decides"; JVCC UHMW-PE-15 $4.91, findtape.com, checked 2026-11-14 per `redesign/review/DESIGN-ROUND-2026-11-14/C5.md` l.167). **A has no pad-face tape**: A's drawn pad is bare EVA/PE foam (BOM has no foam or tape line; build step C9). The previous "mu <= 0.3 pad (EST)" mitigation therefore has no part behind it in A. mu for A is "-" (not measured).
- Sensitivity to mu (`landing_side_a.py` s2, rib-root RF in the worst case, 7/7 combo): mu 0.30: 1.54; 0.40: 1.26; 0.50: 1.01; 0.60: 0.90; 0.70: 0.79. Interpolated RF = 1 at **mu 0.51**. Side load at mu 0.50: 21-23 N, at 0.70: 28-31 N, i.e. above the 24.0 N torsion-only capacity too. So the design depends on the pad face being no more slippery-resistant than about 0.5; bare foam on dry asphalt/concrete may be higher (unmeasured, "-").
- Foot geometry (rung 2): plateau sweep at mu 0.40 (`landing_side_a.py` s3): 24 N: stroke 18.9 mm of 20, n 8.9, side 15.6 N, rib RF 1.52; 27 N: stroke 17.0, n 9.85, side 17.2, RF 1.38; 30 N: stroke 15.4, n 10.79, side 18.7, RF 1.26. A lower plateau buys 0.26 RF for 3.5 mm of the 4.6 mm stroke margin; not needed.

## 7. Decision (failure-resolution ladder) and trade rows (proposed CHANGE-LOG entry D-213, number to be assigned)
Failure: side load 18.7 N against a stated 14 N cap (FAIL as stated). Ladder, in order:
1. Subtract: the only removable items are the cases (hard sink + 10 deg + 0.5 m/s drift) or the pad foam; dropping the case loosens the requirement, removing the pad removes the stroke that holds n < 12 g. Not done.
2. Modify geometry: lower plateau (pad area) and foot position were evaluated (rows B, C); neither is needed once the true capacity is used. Foot z can only move to 0.160 m before static tip-over falls to 30 deg (0.277 x tan 30), which cuts the torsion lever by 11 % and leaves 0 margin: rejected.
3. Rearrange: none identified (rib and foot are the only load path; the rib lies on the fin centreline).
4. Change approach: **adopted**, replace the unfounded cap with the computed capacity of the limiting part (rib root combined load) and move the real uncertainty to two stated, testable inputs (the shear allowable and the pad friction). The operational limit on touchdown drift is kept at the existing 0.5 m/s as a flight-card item with SITL evidence, not as a controller limit.
5. Add material/parts: not needed at the calculated margin. Held as the named fallback: UHMW-PE tape pad face (about 0.2 g on four pads, about $4.91 per K09-L price record), triggered if the drag test gives mu > 0.40; stronger rib not needed.

- **Category:** rigid-body dynamics (explicit 6-DOF, 4 elasto-plastic foot springs, Coulomb friction) plus hand-calculation part capacities (tube beam/torsion); no FEA.
- **Solver / engine:** `redesign/qp/A/structures/landing_a.py` (`python -I landing_a.py 30`, rib-root check added), `landing_side_a.py`, `landing_sitl_env_a.py`; Python 3.14, numpy; dt 1e-4 s, T 1.5 s.
- **Model version:** git `7d94485d` plus the uncommitted tree of 2026-11-19; `landing_a.py` ITEMS with motor assemblies 4 x 88.26 g (e29526c3).
- **Inputs:** as AR-008; rib 14x12 CF tube (CAD `wing.py` RIB_OD/ID, RIB_X0 0.0705), foot arm 0.18 m (z) and 0.2295 m (x), KF 1.725; allowables 30 MPa shear and 350 MPa bending (project EST, no coupon); shoe bond 0.1 MPa (C6 EST); pad mu 0.40 (assumed, A pad unmeasured); SITL touchdown records (29 runs): lateral <= 0.52 m/s, peak sink <= 0.34 m/s, lean 0-29.1 deg.
- **Assumptions:** fin and rib rigid in the dynamics; fin load carried to the rib end and applied at the saddle (conservative arm); both feet of one rib summed each step; foot normal and side peaks simultaneous for the bond and bearing checks; side load fully along the span axis for torsion (worst).
- **Boundary conditions / loads:** as AR-008 (10 cases); additional sweeps mu 0.30-0.70 (4 cases), plateau 24/27/30 N, six SITL-record cases (narrow and wide, both signs).
- **Numerical settings:** as AR-008; 2 s per case.
- **Outputs:** `redesign/qp/A/mechanism/out/T064_landing_side.md`, `T064_data/*` (landing_30_before/after, landing_side_a_out, sitl_record_env_out, lean_scan_out, structures_run_full_regen).
- **Acceptance criteria:** side load per foot <= the computed capacity of the limiting part (rib-root combined RF >= 1.0 at KF 1.725; project 0.15 margin rule would want RF >= 1.15) at pad mu <= 0.40; the other four AR-008 criteria unchanged.
- **Result:** side 18.7 N at mu 0.40 (worst). Rib-root RF minimum 1.26 (hard 7/7 combo), shoe bond 1.38, foam bearing 2.26, fin skin 14: PASS at EST allowables. The scaled "14 N cap" is withdrawn (it was K09-L's design demand scaled by mass, not a capacity). RF = 1 at mu about 0.51; 21-23 N side at mu 0.50. Dynamic tip-over appears at narrow-axis lean >= 28 deg with 0.16 m/s lateral speed (SITL Z5_b0, belly-on 5 m/s, card-prohibited).
- **Limits / validity:** both CF allowables are EST until a tube twist coupon (the same test closes the bridge 30 MPa EST); pad mu unmeasured; foam plateau unmeasured; rigid-body with no rib twist (7 deg, foot moves 24 mm at 18.7 N one-foot) or fin flexibility; no controller or thrust during touch; sim and SITL disagree at 29 deg lean.
