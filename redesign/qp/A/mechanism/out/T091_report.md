## 0. Outcome
- **No structural change is worth buying now.** No candidate clears D-185 (strong 95-109 Hz mode in band in 57-69 of 72 corner cases for every candidate, baseline 65) and none brings the 6,000-11,415 rpm sweep forced response under 30 m/s2 (the best is 163 m/s2, baseline 187). The 94.6-108.7 Hz problem stays with the T-A-TAP tap test (D-236).
- **The pitch GM has a zero-mass, zero-cost fix in the model: put the FC 75 mm off the wing centre along the spar (IMU node s+0.075).** Over all 24 T-085 bound groups x kd / nom / hi: pitch GM **2.81 / 4.00 / 2.52** (modes x 1.0 / 0.8 / 1.2; baseline at the centre 1.13 / 1.61 / 1.05), roll 2.52-3.38, yaw 2.13-2.48 (baseline 2.44-3.38 / 2.13-2.48): every axis >= 2.0, limit unchanged. At 150 mm: pitch 4.59-5.97. The baseline reproduces T-089 exactly (1.13 / 1.05 / 2.44 / 2.13) from the same scripts. T-089 s2 and the D-237 text said the node choice "does not remove the pole": that was for the cage hub node (which is worse, GM 1.0); the spar stations were not scanned. This corrects it.
- Cause (FEA-lite): the limiting pole is the first symmetric wing-flap / cage mode (26.6-29.6 Hz; spar 53 %, hub joint 15-19 %); the IMU at the spar centre sees it at 0.22-0.25 of the rigid pitch gain, at +75 mm at about 0.1 or less (`nodes_b0.json`). The centre is where the cage's pitch moment enters the spar; the rate gyro reads that local rotation.
- **A stiffer centre spar (CF 14x12 sleeve) also removes it but not robustly**: nominal group GM 3.3-4.2 (150 mm sleeve) but over all 24 groups (lower wing cell) 1.69 / 2.13 / 1.69, still below 2.0.
- Not recommended: struts 12x10 (GM only 1.45 over all groups; moves the cage mode so the hover-window forced response goes to 89-121 m/s2), bridge stiffening (GM worse, 1.06-1.24), arm / hub-joint stiffening (worse).
- Illustrative baseline (T-089 s2, `t089_sens.py`): pitch rate gains x 0.5 give GM 2.09, crossover 1.9 -> 1.05 Hz, PM 56 -> 58 deg. Handling cost of a 1.05 Hz pitch crossover (attitude hold, disturbance rejection, transition pitch-over): "—" (no sim round allowed; the tethered hover measures it). The FC move keeps the D-191 gains.

## 4. Recommendation
1. **Do not buy a structural change with the 18 Dec order.** The nominal-group screen is misleading for the sleeve and the arms (they pass there and fail 2.0 over all 24 groups), and nothing moves the 95-109 Hz forced response robustly. An optional $16.93 spare R09 14x12 tube keeps the 150 mm sleeve possible later; it is not needed for the recommendation.
2. **Take the FC move into the design (a free change, decision for the owner):** FC hard-mounted 75 mm off the wing centre along the spar (a pitch GM of 2.52-4.00 in the model with the D-191 gains unchanged, roll / yaw not harmed). Check list before it is credited: (a) T-A-TAP (TP-10b) measures the 27-30 Hz mode and the pitch-rate mode shape on the built airframe, and the tether hover logs the pitch rate at the new site (the benefit rests on the beam FE mode shape; zeta, motor lag and delay are EST); (b) CAD: FC mount, cable route, `collide.py --glb`, `assembly_check.py`, CG / mass sheet; (c) re-run `proofs/run_all.py` with the FC node in `fu_cage.py` (this study did not edit it or the claims, so CL-A-VIB-GM / -SHIFT stay FAIL until then); (d) the VIBE check A-P10(d) at the new site (FC window 32.2-32.7 m/s2 vs 32.9 at the centre: no better).
3. **If T-A-TAP or the tether hover does not confirm it:** fall back to the low-gain pitch start of D-237 (pitch gains x 0.5: GM 2.09 at crossover 1.05 Hz, handling cost "—") and the sleeve as a rung-5 add-on (spare R09, +9.8 g, with the checks in s3) in combination with the FC move (GM 10.1+).
4. **D-185 / VIB-FC remain open and FAIL on the model.** No limit relaxed. Arms 10x8 is the only change that lowers the hover-window FC value (19.7-23.1) but not the sweep (163) or D-185; it costs strength re-runs and a tube not yet priced: hold as the T-A-TAP fallback, as in T-085 s7.
Ladder (docs/engineering-decisions.md): rung 1 subtract: none; rung 2 modify geometry (FC position, arms 10x8, strut sections): FC position works for GM, none works for D-185 / VIB-FC; rung 3 rearrange (FC relocation = rung 3, chosen); rung 4 change approach (measure with T-A-TAP before buying); rung 5 add material (sleeve, struts, bridge, diagonals): the sleeve works for GM only at the centre IMU and not over all groups, rung 3 needs no mass, so rung 5 is not justified.
