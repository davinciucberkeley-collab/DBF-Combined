### m-7 free play basis (decision)
Chosen basis: **flutter** (the 1 deg SERVO.md s4 guide stays the CL-A-LINKAGE-FREEPLAY limit; value 1.28 deg unchanged; claim stays FAIL). Reasons: (1) it is the only sourced limit; a control-precision limit has no source in the repo (the A-P11 roll numbers give about 8 deg/s per deg of elevon, EST, i.e. about +-5 deg/s for the +-0.64 deg of a 1.28 deg dead band: informative, but there is no sourced criterion to compare it against). (2) The linear flutter sweep with the damper (FIXES2 s1, K 0.01-20 N m/rad) shows no dependence on K, but a free-play limit cycle is nonlinear and that sweep does not cover it, so the flutter basis cannot be retired by it. The claim limit therefore does not change; the `how` text no longer calls it a control-precision item. The 1.28 deg budget leaves out servo gear backlash and the clevis cheek holes (both "-"), so the BUILT value is measured at BUILD D5b / E1 and compared with 1 deg; a value above 1 deg is reported as a FAIL, not waived.
Endpoints +-20 deg vs the 21 deg stop: the 1 deg of margin is smaller than the free play, so the surface can reach the stop under a reversed aerodynamic load. That load is hold-level (17 N), not the servo stall (51 N), because the endpoint keeps the servo itself off the stop. Rule written into BUILD D5b: if the measured free play exceeds 1 deg, set the endpoints to +-19 deg (authority is not limited by that: A-P11 needs about +-11 deg for 90 deg/s).
Proposed replacement for FIXES2 E1 step 7 (not edited here, the file is under `redesign/review/QP-4/`): "Probe the free play at the BUILT value (CAD budget 1.28 deg + clevis cheek holes + servo backlash, measured at the trailing edge with a dial gauge), not at a deliberate 1 deg; the flutter check is then run at that measured K."

## AR-005 Elevon servo torque and speed through the linkage (T-056)
- **Category:** kinematics (gearing from the solved linkage) + hand calculation (DC-motor torque-speed line). Not rigid-body dynamics: no integration (the dynamic step is AR-002).
- **Solver / engine:** `redesign/qp/A/mechanism/linkage_loads_check.py` `servo_speed()`; Python 3.14.2, numpy 2.5.4; reads `linkage_core.py` (AR-001).
- **Model version:** git `251702a2` plus the uncommitted working tree of 2026-11-19.
- **Inputs:** gearing 0.909-0.928 (0.9282 at +-20 deg); hinge 0.146 N m VD (FIXES2 s2, EST); preload 0.0093 and damper 0.0098 N m (FIXES2 s2, EST); stall 0.333 N m at 6.0 V (SERVO.md s2), 0.397 N m at 7.2 V (EST); no-load 0.13 s / 60 deg at 6.0 V (SERVO.md s2); required rates 80 / 152 / 276 deg/s (EST: elevon_dynamics D5, A-P11 +-11 deg, crossover 2.2 Hz CLAIMS-FIX.md).
- **Assumptions:** linear torque-speed line; steady loaded speed at the end-point load (conservative); servo speed at 7.2 V "-" so the 6 V speed is used; rotor / gear inertia, rail sag and stall current not modelled ("-").
- **Boundary conditions:** servo at 6.0 V (published pair) and at 7.2 V (EST torque); elevon at +-20 deg under the full VD hinge moment.
- **Numerical settings:** closed-form; gearing sampled every 0.5 deg over +-20 deg both sides.
- **Outputs:** `out/linkage_loads_check.json` `servo_speed_torque_T056` (loaded rate 231 / 263 deg/s; torque margins 2.46 / 2.93 hold, 2.17 / 2.59 loaded; speed margins 2.89 / 3.29 (R1), 1.52 / 1.73 (R2), 0.84 / 0.95 (R3)).
- **Acceptance criteria (set before the run):** speed margin >= 1.0 for R1 and R2; torque margin >= 1.5 (project factor, AR-002 D4).
- **Result:** PASS on R1, R2 and torque in both cases; R3 (full-throw sinusoid at the crossover) not met, not a required case. Independent hand check: 0.9282 x 0.1653 = 0.1534 N m; 8.055 x (1 - 0.1534 / 0.397) = 4.94 rad/s x 0.9282 = 4.59 rad/s = 263 deg/s.
- **Limitations:** EST required rates and 7.2 V torque; 7.2 V speed unpublished; no servo dynamics; bench ramp test at the built rail closes it. Motor mount: cites D-209 / D-210 FEA (FEA-ARM.md section 8, 8.1), not rerun.
