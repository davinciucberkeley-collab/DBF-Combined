## Order to make them

Shop access ends 18 Dec (MF-04): items 1-6 before then.

1. **Print the jigs and the fit-test set** (`jigs/`, then one each of FIN-004, FIN-003-A, GER-001, GER-002). Check the 22.3 mm bores on a real tube, the shoe/piston slide, the block on the spar. If a fit is off, change the CAD params (not the STL) and re-run.
2. **Laser-cut the hot-wire templates** (`hotwire/*.dxf`, 2 mm hardboard). Sand the riding edge. Pin both to the blank ends through the chord-line holes so both LEs sit on one line. Cut 2-3 practice cores; measure the kerf and update `shopconfig.HOTWIRE["kerf_mm"]` if it is not 1.0 mm (MF-05).
3. **Laser-cut the ply** (`laser/ply3_*`): JIG-01 board and top bar first (needed for the spar bond), then handle strips and mat panels.
4. **Print the long-lead flight parts**: FIN-004 blocks (+1 spare; MF-06), FIN-003 mounts in ASA (A and B, 8 total), WNG-004/005 plugs, POD-002 tray set, POD-007 handle, PWR-006 frame, MEC-* horns/frames/covers, GER-001/002 (colours), GER-007 in TPU. **FIN-008 collars are ON HOLD** until the 18 Dec freeze (MF-13).
5. **Cut the foam-board** (`laser/foamboard_*`): laminate fin boards in pairs (3M 77, spray booth) - either cut single boards and laminate on pins through the MARK circles, or laminate first and knife-cut the pair from the 1:1 PDF. Then the pod board set.
6. **Wing core** (wing/BUILD.md 2-5) with the templates; then **spar + block bond in JIG-01**: bolt the two posts to the base board, stand dummy 22 mm tubes (tape-wrapped as release) in the posts, thread both blocks, slide the spar through, drop the top bar over both tube tops, check the tubes with a square in two directions, bond and cure 24 h without moving the jig.
7. **Half-fin root bonds with JIG-02**: slide a collar down each motor tube to the tube end, half-fin in the slot, root fillet, cure. Remove before the station collars go on.
8. **Motor mounts with JIG-03** (fins/BUILD.md step 18): hold the gauge (A on UR/LL, B on UL/LR) flat on the outboard fin face against the tube at |z| 60-85 mm (clear of the ESC pad), zero an angle cube on face D (the face looking forward, -x), move the cube to the bell top (prop off): 0.0 +/- 0.5 deg; turn the cube 90 deg and repeat. Face D is 7.00 deg from square to the tube (the modelled cant, params propulsion.stations.cant_deg).
9. **Coroplast box** (`laser/coroplast_*`, knife): panels, corner angles, then transport/BUILD.md 5-16.

## Jigs: how to use

| Jig | Files | Use |
|---|---|---|
| JIG-01 tube-bore / block clocking (MF-06) | `jigs/JIG-01_*` (2 posts), `laser/ply3_*` (base board, top bar) | Board flat on the bench; posts bolted with 4 x M4 each into the engraved squares; the LE line on the board is parallel to the tube line. Bores in post and bar are tube OD + 0.3 / + 0.4 mm. The two supports 45 mm and ~cradle + core height mm apart hold each tube within about 0.4 deg, so both blocks are clocked by the tubes, not by eye |
| JIG-02 fin-alignment collar (MF-08) | `jigs/JIG-02_*` (x4) | On the motor tube near its end; the half-fin laminate sits in the slot (laminate + 0.3 mm), so the fin plane passes through the tube axis and stays square to the wing while the root fillet cures |
| JIG-03 cant gauge (MF-07) | `jigs/JIG-03-A_*` (UR/LL), `JIG-03-B_*` (UL/LR) | Datum face D = the bell top of that station as modelled (7 deg tangential cant). Relieved over the 10 mm LE edge strip so it sits on the board face. Check after every prop strike or mount slip |

## Not exported, or exported with a caveat

- POD-001 "fb floor bevel": not a flat sheet body (depth 130.4 mm, section 4.8 x 4.8 mm) -> shaped by hand (pod/BUILD.md step 3)
- Hot-wired and hand-cut foam parts (wing core cuts, elevons, pod LE filler and crush block, cradle saddles, prop tray, cartridges) are not sheet or print parts: the templates and BUILD.md numbers cover them.
- DXF outlines are nominal (no kerf offset). Laser kerf is listed per sheet; the hot-wire templates DO include the wire kerf.
